For a **Chamber of Gold Buyers B2B Gold Sales Platform** that facilitates transactions between **Tier 1 Buyers (large refiners, bullion banks, international traders)** and **Tier 2 Buyers (licensed local exporters, aggregators, jewelers, manufacturers)**, I would **not** recommend a general marketplace model like Alibaba.

Instead, I recommend a **controlled exchange platform** that combines an RFQ (Request for Quote), auction, and negotiated trading model. This aligns better with the high-value, regulated nature of gold trading.

### 2. Transaction Fee (Secondary Revenue)

Charge a small fee per completed trade.

Example:

* 0.10–0.30% of trade value
* Or a fixed fee per kilogram traded

Given the high value of gold, even a low percentage generates substantial revenue.

---

### 3. RFQ Marketplace (Recommended)

Rather than listing gold for sale, sellers publish availability and buyers submit requests.

Example workflow:

1. Chamber of Gold Buyers lists available lots.
2. Tier 1 and Tier 2 buyers submit bids.
3. Sellers compare offers.
4. Negotiation takes place.
5. Chamber of Gold Buyers approves the transaction.
6. Payment is placed into escrow.
7. Gold is delivered.
8. Ownership transfers.

This model provides greater control and price discovery than a simple catalog.

---

For high-demand lots:

* Timed auctions
* Reverse auctions
* Sealed bids

Highest qualified bidder wins.

Revenue comes from escrow fees.

### 6. Logistics Coordination

Revenue is earned through service fees or logistics partnerships.

### 7. Assay & Certification

### 8. Market Intelligence

### One important consideration

Because this platform deals with **high-value gold transactions**, the commission structure should be designed carefully. Very high percentage commissions can discourage adoption. Many commodity exchanges instead charge a **small fixed platform fee per transaction**, a **small fee per kilogram traded**, or a **modest percentage capped at a maximum amount**. This keeps fees predictable while still generating sustainable revenue.

For a Chamber-led platform, a **hybrid model** often works best:

* Free access for verified members.
* Low transaction/platform fees.
* Optional premium subscriptions and value-added services.
* Revenue sharing between the Chamber and your company.

This approach minimizes barriers to joining the platform while creating multiple income streams that grow with member adoption.

The Problem

Five pains the status quo cannot relieve

Gold trading today runs on phone calls, spreadsheets, paper assay certificates, and trusted intermediaries — under a regulatory environment that changed fundamentally on 2 April 2025 with the GoldBod Act 1140.

P1
Price Opacity

Buyers cannot see competing bids; discover overpayment only after trade closes. LBMA PM Fix daily swings of US$30–80/oz.

Quantified impact
~US$1,200–4,500/kg overpayment on 1-in-4 trades
P2
Counterparty Risk

No reliable verification of Minerals Commission licence or assay history before payment.

Quantified impact
1-in-8 deals end in dispute, avg. US$18,000, 47-day resolution
P3
Export Documentation Drag

Post-GoldBod Act 1140: coordination across GoldBod, PMMC, GRA Customs, BoG forex verification.

Quantified impact
6 working days delay, ~14 staff-hours per shipment
P4
Funds-at-Risk Window

Funds leave buyer's bank before gold title transfers; risk of attachment by third-party creditors.

Quantified impact
US$840K combined exposure in 3 incidents logged in 2024
P5
Audit Trail Fragmentation

WhatsApp + paper + PDFs cannot reconstruct LBMA RGG chain-of-custody.

Quantified impact
6 staff-hours per audit, frequently fails first LBMA review
REGULATORY SHIFT
GoldBod Act 1140

On 2 April 2025, Ghana established the Ghana Gold Board (GoldBod) as the sole buyer, assayer, and exporter of small-scale-mined gold. Every platform trade touching that supply chain must integrate with GoldBod.

Source
UNCTAD Investment Policy Monitor, 2025

Document 1 — Value Proposition Canvas

Four personas, one Fit Statement

Every participant is licensed and verified before they can trade. The platform serves both sides of every trade plus the Chamber as governance.

The Fit Statement

"For licensed Tier 1 and Tier 2 gold buyers and sellers in Ghana who must trade high-value gold under strict GoldBod, PMMC, Minerals Commission, and LBMA Responsible Gold Guidance compliance without exposing themselves to price opacity, counterparty risk, or funds-at-risk windows, AurumXchange is a regulated B2B gold trading infrastructure that combines RFQ-driven negotiation, Chamber-approved escrow, and an immutable LBMA-RGG-aligned audit trail into a single compliance-gated exchange."

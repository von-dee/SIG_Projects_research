
Here's a categorized list of the actual platforms and sources referenced above.

## Company & Financial Data

- **SEC EDGAR** (US public company filings) — https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search
- **Companies House** (UK company filings/ownership) — https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk
- **OpenCorporates** (global company registry aggregator) — https://opencorporates.com
- **Crunchbase** (funding rounds, investors, company profiles) — https://www.crunchbase.com
- **PitchBook** (private market/M&A data, subscription) — https://pitchbook.com

## Job Postings & Hiring Signals

- **LinkedIn Jobs** — https://www.linkedin.com/jobs
- **Indeed** — https://www.indeed.com
- **Levels.fyi** (compensation + hiring trends by company) — https://www.levels.fyi

## Tech Stack Detection

- **BuiltWith** (what technology a site runs on) — https://builtwith.com
- **Wappalyzer** (browser extension + API for tech detection) — https://www.wappalyzer.com
- **StackShare** (companies self-report their stack) — https://stackshare.io

## Review & Sentiment Sites

- **G2** (B2B software reviews) — https://www.g2.com
- **Capterra** — https://www.capterra.com
- **TrustRadius** — https://www.trustradius.com
- **Glassdoor** (employee sentiment, culture, leadership signals) — https://www.glassdoor.com

## Patents & IP

- **Google Patents** — https://patents.google.com
- **USPTO Patent Public Search** — https://ppubs.uspto.gov/pubwebapp
- **WIPO Global Brand/Patent Database** — https://www.wipo.int/reference/en/branddb

## Web Archives & Change Tracking

- **Wayback Machine** — https://web.archive.org
- **Visualping** (monitor website changes) — https://visualping.io

## Domain & Infrastructure Intel

- **WHOIS lookup** (ICANN) — https://lookup.icann.org
- **DNSDumpster** (DNS reconnaissance) — https://dnsdumpster.com
- **Shodan** (internet-connected device/infrastructure search) — https://www.shodan.io

## Professional/Social Signal Sources

- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** (org mapping, warm-path finder) — https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/sales-navigator
- **X/Twitter Advanced Search** — https://twitter.com/search-advanced

## News, Press & Trigger Events

- **Google News** — https://news.google.com
- **PR Newswire** — https://www.prnewswire.com
- **Business Wire** — https://www.businesswire.com

## OSINT Aggregator/Framework (for structuring research)

- **OSINT Framework** (categorized directory of OSINT tools) — https://osintframework.com
- **Maltego** (link-analysis/visualization tool, freemium) — https://www.maltego.com

## Professional Bodies (methodology, training, standards)

- **OSINT Curious** (community, training resources) — https://osintcurio.us
- **SANS OSINT resources** — https://www.sans.org/blog/?focus-area=open-source-intelligence-osint

A practical note: several of these (PitchBook, Sales Navigator, Maltego) are paid tools — the free tier or public-search version is usually enough to start, and I'd size the investment against deal value before subscribing to anything premium. Want me to build a workflow that chains a few of these together for a specific use case, like prospecting a target account list?

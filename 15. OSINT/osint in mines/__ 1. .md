## Sublist: Mining-Relevant OSINT Sources

**Direct relevance (mining-industry-specific)**

- Geological survey databases (USGS, national geological surveys)
- Mining claims/tenement registries (cadastral mining maps)
- Commodity price indices (gold, copper, lithium, coal, etc.)
- Mining company stock exchange filings (TSX-V, ASX, LSE — many mining juniors)
- National mining regulator databases (permits, licenses, production data)
- Environmental impact assessments for mine sites
- Satellite imagery (monitoring active mine sites, tailings, deforestation)
- Aerial/drone imagery databases
- LiDAR/elevation datasets (site topography, subsidence)
- Trade/industry publications (Mining.com, Mining Weekly, Northern Miner)
- Patent databases (mining/extraction technology)
- Occupational safety incident databases (mine safety records, MSHA)
- Import/export & customs records (ore/mineral shipments)
- Vessel tracking/AIS data (bulk carrier shipments of ore)
- Property/land registries (surface and mineral rights)
- Sanctions/watchlists (conflict minerals, sanctioned mining entities)
- Corporate registries (ownership of mining companies, subsidiaries)
- Beneficial ownership registries (opaque mining ownership structures)

**Indirect relevance (supporting/contextual)**

- Local/regional news (community impact, protests, labor disputes)
- NGO/nonprofit filings (environmental watchdog reports)
- Weather/climate data (operational disruption risk)
- Job postings/employee reviews (workforce conditions, expansion signals)
- Court records (litigation involving mining firms)
- Lobbying disclosure databases (regulatory influence by mining interests)
- Political donation records (mining industry political ties)
- Academic journals/theses (geology, mineralogy, environmental studies)
- Social media (activist campaigns, local community sentiment)
- Procurement/government contract databases (equipment/service contracts)
- Vessel/aviation tracking (logistics supporting remote mine sites)
- Breach/leak databases (leaked internal mining company documents)
- Genealogy/vital records (indigenous land claim disputes)
- Supply chain/logistics tracking (downstream mineral processing)

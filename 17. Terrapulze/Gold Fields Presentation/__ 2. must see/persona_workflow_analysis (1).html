Purpose of This Evaluation

For each persona and each major daily task, this evaluation determines: what the user is trying to accomplish, what problem occurs, what TerraPulze currently addresses, whether the workflow is fully/partially/unresolved, what would close the gap, and the resulting outcome.

Capabilities publicly described for TerraPulze are treated as current capabilities. Proposed features/endpoints are recommendations, not existing functionality. The public application describes ML-based identification of copper, gold and lithium anomalies with scientifically rated confidence.

Persona 1

Field Geologist

Find → Navigate → Observe → Sample → Record → Validate → Report

1

Review Exploration Assignment

🟡 Partial

Pain: Information distributed across GIS, PDFs, Excel, emails, maps, reports. Geologist spends significant time determining what to investigate.

Recommendation: Create a Field Mission attached to a project — containing target coordinates, ranking, geological context, field objectives, required samples, assigned geologist, due date.

2

Review Available Geological Data

🟢 Addressed

One of the strongest use cases. Brings datasets and spatial context into a unified environment. Outcome: Less tool switching + faster interpretation.

3

Acquire/Prepare Datasets

🟢 / 🟡

Ingestion workflow exists. Improve: Add Data Quality Gate: Upload → Format check → CRS check → Coordinate validation → Missing-value → Duplicate → Spatial → Quality score → Ready.

4

Examine Satellite Context

🟢 Addressed

Satellite context and remote-sensing-derived indicators are in the workflow. Outcome: Faster from imagery → interpretation.

5

Generate Exploration Targets

🟢 Strong

Core value proposition. ML identification of anomalies with scientifically rated confidence. Outcome: Prioritized areas instead of manual review.

6

Understand Why Target Generated

🟢 Addressed

Explainable scoring/SHAP. Geologist treats AI as decision support, not a black box.

7

Plan Field Visit

🟡 Partial

Target locations help, but full field-program management is a gap. Feature: Field Mission Planner — moves from "Here are targets" to "Here is what you need to do next."

8

Navigate to Target Offline

🟢 Strong

Offline field functionality is an important part of the value proposition.

9

Record Geological Observations

🟡 Partial

Feature: Digital Field Observation Form — every observation auto-inherits project, target, coordinates, date/time, user. Observation becomes structured evidence, not just a note.

10

Collect & Track Samples

🔴 Major Gap

Feature: Sample Management — Target → Sample → Chain of custody → Laboratory → Assay → Validation → Target update. Connects physical evidence to digital intelligence.

11

Compare Field Evidence with AI Prediction

🟡 Partial

Feature: Target Validation — Status: AI Generated → Field Pending → Confirmed → Partially Confirmed → Rejected. Makes TerraPulze a learning workflow.

12

Recommend Next Action

🟡 Partial

Target ranking helps, but decision should incorporate field and assay evidence.

The Biggest Unresolved Workflow

Across all personas, the single biggest gap is the break between target generation and verified exploration evidence.

Current Conceptual Flow

Data → AI → Target → Report

Required Future Flow

Data → AI Analysis → Target → Field Validation → Observation → Sample → Assay → Target Update → Drill Decision → Evaluation → Investment Decision → Regulatory Evidence

Each persona consumes the output of the previous persona. The evidence generated at the first step should be trustworthy, traceable and reusable through the final decision.

Final Evaluation

Current Position

  • Strongly addresses: Data ingestion, spatial interpretation, remote sensing, AI-assisted target generation, target explanation, technical reporting.
  • Partially addresses: Field execution, exploration management, executive decision-making, due diligence, compliance.
  • Does not currently cover: Laboratory management, drilling operations, financial modelling, corporate finance, mine planning, production, full regulatory case management.

Strategic Opportunity

Make TerraPulze the shared evidence and intelligence layer connecting exploration to downstream decisions:

Collect → Analyze → Predict → Validate → Decide → Finance → Regulate

One geological dataset serves all six personas while each receives information relevant to their specific decision.

Field Geologist:

"Where should I investigate?"

Exploration Manager:

"Which target should I fund?"

CEO:

"Which asset should I advance?"

Consultant:

"Can the evidence support that claim?"

Investment Firm:

"Is the risk-adjusted return worth funding?"

Regulator:

"Is the activity legal, compliant and properly evidenced?"

TerraPulze's opportunity is to make the evidence generated at the first step trustworthy, traceable and reusable all the way through the final decision.

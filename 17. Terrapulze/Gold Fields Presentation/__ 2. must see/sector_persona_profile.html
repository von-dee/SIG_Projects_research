Persona 1

Field Geologist

Responsible for collecting, interpreting, validating and documenting geological information directly from the field. Converts desktop exploration hypotheses into physical geological evidence.

Job Description

A Field Geologist investigates geological formations, identifies mineralization, collects samples, maps structures, records observations and validates exploration targets generated from geological, geochemical, geophysical and remote-sensing data.

Primary Responsibilities

Conduct geological mapping
Identify lithologies, structures, alteration & mineralization
Collect rock, soil & stream sediment samples
Record GPS coordinates & field observations
Validate remotely generated exploration targets
Interpret geological & geophysical information
Take & organize field photographs
Maintain field notebooks & digital records
Prepare samples for laboratory analysis
Coordinate with laboratories & field crews
Compare observations with exploration models
Provide follow-up recommendations

Day-to-Day Workflow

Morning — Planning

Reviews: current targets, previous observations, geological maps, satellite imagery, geophysical/geochemical anomalies, weather, accessibility, field objectives.

"Which areas and targets do I need to investigate today?"

1
Travel to Target

Navigate using GPS, GIS maps, offline maps, mobile devices, physical maps.

2
Geological Observation

Examine: rock type, mineralization, alteration, veining, structures, contacts, weathering, outcrop conditions.

3
Record Observations

Capture: coordinates, geological description, structural measurements, photographs, field notes, confidence/interpretation.

4
Collect Samples

Identified → Labelled → Logged → Bagged → Associated with coordinates → Sent for laboratory analysis.

5
Validate Target

Compare physical evidence with original exploration hypothesis. "Do I actually see alteration, structure and mineralization here?"

6
End-of-Day Synchronization

Data uploaded/synchronized when connectivity becomes available.

7
Interpretation

Review findings: Target confirmed / Partially confirmed / Rejected / More data required.

Major Pain Points

Data Fragmentation

Excel, GIS, PDFs, paper notebooks, lab reports, GPS devices, cloud drives

Poor Connectivity

No internet, weak mobile, limited electricity in field locations

Repetitive Data Entry

Same information entered into multiple systems

Combining Datasets

Manual comparison of geochemistry + geophysics + geology + satellite + field

Target Uncertainty

AI/GIS anomaly ≠ meaningful mineralization — requires field validation

Sample Tracking

Difficult to maintain: sample → location → target → lab → assay → interpretation

Data Quality

Incorrect coordinates, sample IDs, missing observations, duplicate samples, wrong CRS

Inputs

  • Geological maps
  • Geophysical data
  • Geochemical data
  • Satellite imagery
  • Historical reports
  • Existing targets
  • Licence boundaries
  • Field observations
  • Laboratory results

Outputs

  • Geological maps
  • Field observations
  • Sample records
  • Target validation
  • Geological interpretations
  • Updated targets
  • Technical recommendations

End Goal

Generate reliable physical and geological evidence that determines whether a target is worth further investigation, sampling or drilling.

"There is sufficient geological evidence here to justify the next exploration activity."

Persona 2

Junior Explorer / Exploration Manager

Oversees exploration activities and translates geological information into exploration programs and investment decisions. Primary job: "Where should the company spend its limited exploration resources?"

Job Description

The Junior Explorer may be more operational, while the Exploration Manager has greater responsibility for strategy, people, budgets, schedules, contractors, targets, reporting and project progression.

Primary Responsibilities

Develop exploration programs
Define exploration objectives
Manage licences/projects
Review geological information
Rank exploration targets
Coordinate field geologists
Manage contractors
Plan surveys & drilling
Manage exploration budgets
Review laboratory results
Evaluate exploration risks
Recommend targets for drilling

Day-to-Day Workflow

Morning Review

Project status, field activities, target progress, lab results, contractor activities, budget, safety, schedule.

1
Review New Data

Assays, geological mapping, geophysical surveys, satellite analysis, field observations.

2
Review Targets

Rank by: geological evidence, geochemical strength, geophysical response, structural interpretation, historical evidence, field validation.

3
Assign Activities

Which target needs mapping? Sampling? Additional geophysics? Drilling?

4
Manage Field Team

Communicate with geologists, GIS specialists, technicians, survey teams, drilling contractors, laboratories.

5
Review Budget

Planned expenditure vs actual expenditure.

6
Make Decisions

Each target: Continue / Investigate / Validate / Drill / Hold / Reject.

7
Report

Communicate project status to senior management.

Major Pain Points

Too Much Data

Challenge isn't obtaining data — it's determining which information actually matters.

Fragmented Information

GIS, Excel, email, cloud, PDFs, laboratory systems.

Target Prioritization

Dozens/hundreds of anomalies — which deserve money?

Budget Constraints

Technically attractive target may be too expensive to test.

Poor Visibility

Cannot see status of all projects at once.

Communication

Every handoff (Field → Mgr → CEO → Investors → Regulators) introduces delays or information loss.

Inputs

  • Geological/geochemical data
  • Geophysical surveys
  • Satellite analysis
  • Field observations
  • Laboratory results
  • Budgets & plans
  • Licence information

Outputs

  • Exploration plans
  • Target rankings
  • Field programs
  • Drill recommendations
  • Budget forecasts
  • Progress & technical reports
  • Management recommendations

End Goal

Maximize geological value from the exploration budget by identifying, validating and advancing the highest-potential targets.

"Where should we spend the next dollar?"

Persona 3

Junior Mining CEO / Major Mining Corporation

Responsible for strategic decisions affecting the company's mineral assets, capital allocation, growth and long-term value.

Job Description

For a junior: decide whether to advance, raise capital, acquire, JV, farm out, or sell. For a major: manage a larger portfolio of exploration, development and producing assets.

Primary Responsibilities

Define corporate strategy
Evaluate mineral assets
Allocate capital
Approve exploration budgets
Evaluate acquisitions
Negotiate partnerships
Manage investors & boards
Evaluate project economics
Monitor regulatory risks
Decide whether projects advance or stop

Day-to-Day Workflow

Morning Review

High-level information: project status, major risks, budget, exploration results, significant discoveries, regulatory issues, investor concerns.

1
Review Portfolio

Which projects performing? Consuming capital? Highest potential?

2
Review Exploration Results

New targets, assays, interpretations, drill results, updated resources.

3
Challenge Assumptions

How reliable is the evidence? What is still unknown? What could invalidate the project?

4
Make Capital Decisions

Advance / Hold / Acquire / Farm-in / Joint Venture / Divest / Abandon.

5
Communicate Externally

Board presentations, investor updates, corporate announcements, regulatory disclosures.

Major Pain Points

Technical Information Overload

Need concise information without losing critical technical evidence.

Non-Standardized Reporting

Different teams present information differently.

Difficulty Comparing Assets

Different methodologies across projects.

Information Trust

Need confidence data is accurate, current, traceable, professionally reviewed.

Slow Decision-Making

Waiting for multiple reports delays capital allocation.

Disintegrated Intelligence

Geology, licences, environment, economics stored separately.

Inputs

  • Exploration reports
  • Target rankings
  • Drill results & resources
  • Budgets
  • Licence information
  • Financial models
  • Regulatory information

Outputs

  • Capital allocation decisions
  • Acquisition decisions
  • Investment proposals
  • Board reports
  • Investor communications
  • Strategic plans

End Goal

Increase shareholder value by identifying the right mineral assets and allocating capital to projects with the strongest risk-adjusted potential.

"Which asset should the company bet on?"

Persona 4

Investor / Independent Consultant

Provides objective technical analysis of mineral projects. Central question: "Can I independently verify what the company is telling me?"

Job Description

The Consultant reviews exploration data, geological models, resource estimates, technical reports, sampling, assays, methodologies and project risks. The Investor uses technical information to assess investment merit.

Consultant Responsibilities

  • Review exploration datasets
  • Verify technical claims
  • Assess data quality
  • Review geological interpretations
  • Evaluate targets & risks
  • Prepare independent reports
  • Provide recommendations

Investor Responsibilities

  • Understand project potential
  • Assess technical credibility
  • Evaluate risk
  • Compare projects
  • Determine investment justification

Day-to-Day Workflow

1
Receive Project Information

Reports, maps, assays, GIS data, drill information, licences, historical data.

2
Organize Information

What exists? What is missing? Which datasets are reliable?

3
Validate Provenance

Where did this data come from? When collected? Modified? Who generated it?

4
Reinterpret Data

Independently review geology, geochemistry, geophysics, structure, satellite.

5
Compare Claims vs Evidence

"High-potential gold corridor" — Does the evidence support this?

6
Identify Risks

Poor sampling, insufficient drilling, data gaps, geological/licence uncertainty, environmental constraints.

7
Prepare Recommendation

Proceed / Proceed with conditions / Additional exploration required / Insufficient evidence / Do not proceed.

Major Pain Points

Data-Room Chaos

Documents from many sources and formats.

Poor Data Provenance

Difficult to establish original source of information.

Manual Verification

Large amounts of time checking datasets.

Inconsistent Formats

GIS, Excel, PDFs and databases don't align.

Time Pressure

Expected to produce defensible reports quickly.

Independence

Must demonstrate conclusions are evidence-based, not company claims.

Inputs

  • Client datasets
  • Exploration reports
  • Assays & GIS
  • Geological models
  • Satellite imagery
  • Licence information
  • Historical reports

Outputs

  • Independent assessment
  • Due diligence report
  • Risk assessment
  • Target evaluation
  • Technical recommendations
  • Investor briefing

End Goal

Provide an independent, evidence-based assessment for a defensible decision.

"Can I independently verify what the company is telling me?"

Persona 5

Investment Firm / Mining Finance Professional

Evaluates mineral projects from an investment and risk perspective. "Can this geological opportunity become an economically viable investment?"

Primary Responsibilities

Evaluate mining investment opportunities
Conduct technical due diligence
Assess project risks
Review financial models
Analyze CAPEX/OPEX
Calculate investment returns
Compare investment opportunities
Prepare investment committee materials

Day-to-Day Workflow

1
Receive Investment Proposal

Business plan, technical reports, exploration results, financial model, licence documentation.

2
Initial Screening

Commodity, location, licence, stage, company, capital requirement, potential scale.

3
Technical Due Diligence

Geological model, exploration data, targets, drilling, assays, resource estimates.

4
Financial Analysis

CAPEX, OPEX, revenue, commodity prices, NPV, IRR, payback, sensitivity.

5
Risk Assessment

Geological, technical, financial, regulatory, environmental, political risks.

6
Investment Committee

Invest / Do not invest / Invest with conditions / Request additional information.

7
Monitor Investment

Exploration progress, spending, milestones, results, regulatory, performance.

Major Pain Points

Technical-Financial Disconnect

"High-priority target" — what does that mean financially?

Technical Info Difficult for Finance

Financial professionals may not be geologists.

Due Diligence Is Slow

Large amounts of information reviewed manually.

Hidden Risks

Data gaps hidden inside large technical reports.

Inputs

  • Technical reports
  • Exploration datasets
  • Resource estimates
  • Financial models
  • Commodity prices
  • Licence & enviro docs

Outputs

  • Investment recommendation
  • Technical risk assessment
  • Financial model
  • Committee report
  • Monitoring report

End Goal

Allocate capital to projects with acceptable balance of geological potential, financial return and risk.

"Is the potential return worth the technical and financial risk?"

Persona 6

Government Regulator

Oversees mineral exploration and mining to ensure companies operate within legal, environmental, technical and reporting requirements. "Is this activity legitimate, compliant and in the public interest?"

Primary Responsibilities

Review licence applications
Validate licence boundaries
Monitor licence holders
Check compliance
Identify licence overlaps
Review environmental constraints
Conduct inspections
Approve/reject applications
Maintain government records
Investigate violations

Day-to-Day Workflow

1
Receive Application/Report

Licence application, exploration program, coordinates, technical report, environmental info, company details.

2
Validate Information

Applicant identity, licence area, coordinates, commodity, existing rights, overlaps.

3
Spatial Analysis

Check overlaps: existing licences, protected areas, restricted zones, water bodies, sensitive areas.

4
Technical Review

Proposed program, technical capability, expenditure, geological info, previous work.

5
Environmental Review

Check environmental requirements and constraints.

6
Decision

Approved / Rejected / Approved with conditions / Additional information required.

7
Monitoring & Audit

Exploration activities, reporting, licence conditions, expenditure, environmental obligations. Full audit trail.

Major Pain Points

Large Volumes

Many projects processed simultaneously.

Manual Document Review

PDFs, Excel, paper, GIS, email.

Spatial Complexity

Overlaps difficult without good GIS.

Compliance Monitoring

Difficult to continuously monitor after licences granted.

Limited Visibility

No single real-time view of mining landscape.

Auditability

Must be able to defend decisions.

Inputs

  • Licence applications
  • GIS coordinates
  • Company information
  • Exploration programs
  • Geological reports
  • Environmental documents
  • Production/expenditure reports

Outputs

  • Licence decisions
  • Compliance reports
  • Inspection reports
  • Regulatory decisions
  • Licence databases
  • Environmental screening
  • Sector intelligence

End Goal

Ensure mineral resources are explored and developed legally, transparently, responsibly.

"Is this activity legitimate, compliant and in the public interest?"

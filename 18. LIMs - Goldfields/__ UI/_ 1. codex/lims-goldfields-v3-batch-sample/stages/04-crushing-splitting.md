# 04 — Crushing & splitting

**Role:** Prep technician  
**Record:** [Crush / split worksheet](../forms/04-crush-split-worksheet.md)  
**Outcome:** A defined coarse split proceeds; a retrievable reject is retained.

**Batch handling:** A crusher/split run groups the work, while every member produces its own work-split and reject children, mass balance and archive location.

## User flow

1. Scan the dried sample, inspect readiness, and record crusher and riffle splitter IDs.
2. Run the jaw crusher under the configured method; record run identifiers and any cleaning or carryover controls.
3. Riffle-split the crushed material into the working split and reject. Assign unique child IDs and weigh each portion where required.
4. Bag, label, and shelf the reject; record its archive/retrieval location.
5. Send the working split to the pulverising queue. Sign off the worksheet and resolve any material-balance flag.

## Form fields entered

Parent dried sample ID; crusher ID/run; splitter ID; work split ID/mass; reject ID/mass; recovery or mass-balance result; reject bag/seal and shelf location; cleaning status; operator, timestamp, and deviations.

## Controls and hand-off

- The working split and reject are children of the same dried sample, not replacement IDs.
- A missing reject location blocks completion; a material loss exception must be explained.

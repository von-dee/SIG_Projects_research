User flow

Six steps, in order.

01

Locate the interval.

Find the approved drill-core interval. Confirm hole ID, from depth, and to depth against the sampling plan before touching the core.

02

Cut or collect ~3 kg.

Using site procedure, take roughly three kilograms of material. Keep sample boundaries aligned exactly to the recorded depths.

03

Bag, tag, identify.

Place all material in a suitable bag. Apply a durable tag or barcode bearing the field sample ID, hole ID, and from–to interval.

04

Inspect for integrity.

Inspect bag and tag for legibility, closure, and damage. Record sample condition and flag any exceptions on the form.

05

Complete & sign custody.

Fill in the submission/chain-of-custody form — collector, collection time, signatures — then sign the hand-off.

06

Seal & dispatch.

Seal the bag and dispatch to the laboratory. The next custodian acknowledges receipt; custody remains open until lab intake.

Controls & hand-off

Three rules, inviolable.

Custody is identity.

Every rule below protects a single principle: the sample that arrives at the laboratory must be the same one cut from the core, by the same person, on the same day, with the same depths.

Control · 01

Never combine intervals under one tag, and never reuse a field sample ID. One interval, one identity, forever.

Control · 02

A damaged or unsealed bag must be recorded before transfer — not after, not at the next station, before.

Control · 03

Receiving uses the field sample ID and custody event to accept, hold, or reject the sample. No ID, no intake.

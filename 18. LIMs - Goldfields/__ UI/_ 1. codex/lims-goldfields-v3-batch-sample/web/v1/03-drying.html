User flow

Five steps from wet to dry.

01

Scan & confirm method.

Scan the accepted lab sample. Confirm the required drying method and verify that the sample container is compatible with the oven.

02

Load the oven.

Load the sample into an identified oven at an assigned shelf or position. Start a new drying run or join an existing active one.

03

Set & record parameters.

Set and record the target temperature, start time, and planned duration according to the approved lab method for the material type.

04

Unload & weigh dry.

At completion, unload safely. Inspect the sample and container, then weigh it for dry mass using the qualified balance.

05

Sign-off & route.

Record actual end time, dry mass, and any deviations. Sign off the run. Mark the sample ready for crushing only when fully complete.

Controls & hand-off

Trust the mass.

Drying is a calculated risk.

The transition from wet mass to dry mass tells a story about the sample's moisture content. The LIMS exists to check that story automatically. If the math fails, the sample stops.

Control · 01

The LIMS calculates and flags unusual wet-to-dry mass loss using configured limits. Anomalous loss is a data integrity warning, not a convenience.

Control · 02

An interrupted run or abnormal mass requires a hold/review before crushing. The sample cannot proceed until the deviation is investigated and cleared.

!
Interrupted runs are isolated.

Completion, mass flags, and holds are decided per sample. An interrupted member does not hide the status of the rest of the oven load. The batch moves forward; the problem stays behind.

Brief

One becomes two.

Role
Prep technician. Operates the crusher and splitter, assigns child IDs, and manages the archive location of the reject material.
Record
Crush / split worksheet tracking parentage, mass balance, and the archive path of the reject.
↳ ../forms/04-crush-split-worksheet.md
Outcome
A defined coarse split proceeds to pulverising; a retrievable reject is retained and shelved.
↳ A crusher/split run groups the work, but every member produces its own children and mass balance.
User flow

Five steps from whole to split.

01

Scan & inspect.

Scan the dried sample. Confirm readiness from the drying stage and record the jaw crusher and riffle splitter IDs to be used.

02

Run the crusher.

Crush the sample under the configured method. Record run identifiers and execute any required cleaning or carryover controls between samples.

03

Riffle-split.

Riffle-split the crushed material into the working split and the reject. Assign unique child IDs and weigh each portion where required by the method.

04

Archive the reject.

Bag, label, and shelf the reject portion. Record its exact archive and retrieval location so it can be found months or years later.

05

Route & sign off.

Send the working split to the pulverising queue. Sign off the worksheet and resolve any material-balance flags before closing the run.

Controls & hand-off

Lineage and loss.

Children are not replacements.

The working split and the reject are unique entities born from the same parent. Their relationship is strictly hierarchical, and the mass that goes in must account for the mass that comes out.

Control · 01

The working split and reject are children of the same dried sample, not replacement IDs. The parent's identity is preserved in the lineage tree.

Control · 02

A missing reject location blocks completion. A material loss exception must be investigated, documented, and explained before the run can close.

!
A missing reject is a broken audit trail.

The reject is the physical backup of the analytical process. If it cannot be located on a shelf, the worksheet cannot be signed off. Unexplained material loss triggers an immediate hold on the working split.

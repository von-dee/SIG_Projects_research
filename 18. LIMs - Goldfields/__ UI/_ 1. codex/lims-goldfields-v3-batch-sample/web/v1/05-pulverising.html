Brief

The final fineness.

Role
Prep technician. Operates the ring mill, executes the sieve check, and manages regrinds until the pulp meets the P80 specification.
Record
Pulverising & sieve QC form, tracking grind time, fineness result, and the complete regrind history.
↳ ../forms/05-pulverising-sieve-qc-form.md
Outcome
A pulp that passes the specified P80 fineness requirement, ready for charge weigh-out.
↳ A failed member is removed to regrind without stopping passing members of the same mill run.
User flow

Five steps from chip to pulp.

01

Scan & select method.

Scan the coarse working split. Select the approved grind method for the material and identify the ring mill to be used.

02

Pulverise.

Pulverise the material in the ring mill, applying all documented cleaning and contamination-control steps between samples.

03

Sieve & check P80.

Take the defined sieve cut, perform the P80 fineness check, and record the sieve ID, cut mass, and the percent-passing result.

04

Assign pulp & release.

If the result meets specification, assign the unique pulp ID and release the pulp to the charge weigh-out queue.

05

Regrind on fail.

If it fails, put the pulp in regrind status, re-pulverise, and record the repeat QC event. Never overwrite the original failed result.

Controls & hand-off

The QC gate holds.

No pass, no charge.

The pulp cannot move forward based on assumption. A verified, current Quality Control result is the only key that unlocks the door to analytical charge creation.

Control · 01

Only a passing, current QC result permits charge creation. A pending, failed, or stale QC result physically and digitally blocks the creation of a charge.

Control · 02

Pulp remains traceable to the original interval and the retained reject. Lineage is never broken, even after multiple regrinds.

↻
Failed results are preserved, not overwritten.

If a pulp requires regrinding, the original failed QC event is kept in the history. This ensures an auditable trail of the sample's preparation and prevents the hiding of preparation anomalies.

User flow

Five steps to the prill.

01

Scan & confirm.

Scan the lead button. Confirm the matching fusion batch, the original tray position, and the approved cupellation method.

02

Place on cupel.

Place the button onto an identified cupel. Record the specific cupel position and the cupellation furnace or run to be used.

03

Run the cycle.

Run the cupellation cycle using the approved program. Record the load, start, and end details for the furnace run.

04

Verify & recover prill.

Verify that lead absorption into the cupel is complete. Recover the precious-metal prill and assign the unique prill ID and container position.

05

Record & route.

Record the recovery condition of the prill. Route the prill container to the selected finish method for final analysis.

Controls & hand-off

Provenance over inference.

The prill holds the history.

The lead is gone, absorbed into the cupel. What remains is the pure precious metal. This prill carries the entire geological and preparation history of the sample forward to the finish method.

Control · 01

A missing or abnormal prill triggers an assay investigation rather than an inferred result. We do not guess grades based on empty cupels.

Control · 02

Prill labels retain the original sample and batch provenance. The link to the drill hole and the fusion tray position is never severed.

∅
No inferred results.

If a prill is lost, spilled, or exhibits abnormal physical characteristics (e.g., "sprouting"), it cannot be silently assigned a grade. The anomaly is recorded, the result is flagged for investigation, and the chain of custody is audited.

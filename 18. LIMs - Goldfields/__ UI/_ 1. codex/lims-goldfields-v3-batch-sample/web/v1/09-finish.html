Brief

From physical prill to digital result.

Role
Assay technician. Executes the final analytical finish, captures the raw data, and verifies the LIMS calculation before submitting to QA.
Record
Finish & prill weight record capturing microbalance data, instrument reads, and the provisional result.

Outcome
A complete technical result ready for QA review. Technical completion is a member status, not a batch assumption.
↳ Raw data and provisional result are captured per prill/sample.
User flow

Five steps to technical completion.

01

Scan & select route.

Scan the prill and select the approved finish route: gravimetric parting or dissolution followed by ICP/AAS analysis.

02

Weigh prill.

Weigh the prill on the identified microbalance. Capture tare, gross, and net values as required by the method.

03

Execute finish method.

For gravimetric: perform parting and enter weights. For ICP/AAS: record dissolution, dilution, sequence, calibration, and the raw read.

04

Calculate result.

Let the LIMS calculate the provisional result using the approved method version. Review the calculation inputs and any system flags.

05

Submit to QA.

Mark the result technically complete and submit its batch to the QA queue. Do not report directly from this stage.

Controls & hand-off

Calculations and amendments.

Data is immutable.

The raw signal from the instrument and the method version used for calculation are locked at the moment of technical completion. If a result must change, it happens through a controlled amendment, not an overwrite.

Control · 01

Raw readings and calculation versions are retained with the result. The calculation can be verified and reproduced at any time.

Control · 02

Any manual amendment needs a reason, authorisation, and a new version. The original result is never silently overwritten.

✕
Do not report from this stage.

A technically complete result is not a reportable result. It must be submitted to the QA queue, validated against standards and duplicates, and officially released before it can leave the laboratory.

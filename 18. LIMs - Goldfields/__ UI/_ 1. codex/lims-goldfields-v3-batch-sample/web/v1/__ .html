Drill Core Sampling Protocol
Complete Lifecycle Overview · v1.0

From ground to Certificate of Analysis.

The complete 11-stage journey of a drill core sample. Every step preserves identity, tracks custody, and builds the immutable lineage graph required for defensible geological data.

Operational Phases
Field & Logistics (01–02)
Sample Preparation (03–05)
Fire Assay Chemistry (06–08)
Analysis & Release (09–11)
01

Action

Field Sampling
Field Geologist

Process

Locate drill core interval, cut ~3kg sample, bag and seal with durable field ID tag.

Outcome

A sealed, uniquely identified field sample tied to its drill-hole interval.
02

Action

Receiving & Login
Receiving Clerk

Process

Verify bag/manifest, weigh wet mass, assign lab barcode. Field ID becomes lineage metadata.

Outcome

The physical bag becomes a registered lab sample with a barcode.
03

Action

Drying
Prep Technician

Process

Load into oven at specified temp/time. Weigh dry mass. System flags unusual moisture loss.

Outcome

A dry, weighed sample linked to the oven run.
04

Action

Crushing & Splitting
Prep Technician

Process

Jaw crush to uniform size, riffle-split into working split and archive reject. Assign child IDs.

Outcome

A defined coarse split proceeds; a retrievable reject is retained.
05

Action

Pulverising
Prep Technician

Process

Ring mill to pulp. Sieve check for P80 fineness. Regrind if fail (never overwrite failed QC).

Outcome

A pulp that passes the specified P80 fineness requirement.
06

Action

Charge Weigh-out
Assay Technician

Process

Microbalance exact assay charge. Archive remaining pulp with exact location mapping.

Outcome

A toleranced assay charge and a locatable pulp archive.
07

Action

Fusion
Assay Technician

Process

Add flux, load crucibles into furnace. Map tray positions. Recover lead button.

Outcome

A traceable lead button for every batch position, validated alongside controls.
08

Action

Cupellation
Assay Technician

Process

Heat lead button in cupel. Lead oxidizes/absorbs. Recover glowing precious-metal prill.

Outcome

A recovered prill with continuous batch and position provenance.
09

Action

Finish & Analysis
Assay Technician

Process

Weigh prill (gravimetric) or dissolve (ICP/AAS). LIMS calculates provisional result. Do not report.

Outcome

A complete technical result ready for QA review.
10

Action

QA Review
Senior Chemist / QA

Process

Independent review of controls, blanks, duplicates, and mass balances. Authorize or hold.

Outcome

A documented approval, rejection, or hold for the analytical batch.
11

Action

Reporting & Release
LIMS Administrator

Process

Compile approved results. Generate immutable Certificate of Analysis (COA). Deliver to client.

Outcome

A released client-facing COA tied to the original drill-hole intervals.

The three pillars of lineage.

Identity Preservation

Field IDs are never deleted; they become immutable lineage metadata. Child samples (splits, pulps, charges) inherit parentage, ensuring a result traces back to the exact drill hole metre.

Physical Traceability

From oven shelf to furnace tray position to pulp archive box, every physical location is mapped. Contamination investigations rely on exact spatial history, not assumptions.

Immutable Release

Raw data and provisional results are locked at technical completion. Approved results become immutable Certificates of Analysis. Corrections create superseding versions, preserving the audit trail.

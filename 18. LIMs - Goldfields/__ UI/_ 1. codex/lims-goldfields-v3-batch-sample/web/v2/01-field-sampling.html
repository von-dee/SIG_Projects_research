User flow

Eight steps, in order.

01

Capture at the rig.

Capture drill hole, coordinates, and positioning at the rig. Confirm the campaign and hole type (HQ-2 / NQ-2) before sampling begins.

02

Tie to borehole ID.

Tie the sample submission to the borehole ID. Confirm drill hole type (HQ-2 / NQ-2) and link depth interval to the sampling plan.

03

Assign Batch ID.

Assign the sample to its primary Batch ID (e.g., B-700). Auto-generate sample codes and print sticker tags per sample and batch tag per bag.

04

Print & seal.

Auto-generate sample codes. Print sticker tags and batch tags. Seal the bag and complete the chain-of-custody for lab dispatch.

05

Sign chain-of-custody.

Sign off chain-of-custody for lab dispatch. The hand-off is recorded per sample; custody remains open until receiving clerk confirms receipt.

06

Verify manifest at receiving.

Verify the manifest at receiving. Flag mismatches for review. Samples that do not reconcile against the COC cannot proceed without a deviation record.

07

Weigh on digital scale.

Weigh the received sample using digital scale integration. Record gross wet mass against the Lab ID auto-generated at this step.

08

Accept, Hold, or Reject.

Accept, Hold, or Reject each sample with mandatory reason codes. No sample may enter the prep queue without a recorded disposition.

Controls & hand-off

Three rules, inviolable.

Batch identity is locked here.

Every transformation downstream logs the specific Sample ID (e.g., DDH-2214), Batch ID (e.g., B-700), and Tray ID. The Lab ID replaces manual numbering and is auto-generated at receiving — it can never be reused or reassigned.

Control · 01

Lab ID is auto-generated at receiving — replaces manual numbering. Mismatch between the bag label and manifest must be flagged before any ID is issued.

Control · 02

A sample cannot enter the prep queue without a recorded Accept / Hold / Reject disposition with a mandatory reason code. No silent intake.

Control · 03

Rig campaign data (drill holes, coordinates, positioning) is captured at the field stage. Borehole type (HQ-2 / NQ-2) is tied to every sample submission — not assumed.

Brief

Sequential crushing & pulp reject capture.

Role
Prep Technician. Operates Jaw Crusher 1 or 2 (2mm gap), executes prep methods (Grind/Screen Fire/Ultra Fine), crushes samples sequentially, and logs technician ID.
Record
Crushing worksheet recording equipment selection, 2mm jaw gap compliance, sample IDs, and Pulp Reject mass (1405g for DDH-2214).

User flow

Six steps for controlled sample preparation.

01

Select prep method & crusher.

Select the prep method (Grind, Screen Fire, or Ultra Fine) and choose Jaw Crusher 1 or 2 with verified 2mm jaw gap setting.

02

Scan sample & log technician.

Scan dried sample DDH-2214 from Batch B-700 and log the prep technician ID before initiating the crushing sequence.

03

Sequential jaw crushing.

Crush samples strictly one-at-a-time through the selected crusher to prevent cross-contamination and preserve sample identity.

04

Riffle split sample.

Pass crushed material through the riffle splitter to separate the assay working split from the coarse pulp reject portion.

05

Weigh & archive pulp reject.

Weigh the Pulp Reject (DDH-2214 = 1405g) and log its archive location in Batch B-700 storage rack.

06

Route to pulverizing.

Forward the working split to Stage 04 (Pulverizing). Note: Tray tracking remains suspended throughout prep stages.

Controls & hand-off

One sample at a time, strictly logged.

Technician accountability & equipment traceability.

Prep method selection (Grind, Screen Fire, Ultra Fine) and Jaw Crusher selection (1 or 2 with 2mm gap) must be explicitly recorded. Samples are processed sequentially one-at-a-time under logged technician sign-off.

Control · 01

Equipment selection (Jaw Crusher 1 vs 2 with 2mm jaw gap) and prep method selection are required before initiating crushing. Technician ID is linked to every single run.

Control · 02

Tray tracking is suspended during sample prep (Crushing & Pulverizing). Samples maintain individual identity and batch parentage (B-700) until re-loaded into trays at Fusion.

!
Sequential processing is strictly enforced.

Attempting to log multiple samples simultaneously or bypassing the 2mm jaw gap verification triggers an immediate hold. All Pulp Rejects (e.g. 1405g for DDH-2214) must be logged to Batch B-700 storage before hand-off.

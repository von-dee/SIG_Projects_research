User flow

Six steps for 50g charge weighing & reagent logging.

01

Scan pulp sample.

Scan fine pulp DDH-2214 from Batch B-700 and select the 50g Fire Assay method protocol.

02

Weigh 50g assay charge.

Weigh out the 50.00g (±0.20g) assay charge on analytical balance BAL-M01 and log actual mass (e.g. 50.02g).

03

Archive remaining pulp.

Bag and label remaining pulp (Pulp Archive DDH-2214 = 1330g) and store in Batch B-700 archive rack.

04

Add flux & litharge.

Add standard flux charge and litharge reagent into crucible along with the 50g assay charge.

05

Log reagents before fusion.

Record flux lot numbers, litharge batch, and reagent additions in LIMS before fusion is initiated.

06

Queue for fusion.

Forward mixed crucible charge to Stage 06 (Fusion). Note: Reagent logging position enforced prior to furnace entry.

Controls & hand-off

Reagent logging before fusion & pulp archive preservation.

Pre-fusion reagent verification & 50g charge standard.

Flux and reagent dosages (litharge, soda ash, borax) must be fully recorded in LIMS BEFORE fusion is initiated. The 50g charge and 1330g retained Pulp Archive are logged in tandem.

Control · 01

Reagent logging is enforced pre-fusion. Crucible charges cannot be loaded into Fusion Furnace 1 or 2 without completed flux and litharge reagent entries.

Control · 02

Retained Pulp Archive (1330g for DDH-2214) is mandatory. The remaining pulp must be logged and archived to Batch B-700 storage before hand-off.

!
Unlogged reagents block fusion loading.

Attempting to route crucible charges to fusion furnaces without confirmed pre-fusion reagent logging triggers an immediate system hold to ensure complete reagent traceability.

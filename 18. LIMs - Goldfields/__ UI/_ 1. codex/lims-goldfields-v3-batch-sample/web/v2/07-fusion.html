Brief

Fusion, lead buttons & tray integrity enforcement.

Role
Fire Assay Technician. Selects Fusion Furnace 1 or 2, loads multi-batch Tray 4, fuses charges at ~1000°C, weighs lead buttons, discards slag, and locks tray sequence.
Record
Fusion run sheet capturing furnace ID (Furnace 1/2), run temp (~1000°C), multi-batch map (Teal B-700 / Rust B-701), Tray 4 Position 14 (DDH-2214), and lead button mass.
↳ DDH-2214 / Tray 4 Pos 14 / Furnace 1 / Pb Button: 30.12g
Outcome
Lead buttons isolated and weighed, slag discarded, and tray integrity locked from Fusion to Cupellation (AUDIT-CRITICAL).
↳ Tray ID and sample sequence order are strictly locked and enforced into Cupellation.
User flow

Six steps for fusion & tray sequence locking.

01

Select Fusion Furnace 1 or 2.

Select Fusion Furnace 1 or 2 and configure the high-temperature fusion heating profile (~1000°C).

02

Load multi-batch Tray 4.

Load crucibles from Batch B-700 (Teal border) and Batch B-701 (Rust border) into Tray 4 (9×9 grid / 81 slots).

03

Verify position 14 for DDH-2214.

Scan and confirm DDH-2214 is placed at Tray 4 Position 14 in exact sequential order.

04

Fuse at ~1000°C.

Run the fusion fire cycle at ~1000°C to melt charge, reduce lead, and collect gold/silver into lead button.

05

Pour melt & discard slag.

Pour melt into conical mold, separate lead button from silicate slag, and discard slag residue.

06

Weigh button & lock tray map.

Weigh lead button (~30g) and lock Tray 4 layout. LIMS enforces identical tray position mapping into Stage 07 Cupellation.

Controls & hand-off

Enforce tray integrity from fusion to cupellation (AUDIT-CRITICAL).

Multi-furnace selection & unbroken tray position mapping.

Selection of Fusion Furnace 1 or 2 is recorded. Multi-batch visual borders (Teal B-700, Rust B-701) track mixed tray loading. Tray 4 Position 14 MUST be preserved into Cupellation without alteration.

Control · 01

Furnace selection (Furnace 1 vs 2) and combined batch tracking (B-700 Teal, B-701 Rust) are recorded for every fusion run at ~1000°C.

Control · 02

Tray integrity is locked from Fusion to Cupellation. The system strictly prevents changing tray IDs or position sequences between Fusion and Cupellation.

!
AUDIT VIOLATION: Position sequence changes are blocked.

Attempting to swap positions or alter tray layouts between Fusion and Cupellation violates audit integrity and causes an immediate system hold on the entire tray load.

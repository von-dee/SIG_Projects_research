Brief

Prill extraction, microbalance mass & litharge flux recycling.

Role
Fire Assay Technician. Selects Cupellation Furnace 1 or 2 (~950°C), loads lead buttons into exact Tray 4 fusion positions (Pos 14 DDH-2214), weighs prills on microbalance, and saves litharge as flux.
Record
Cupellation log recording furnace ID (1/2), run temp (~950°C), global cupel matrix, Tray 4 Pos 14 prill mass (2.14 mg), and saved litharge flux lot.
↳ DDH-2214 / Tray 4 Pos 14 / Prill: 2.14 mg / Saved Litharge: 28g
Outcome
Precious metal prill extracted and weighed on microbalance, litharge saved as flux for future fusion, and prills routed to wet chemistry digestion (not read directly).
↳ Prill is not read directly at cupellation; it proceeds to wet chemistry acid digestion.
User flow

Six steps for cupellation & prill extraction.

01

Select Cupellation Furnace 1 or 2.

Select Cupellation Furnace 1 or 2 and set operating temperature to ~950°C.

02

Set global cupel matrix.

Confirm standard cupel matrix configuration (set globally once, not per-batch).

03

Load Tray 4 in exact fusion position.

Place lead buttons into cupels preserving exact Tray 4 Position 14 mapping for DDH-2214.

04

Run cupellation at ~950°C.

Execute cupellation fire cycle; lead oxidizes and absorbs into bone ash cupel leaving precious metal prill.

05

Save litharge as flux.

Collect and log absorbed litharge residue for reuse as flux in future fusion batches.

06

Weigh prill & route to wet chem.

Weigh prill (2.14 mg) on microbalance and route to Stage 08 (Digestion). Note: Prill is NOT read directly.

Controls & hand-off

Global cupel matrix & litharge recycling.

Microbalance weighing & wet chemistry digestion hand-off.

Cupellation Furnace selection (Furnace 1 or 2) is required. Standard cupel matrix is set globally (not per-batch). Absorbed litharge is saved as flux, and prills MUST proceed to wet chemistry digestion.

Control · 01

Global cupel matrix setup: Set once globally for all cupellation runs. Furnace selection (Furnace 1 vs 2) is logged per cycle at ~950°C.

Control · 02

Prills are NOT read directly. They are weighed on a microbalance and routed to wet chemistry acid digestion for AAS/Gravimetric finish.

!
Direct prill reading bypass is blocked.

Attempting to bypass wet chemistry digestion and directly report un-digested prill readings causes an immediate system hold to enforce standardized digestion protocol.

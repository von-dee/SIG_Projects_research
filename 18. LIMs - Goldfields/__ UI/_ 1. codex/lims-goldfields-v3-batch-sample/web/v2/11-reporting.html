Controls & hand-off

Control · 01

Internal standards & blanks are stripped from exported COA documents. Client duplicates are reported as standard unknown field samples.

Control · 02

Released COAs are immutable artifacts. Any post-release correction requires issuing a formal superseding version (v2.0) with an audit trail.

!
Unapproved or un-locked samples cannot be exported.

The reporting engine enforces strict QA lock checks. Samples that have not passed Stage 10 QA/QC lock are physically excluded from certificate generation.

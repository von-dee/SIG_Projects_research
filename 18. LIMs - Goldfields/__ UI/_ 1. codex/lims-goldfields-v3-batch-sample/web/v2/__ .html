HGF Prestea Mine — Fire Assay LIMS
13-Stage End-to-End Workflow Overview

From drill core to Certificate of Analysis.

The complete 13-stage end-to-end fire assay journey for Heath Gold Fields (HGF) Prestea Mine. Every step preserves sample, batch, and tray lineage, enforces strict tray integrity from Fusion to Cupellation, and maintains an immutable audit graph.

Operational Phases
Sampling & Receiving (Stage 01)
Sample Prep: Drying, Crushing, Pulverizing (Stages 02–04)
Fire Assay: Weighing, Fusion, Cupellation (Stages 05–07)
Digestion, AAS Finish, QA Review & Reporting (Stages 08–13)
01

Stage 01

Field Sampling & Receiving
Field Geologist / Intake Clerk

Process

Sample core (DDH-2214), verify COC manifest, assign Batch B-700, log wet mass.

Outcome

Sealed, registered sample tied to Batch B-700 and field lineage.
02

Stage 02

Drying
Prep Technician

Process

Load Batch B-700 (250 samples) into Oven 1; split across Trays 1–4 at 81-sample limit at 105°C (6–8h).

Outcome

Dry core samples organized in 9×9 tray grids (Tray 1, Pos 14).
03

Stage 03

Crushing & Splitting
Prep Technician

Process

Jaw Crusher 1/2 (2mm gap), prep method selection, riffle split, archive Pulp Reject (1405g).

Outcome

Working split for pulverizing; Pulp Reject (1405g) archived under B-700.
04

Stage 04

Pulverizing & QC Sizing
Prep Technician

Process

Ring mill pulverizing, 1-in-20 QC sizing check (85% @2mm, 98% @75µm), auto QC graphing.

Outcome

Fine homogeneous pulp ready for assaying, with locked QC sizing curves.
05

Stage 05

Weighing & Fluxing
Assay Technician

Process

Weigh 50g assay charge, retain Pulp Archive (1330g), log flux & litharge BEFORE fusion.

Outcome

Toleranced 50g charge fluxed for furnace; Pulp Archive (1330g) retained.
06

Stage 06

Fusion
Fire Assay Technician

Process

Fusion Furnace 1/2 (~1000°C), multi-batch Tray 4 (Teal B-700 / Rust B-701), weigh lead button, lock tray map.

Outcome

Lead button recovered; tray sequence locked for Cupellation (AUDIT-CRITICAL).
07

Stage 07

Cupellation
Fire Assay Technician

Process

Cupellation Furnace 1/2 (~950°C), global cupel matrix, preserve Tray 4 Pos 14, weigh prill (2.14 mg), save litharge flux.

Outcome

Precious metal prill weighed on microbalance; routed to wet chem digestion.
08

Stage 08

Acid Digestion
Wet Chemist

Process

Digest prill with aqua regia acid solution under heat and dilute to volumetric flask concentration.

Outcome

Digested prill solution ready for instrumental AAS finish analysis.
09

Stage 09

AAS / Gravimetric Finish
Instrument Analyst

Process

Full-page AAS view, select instrument (AAS-02), furnace mode, prill count, and method. Log reading (3.42 g/t Au).

Outcome

AAS absorbance captured and Au grade calculated; sent to QA review.
10

Stage 10

QA/QC Review & Lock
Supervisor / QA Manager ONLY

Process

Review monthly blanks & bias graphs, lock QC figures (immutable), strip internal standards & blanks, report duplicates as unknowns.

Outcome

QC figures permanently locked; client report data sanitized and approved.
11

Stage 11

Reporting & Export
LIMS Admin / QA Manager

Process

Generate printable Certificate of Analysis (COA-HGF-2026-078) matching HGF field layout specifications with digital signature hash.

Outcome

Official COA certificate generated and released to client portal.
12

Stage 12

Client Portal Release
LIMS Administrator

Process

Deliver signed COA certificate and raw data packages to Heath Gold Fields client portal repository.

Outcome

Client receives verified analytical results with full chain-of-custody backing.
13

Stage 13

Archive & Audit Graph
QA Auditor / Database Engine

Process

Permanently seal end-to-end lineage graph (DDH-2214 → B-700 → Tray 4 Pos 14 → Pulp Archive 1330g → COA-HGF-2026-078).

Outcome

Immutable, audit-ready analytical record archived permanently.

The three pillars of HGF LIMS traceability.

1. Strict Tray & Position Integrity

Trays enforce an 81-sample limit (9×9 grid). From Fusion to Cupellation, sample positions (e.g. DDH-2214 at Tray 4 Pos 14) are locked and immutable, preventing any spatial mix-ups.

2. Pre-Fusion Reagents & Archive Rules

Flux and litharge reagents must be logged before fusion entry. Coarse Pulp Rejects (1405g) and Retained Pulp Archives (1330g) are mapped to Batch B-700 storage.

3. QA Lock & Report Data Hygiene

QC figure locking is restricted strictly to Supervisors & QA Managers. Internal standards and lab blanks are automatically stripped from client reports, and client duplicates are reported as unknown field samples.

# 01 — Field sampling

**Role:** Field geologist
**Record:** [Sample submission / chain-of-custody form](../forms/01-sample-submission-chain-of-custody.md)
**Outcome:** A sealed, uniquely identified field sample tied to its drill-hole interval.

## User flow

1. Locate the approved drill core interval and confirm hole ID, from depth, and to depth against the sampling plan.
2. Cut or collect the approximately 3 kg interval using site procedure. Keep sample boundaries aligned to the recorded depths.
3. Place all material in a suitable bag; apply a durable tag/barcode bearing the field sample ID, hole ID, and from–to interval.
4. Inspect the bag and tag for legibility, closure, and damage. Record sample condition and any exceptions.
5. Complete the submission/chain-of-custody form, including collector and collection time, then sign the custody hand-off.
6. Seal the bag and dispatch it to the laboratory. The next custodian acknowledges receipt; the custody remains open until intake.

## Form fields entered

Identity: project/client, hole ID, interval from/to and units, field sample ID, sample type.
Custody: collector, collection date/time, bag/tag/seal ID, dispatched-to party, transfer timestamp, sender/receiver signatures.
Context: expected mass, sample condition, collection method, hazards/handling notes, comments and attachments.

## Controls and hand-off

- Do not combine intervals under one tag or reuse a field sample ID.
- A damaged/unsealed bag must be recorded before transfer.
- Receiving uses the field sample ID and custody event to accept, hold, or reject the sample.

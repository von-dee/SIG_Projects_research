# 08 — Cupellation

**Role:** Assay technician  
**Record:** [Cupellation log](../forms/08-cupellation-log.md)  
**Outcome:** A recovered prill with continuous batch and position provenance.

## User flow

1. Scan the lead button and confirm matching fusion batch, tray position, and cupellation method.
2. Place the button onto an identified cupel; record cupel position and cupellation furnace/run.
3. Run the cycle using the approved program and record load/start/end details.
4. Verify lead absorption is complete, recover the precious-metal prill, and assign the prill ID/container position.
5. Record recovery condition and route the prill to the selected finish method.

## Form fields entered

Lead button ID, fusion batch reference, cupel ID/position, cupellation furnace ID/program, start/end timestamps, operator, prill ID, prill condition/recovery status, and deviations.

## Controls and hand-off

- A missing or abnormal prill triggers an assay investigation rather than an inferred result.
- Prill labels retain the original sample and batch provenance.

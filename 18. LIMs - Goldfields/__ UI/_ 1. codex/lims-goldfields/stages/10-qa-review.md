# 10 — QA review

**Role:** Senior chemist / QA  
**Record:** [QA review checklist](../forms/10-qa-review-checklist.md)  
**Outcome:** A documented approval, rejection, or hold for the analytical batch.

## User flow

1. Open the completed analytical batch and confirm all required stage records are present and complete.
2. Review standards, blanks, and duplicates against current control charts and method acceptance limits.
3. Review preparation mass-balance flags, outliers, failed/repeated sieve checks, furnace/cupellation deviations, and calculation flags.
4. Select a disposition: approve, hold pending investigation, reject/rerun, or approve with documented qualification where policy permits.
5. Electronically sign the checklist. Approved results become reportable; other dispositions remain blocked from release.

## Form fields entered

Batch ID, reviewer, review date/time, control IDs/results/limits/chart references, duplicate precision, blank assessment, prep mass-balance assessment, deviation/investigation references, disposition, reason, rerun scope, electronic signature.

## Controls and hand-off

- QA reviewer must be authorised and independent according to laboratory policy.
- Approval is a batch-level gate; reporting reads only the latest approved result version.

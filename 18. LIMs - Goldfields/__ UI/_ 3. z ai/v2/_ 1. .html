SECTION 02 · MATERIAL & DATA FLOW

The sample loses mass.
The record gains edges.

A 3 kg interval of drill core reduces to a prill weighing micrograms. At each handoff, material branches off — rejects, archive pulps, slag, absorbed lead — and a signed record becomes a new edge in the lineage graph. Path thickness tracks remaining mass.

PRINCIPLE 01
No re-keying.
Operator, timestamp and instrument are captured at the moment of action by the LIMS or instrument client. The graph ingests the structured record — nobody copies values between systems.
PRINCIPLE 02
One form, one edge.
Each completed record corresponds to exactly one edge. The edge inherits the form's identity (form type, version, signed PDF) and its captured fields become edge properties.
PRINCIPLE 03
Branches are first-class.
Rejects, archive pulps and slag are not "lost" — they are separate downstream nodes with their own edges. Any stored material can be re-entered into the workflow and re-traced.

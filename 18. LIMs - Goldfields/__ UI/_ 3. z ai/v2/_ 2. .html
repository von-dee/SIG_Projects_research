SECTION 00 · THE BATCH MODEL

A batch is not a bag.
It's a furnace load.

Samples arrive individually but are processed in batches whose size is dictated by the physical capacity of each instrument — the oven, the furnace tray, the muffle, the ICP autosampler. Understanding batch boundaries is essential: a batch is the unit of QC, the unit of release, and the unit of re-run.

QC material is interspersed at fixed positions — typically every 10th and 20th position, plus first and last — so control charts can detect drift across the tray. The batch ID links all 50 positions to a single furnace batch sheet and a single QA review.

PRINCIPLE 01
No re-keying.
Operator, timestamp, instrument, batch_id and sample_id are captured at the moment of action. The graph ingests the structured record — nobody copies values between systems.
PRINCIPLE 02
One form, many edges.
A batch-level form yields one edge per batch. A sample-level form yields one edge per sample — up to 50 edges from a single form. Both carry the same batch_id for traceability.
PRINCIPLE 03
Branches are first-class.
Rejects, archive pulps and slag are separate downstream nodes with their own edges. Any stored material can be re-entered into the workflow and re-traced to its origin batch.

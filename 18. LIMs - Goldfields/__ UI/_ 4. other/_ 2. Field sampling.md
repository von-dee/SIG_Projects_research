```markdown
# 01 — Field sampling

**Role:** Field geologist
**Record:** [Sample submission / chain-of-custody form](../forms/01-sample-submission-chain-of-custody.md)
**Outcome:** A sealed, uniquely identified field sample tied to its drill-hole interval.

> **Batch handling:** The dispatch creates a submission batch. Each bag is an
> individual member line with its own field ID, interval, and seal — a shared
> dispatch never merges those identities.

## User flow

1. **Locate the interval.** Find the approved drill core interval and confirm
   hole ID, from depth, and to depth against the sampling plan.
2. **Cut or collect the sample.** Collect the approximately 3 kg interval
   using site procedure, keeping sample boundaries aligned to the recorded
   depths.
3. **Bag and tag.** Place all material in a suitable bag and apply a durable
   tag/barcode bearing the field sample ID, hole ID, and from–to interval.
4. **Inspect.** Check the bag and tag for legibility, closure, and damage.
   Record sample condition and any exceptions.
5. **Complete the form.** Fill in the submission/chain-of-custody form —
   including collector and collection time — and sign the custody hand-off.
6. **Seal and dispatch.** Seal the bag and dispatch it to the laboratory.
   The next custodian acknowledges receipt; custody remains open until
   intake.

## Form fields entered

| Group      | Fields                                                                                                    |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identity   | Project/client, hole ID, interval from/to and units, field sample ID, sample type                        |
| Custody    | Collector, collection date/time, bag/tag/seal ID, dispatched-to party, transfer timestamp, sender/receiver signatures |
| Context    | Expected mass, sample condition, collection method, hazards/handling notes, comments and attachments     |

## Controls and hand-off

- **One tag per interval.** Do not combine intervals under one tag, and never
  reuse a field sample ID.
- **Condition before transfer.** A damaged or unsealed bag must be recorded
  before transfer.
- **Receipt decisions.** Receiving uses the field sample ID and the custody
  event to accept, hold, or reject the sample.
```

Short version: **a drill campaign groups *holes* on the planning side; Members · DSP lists the *samples* inside a specific dispatch.** They sit at opposite ends of the data chain.

(I'm reading "DSP" as **dispatch** — the members view of a dispatch record, i.e. the individual bag lines. If your system uses DSP for something else, tell me and I'll re-aim.)

| | **Drill campaigns** | **Members · DSP** |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | A grouping entity for a drilling program | The member lines of one dispatch record |
| **Groups** | Drill holes (and their planned intervals, rigs, targets, date range, budget/metres) | Individual sample bags, each with its own field ID, hole, from–to, seal |
| **When it's created** | Before drilling starts | When samples are bagged, sealed, and ready to ship |
| **Answers** | "Which program did this hole come from, and how is it tracking against plan?" | "What exactly is in this shipment, in what condition, and who has custody?" |
| **Used by** | Geologists / drill planners / project managers | Sample custodian / dispatcher / lab at intake |
| **Lifecycle** | Long-lived — spans weeks or a season | Short-lived — opens at dispatch, closes at lab intake |

**How they connect** — everything upstream flows into everything downstream:

```
Campaign → Hole → Interval → Sample → Dispatch member → Dispatch → Lab intake
```

A sample becomes a dispatch member carrying its hole, interval, and (inherited via the hole) its campaign context. Two consequences worth knowing:

- **Many-to-one in both directions, but differently:** one campaign contains many holes; one dispatch can contain samples from *many holes* — and typically even across campaigns — but each sample is a member of exactly one dispatch.
- **The dispatch never merges identities.** This is the same rule from the SOP: the DSP is the batch, but the Members view is where each bag stays an individual line with its own field ID, interval, and seal. If two bags from the same hole and interval somehow end up in one dispatch, they're still two member lines.

**Practical difference when you're clicking around:** if you want to know "how is the 2024 RC program tracking, which holes are complete" — that's Drill campaigns. If you want to know "what did we ship to the lab on Tuesday and is anything missing or damaged" — that's Members on the dispatch.

```markdown
# 02 — Receiving & login

**Role:** Receiving clerk
**Record:** [Sample intake log (LIMS)](../forms/02-sample-intake-log.md)
**Outcome:** The physical bag becomes a registered lab sample with a barcode.

> **Batch handling:** Work from the submission manifest, but accept, hold, or
> reject each bag independently. The receiving-run membership records the
> lab barcode and current location for every accepted line.

## User flow

1. **Find the submission.** Receive the shipment and locate the matching
   submission/COC entry.
2. **Verify against the manifest.** Compare bag label, hole ID, and from–to
   depth with the submission; inspect the seal and bag condition.
3. **Register the sample.** Scan or enter the field sample ID and create the
   lab sample ID/barcode. Preserve the link to the field sample and the
   original interval.
4. **Weigh.** Weigh the unopened/received bag as defined by the lab method
   and record gross wet mass and the balance ID.
5. **Record exceptions.** Log discrepancies, damage, missing information, or
   unsuitable condition. Place mismatches on intake hold.
6. **Label and route.** Print/apply the lab barcode and route accepted
   material to the drying queue and location.

## Form fields entered

| Group               | Fields                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identity & linkage  | Field sample ID, generated lab sample ID, barcode, submission/COC reference                                   |
| Intake verification | Received date/time, receiver, hole/interval verification, condition, discrepancy code, intake status          |
| Measurement & routing | Gross wet mass and unit, balance ID, current location                                                       |

## Controls and hand-off

- **Barcode is now the operational ID.** The lab barcode becomes the primary
  operational identifier from this point; the field identity remains
  immutable lineage metadata.
- **No drying on hold.** Only accepted samples enter the drying queue, and
  holds require an accountable resolution.
```

Same conventions as 01: fields collapsed into a grouped table, batch handling as a standing callout, bold lead-ins on the flow steps.

Worth noting how it closes the loop on the earlier discussion: this stage is where the **accept / hold / reject** decision that doc 01 defers to the receiver actually happens, and it's where the **dispatch (DSP) custody chain closes** — the receiving-run membership is the lab-side answer to the dispatch's member lines. Also note the identifier hand-off: field ID in, lab barcode out, field ID preserved as immutable lineage — which is why the intake log carries both.

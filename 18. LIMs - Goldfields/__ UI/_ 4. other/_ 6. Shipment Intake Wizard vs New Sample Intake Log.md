Short version: **the Shipment Intake Wizard is the guided check-in *process* for a delivery; the Sample Intake Log is the *record* that process writes — one line per sample.** Same split as your other pairs: wizard = workflow, log = data.

| | **Shipment Intake Wizard** | **New Sample Intake Log** |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Guided workflow for processing an inbound shipment against its submission/COC | The LIMS record of a sample's registration |
| **Operates on** | The whole dispatch — walks the manifest member line by member line | A single sample/bag |
| **When** | When the physical shipment arrives at the dock | Per bag at login, or manually for exceptions |
| **Answers** | "Did everything on the manifest arrive? In what condition? Accept, hold, or reject each bag?" | "What is this sample's lab identity, mass, condition, and location?" |
| **Output** | A receiving run: per-line decisions, plus status/custody close-out on the dispatch | A registered lab sample with barcode; field ID preserved as lineage |
| **Driven by** | Reconciliation — needs a submission to check against | Identity — needs a field sample ID to register |
| **Lifecycle** | One run per shipment | One permanent record per sample |

**How they connect** — the wizard is the writer, the log is the destination:

```
Dispatch (DSP) → shipment arrives
      ↓
Shipment Intake Wizard — reconcile against manifest, accept/hold/reject per bag
      ↓ writes one line per bag
Sample intake log — lab sample ID, barcode, gross wet mass, condition, location
      ↓ accepted only
Drying queue
```

Mapped onto doc 02:

- **Wizard ≈ steps 1–2 plus the batch-handling rule** (work from the manifest, decide per bag independently). It's also where the **accept / hold / reject** decision deferred from doc 01 actually executes, and where the dispatch custody chain closes.
- **Intake log ≈ what steps 3–6 produce** — its "Form fields entered" list *is* essentially the log's schema, and the accepted lines become the receiving-run membership.

Two consequences worth knowing:

- **A sample can get a log entry without the wizard** — walk-ins, re-registration after a resolved hold, samples outside any submission. But then nothing closes the dispatch loop automatically, so linking it back to the COC is a manual job.
- **The wizard can't manufacture an identity.** A bag that resolves to no manifest line and no field sample ID is a hold, not a log entry — that's the discrepancy path, not the registration path.

**Practical difference when clicking around:** "A truck just arrived, I need to check it against the COC and check bags in" → wizard. "This held bag's discrepancy is resolved and needs registering" or "a sample arrived with no submission" → New Sample Intake Log.

One alternate reading depending on your platform: "New Sample Intake Log" might *open* a receiving run that you then populate — with the wizard being the shipment-driven way to fill it and manual entry the exception path.

# Field Geologist: Sample Submission & Chain-of-Custody (COC) Implementation

We have implemented the full **Field Geologist** workflow and **Sample Submission / Chain-of-Custody (COC) Form** in [SIG-Aurumlims/frontend](file:///Users/selasie/Documents/GitHub/SIG-Aurumlims/frontend), adhering to mining-grade JORC (2012) compliance and the system architecture.

---

## Key Features Delivered

### 1. Domain Data & Controls Layer
- Defined TypeScript domain models in [field-sampling-data.ts](file:///Users/selasie/Documents/GitHub/SIG-Aurumlims/frontend/src/lib/field-sampling-data.ts):
  - `SampleSubmissionForm`: Header records, borehole identifiers, batch status, hazard notes, sample member rows, dual custody transfer logs, and blockchain tamper-evident seal hashes.
  - `SampleLineItem`: Unique `fieldSampleId`, `holeId`, from–to depth interval, unit, sample type, ~3 kg expected vs actual mass, `bagTagSealId`, condition, lithology, and barcode verification.
  - `CustodyTransferRecord`: Sender/receiver identities, location, transfer timestamp, tamper seal condition (`Yes/No`), and SHA-256 cryptographic signatures.
  - Pre-populated realistic drill campaigns for **Pit-A2-East (`A2E-114`)**, **Pit-A2-West (`A2W-007`)**, and **Pit-A1-North (`A1N-042`)**.
  - Validation engine enforcing strict rules: duplicate Sample ID prevention, depth interval continuity (`to > from`), mass deviation alerts, and tamper seal verification.

### 2. Sample Submission & Chain-of-Custody (COC) Modal
- Created [SampleSubmissionModal.tsx](file:///Users/selasie/Documents/GitHub/SIG-Aurumlims/frontend/src/components/lims/field-sampling/SampleSubmissionModal.tsx):
  - **Header Section**: Submission/COC ID, batch ID/version, client/project, geologist info, drill rig, pit area, and date opened.
  - **Batch Member Lines Grid**: Dynamic table with inline editing, row addition, interval split calculator, mass tracker, condition selector (`Intact`, `Damaged`, `Wet/Leaking`, `Unsealed`), and row duplication.
  - **Custody Hand-off Section**: Dual electronic signature stamps (Sender Geologist & Receiver Courier) with cryptographic SHA-256 hash generation and seal integrity verification.
  - **Hazards & Special Handling**: Checkbox checklist for arsenopyrite, high moisture, coarse gold nugget effect, and friable core.
  - **Printable Dispatch Manifest**: Full official JORC-compliant COC manifest view ready for printing and archival.

### 3. Weatherproof Bag Tag Barcode Generator
- Created [BagTagBarcodeModal.tsx](file:///Users/selasie/Documents/GitHub/SIG-Aurumlims/frontend/src/components/lims/field-sampling/BagTagBarcodeModal.tsx):
  - Renders durable bag tags with 1D Code-128 barcode simulation and 2D QR code.
  - Single tag inspection or batch print sheet mode with print styling.

### 4. 6-Step SOP Rig-Side Capture Wizard
- Created [SamplingWizardModal.tsx](file:///Users/selasie/Documents/GitHub/SIG-Aurumlims/frontend/src/components/lims/field-sampling/SamplingWizardModal.tsx):
  - Guides field geologists step-by-step through the standard operating procedure:
    1. *Locate & confirm drill interval* against plan
    2. *Cut & collect ~3 kg sample*
    3. *Bag & tag with unique barcode*
    4. *Inspect condition & record exceptions*
    5. *Sign custody hand-off*
    6. *Apply tamper-evident seal & dispatch*

### 5. Interactive Field Geologist Hub
- Upgraded [FieldSamplingPage.tsx](file:///Users/selasie/Documents/GitHub/SIG-Aurumlims/frontend/src/components/lims/pages/FieldSamplingPage.tsx):
  - Real-time KPIs (*Samples today*, *Open dispatches*, *Active boreholes*, *Avg capture time*).
  - Active drill interval plan explorer with visual depth progress bars.
  - Submission batches table with direct access to COC forms, printable manifests, and barcode tags.

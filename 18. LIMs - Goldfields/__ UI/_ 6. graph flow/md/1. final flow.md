# HGF End-to-End Fire Assay Workflow (13 Stages)

This document outlines the confirmed end-to-end assay workflow, stakeholder change requests, and audit requirements for the Heath Gold Fields (HGF) Prestea Mine LIMS prototype.

---

## 1. Field Sampling & Receiving

**Stage Type:** Input / Field & Receiving Clerk Station

**Workflow Details:**

* **Field Geologist:** Rig campaign capture (drill holes, coordinates, positioning). Sample submission tied to borehole ID; type HQ-2 / NQ-2.
* **Tagging:** Auto-generated codes + printed sticker tags per sample, batch tag per bag.
* **Chain of Custody:** Handover sign-off to lab dispatch.
* **Receiving Clerk:** Manifest verification with "mismatch for review" flagging. Lab ID auto-generated (replaces manual numbering).
* **Weighing:** Digital scale integration—weight captured to each sample ID.
* **Routing:** Accept / Hold / Reject with mandatory reason codes. Printable worksheet; searchable intake log by any ID.

---

## 2. Drying

**Stage Type:** Active Path

**Workflow Details:**

* **Operation:** DRY
* **Instrument:** Oven (Max 81 samples/tray, 3 ovens available, max 8-hour cycle)
* **Mass Balance:** Input: 3000 g (Received) → Output: 2810 g (Dried)
* **Note:** Moisture driven off before crushing.

**HGF Change Requests & Notes:**

* Redesign selection: select batch first, then specific samples/tray to load into the oven.
* Handle variable batch sizes (250–350 samples) with 81-sample tray limit; track loaded ranges.
* Record drying start time per sample (max 8-hour cycle, 3 ovens, temperature setpoint).
* Remove "Drain vessel" field from drying form (delete or set once globally).
* Keep operator pre-transfer checklist before moving to the next stage.

---

## 3. Crushing & Splitting

**Stage Type:** Active Path

**Workflow Details:**

* **Operation:** CRUSH_SPLIT
* **Instrument:** Jaw Crusher (2 on site, 2mm gap clearance)
* **Mass Balance:** Input: 2810 g (Dried) → Output: 1405 g (Crushed) + 1405 g (Pulp Reject)
* **Note:** Sequential one-sample-at-a-time processing (batch -> sample).

**HGF Change Requests & Notes:**

* Capture the technician who performed crushing & splitting at every step (accountability).
* Add crusher selection (2 on site) with jaw gap clearance setting (2 mm).
* Sequential one-sample-at-a-time processing; UI must follow batch-then-sample order consistently.
* Add a selectable preparation method list (Grind, Screen Fire, Ultra Fine, etc.).

### ➡️ Pulp Reject (Archive)

* **Mass:** 1405 g
* **Note:** Retained for reference after the crushing and splitting stage.

---

## 4. Pulverizing

**Stage Type:** Active Path

**Workflow Details:**

* **Operation:** PULVERISE
* **Instrument:** Ring mill RM1
* **Mass Balance:** Input: 1405 g (Crushed) → Output: 1380 g (Pulp, 85% passing 75µm)
* **Note:** Pulverizing to powdery form enhances reactivity for fire assay.

**HGF Change Requests & Notes:**

* QC sizing analysis: 1 in every 20 samples.
* Accept entered sieve figures and auto-generate QC graphs for 2 mm crusher check (~85% passing).
* Auto-generate QC graphs for 75 µm pulverizing check (plant performance cited at ~98%).
* HGF to send reference graphs for format replication.

---

## 5. Weighing & Fluxing

**Stage Type:** Active Path

**Workflow Details:**

* **Operation:** WEIGH_OUT
* **Instrument:** Analytical balance B7
* **Mass Balance:** Input: 1380 g (Pulp) → Output: 50 g (Assay charge) + 1330 g (Pulp Archive)
* **Note:** Most important is weight of pulp. Weigh before assay. Fluxing added as configuration.

**HGF Change Requests & Notes:**

* Capture flux & reagents before fusion is initiated.
* Correct reagent capture position/timing in the fusion flow.

### ➡️ Pulp Archive (Archive)

* **Mass:** 1330 g
* **Note:** Bulk of the pulp survives as archived material. Lost or delayed samples revisitable; data pullable at any time.

---

## 6. Fusion

**Stage Type:** Active Path

**Workflow Details:**

* **Operation:** FUSE
* **Instrument:** Fusion Furnace 1/2
* **Mass Balance:** Inputs: 50 g (Assay charge) + 150 g (Flux: Litharge, soda ash, borax, silica) → Outputs: 25 g (Lead button) + 170 g (Slag)
* **Note:** Main purpose: get the lead button.

**HGF Change Requests & Notes:**

* Furnace selection fields: Fusion Furnace 1/2 must be selectable.
* **Audit-Critical:** Enforce tray integrity from fusion to cupellation: same tray, same sequential order. System must not allow changes to tray sequence at this point.

### ➡️ Lead Button Weighed (Active)

* **Mass:** 25 g obtained. Separated from slag, cleaned, hammered into a cube.

### ➡️ Slag (Discarded)

* **Mass:** 170 g. Discarded, but mass logged for balance check.

---

## 7. Cupellation

**Stage Type:** Active Path

**Workflow Details:**

* **Operation:** CUPEL
* **Instrument:** Cupellation Furnace 1/2
* **Mass Balance:** Input: 25 g (Lead button) → Outputs: 0.00073 g (Prill) + 24.99927 g (Litharge)
* **Note:** Tray & sequential order carried through from fusion — never altered.

**HGF Change Requests & Notes:**

* Furnace selection fields: Cupellation Furnace 1/2 must be selectable.
* Cupel matrix type set once globally: standard cupel used throughout; remove repetitive per-batch steps.
* Prill is not read directly — sent to wet chemistry digestion.

### ➡️ Prill (Active)

* **Mass:** 730 µg. Weighed on a microbalance.

### ➡️ Litharge Saved (Archive)

* **Mass:** ~25 g. Saved as flux for future fusion runs.

---

## 8. Digestion

**Stage Type:** Active Path

**Workflow Details:**

* **Operation:** DIGEST
* **Instrument:** Acid digestion (Wet chemistry)
* **Mass Balance:** Input: 0.00073 g (Prill) → Output: Solution (Unmeasured)

**HGF Change Requests & Notes:**

* Split Digestion & AAS Reading into two separate stages (currently captured as one).

---

## 9. AAS / Gravimetric Finish

**Stage Type:** Active Path

**Workflow Details:**

* **Operation:** AAS_READ
* **Instrument:** AAS / Gravimetric parting
* **Mass Balance:** Input: Solution → Output: Reading (Unmeasured)
* **Note:** Atomic Absorption Spectrometry reading or gravimetric parting alternative finish.

**HGF Change Requests & Notes:**

* Move the Finish (AAS/Gravimetric) step to a full-page view (same layout as other stations).
* Instrument, furnace, prill count and method all selectable.
* Cross-check fields against documents HGF shared.

---

## 10. QA/QC & Reporting

**Stage Type:** Final Reported Result

**Workflow Details:**

* **Operation:** REPORT
* **Instrument:** QA Console & AAS
* **Operator:** Supervisor / Manager only.
* **Output:** Final Grade (e.g., 14.6 g/t Au, Printable Report)
* **Note:** Two-Tier UI: Simple forms for operators, full oversight dashboard for admin.

**HGF Change Requests & Notes:**

* **Audit Rule:** Lock QC figures—once entered/picked, they cannot be changed by anyone.
* Strip internal standards & lab blanks from client reports (HGF does not report its own standard values to clients).
* Report client duplicates as unknown samples (client-run blind checks appear as normal samples, never flagged as duplicates).
* Record editing restricted to Supervisors & Managers only (sample recovery).

**QA/QC Reporting Steps:**

1. Select → Get graph to be presented → Work sheet.
2. Select date from and to → select blanks.
3. Give graph report → Give all values for that standard.
4. Bring out all outliers.
5. QA review, sign off, generate results printable.
6. Take off all standards → blanks & standards are taken out.
7. Sent to Boss / Quality Control.

**Required Graph Outputs:**

* **Monthly blanks graph:** Select a month (1st–31st) → system pulls all blank values and generates QC graph against set limit files.
* **Per-standard graphs:** Select a standard's name → system retrieves every recorded value and plots performance (positive/negative bias detection).
* **Worksheet control positions:** Layout reserves defined positions for standards, duplicates, and blanks.
* **Client-facing performance claims:** Track % passing 75 µm (target ≥85%; plant performance cited at ~98%).
* **Report export format:** Match the field layout of the sample reports HGF shared at the previous meeting.

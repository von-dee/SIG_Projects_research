The gold room is the high-security, final recovery stage of a gold processing plant, where concentrated gold (from electrowinning cells or Merrill-Crowe circuits) is melted into bullion dorè bars.

Because operations here deal directly with physical gold, software used in the gold room focuses on **chain of custody, assay management, mass balance accounting, security, and enterprise resource integration**.

### 1. Metallurgical Accounting & Mass Balance Software

These platforms track the inventory of gold entering and leaving the gold room, monitoring fine gold ounces, slag losses, and furnace recovery efficiency.

* **METS (Metallurgical Accounting System):** Tracks physical metal throughput, pour sheets, and gold-in-circuit (GIC) balances.
* **Datamine Reconcilor:** Reconciles gold production data across the value chain, comparing estimated resource ounces against actual poured dore weight and assay results.
* **SysCAD:** Simulates and models hydrometallurgical processing to monitor elution and electrowinning efficiency leading up to the smelting phase.

### 2. Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS)

LIMS software manages the sample tracking, fire assay reporting, and chemical composition analysis of the gold buttons, slag, and final bullion bars.

* **Labsols Gold Mining LIMS:** Purpose-built for precious metal production labs; integrates directly with analytical scales, XRF analyzers, and Atomic Absorption Spectroscopy (AAS) instruments.
* **Core-100 LIMS / Thermo Fisher SampleManager:** Standard industry LIMS used to maintain automated audit trails for bullion bar assays.

### 3. Bullion Tracking & Security Management

High-security digital custody solutions track individual gold bars from the moment they are poured and stamped to their transit to refiners.

* **aXedras Mine Suite (xTrace):** Digitize assay results, bar weights, and gold origins using cryptographic ledger tools to ensure compliance and traceability.
* **Custom Vault & Pour Tracking Systems:** Internal custom-built inventory apps integrated with barcode/RFID scanners and digital scales to log bar identification, gross weight, tare weight, and melt sequence.
* **Security & Access Control (e.g., Genetec, Milestone):** Advanced video management software (VMS) with access logs, biometric entry systems, and synchronized video timestamps for every pour and vault access event.

### 4. Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Integration

Gold room pour records feed directly into enterprise systems for financial reporting, royalty calculations, and logistics.

* **SAP S/4HANA (Mining Solutions):** Used to log final production inventory, record metal sales, and manage precious metals accounting.
* **Intuitive / Pronto Xi Mining:** ERP systems customized for mining site logistics and mineral asset tracking.

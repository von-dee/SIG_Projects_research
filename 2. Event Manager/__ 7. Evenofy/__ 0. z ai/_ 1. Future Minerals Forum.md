Hosting an event on the scale and caliber of the **Future Minerals Forum (FMF)**—which attracts global heads of state, mining CEOs, investors, and NGOs to discuss critical minerals, ESG, and the future of mining—requires a highly structured, professional organization. 

To pull this off, you need to divide your organization into specialized **Departments**, define the **Roles** within them, and outline the specific **Activities** each team is responsible for. 

Here is your comprehensive blueprint:

---

### 1. Executive & Steering Committee Department
This is the brain of the event, responsible for the overall vision, high-level stakeholder management, and financial oversight.

*   **Team Roles:** Event Director, Chief of Staff, Steering Committee Chair.
*   **Team Member Roles:** 
    *   *Event Director:* Ultimate decision-maker.
    *   *Chief of Staff:* Manages the director’s schedule and cross-departmental alignment.
    *   *Advisors:* Subject matter experts on mining and geopolitics.
*   **Activities:**
    *   Define the event theme, mission, and key messaging (e.g., "Future Minerals for the Energy Transition").
    *   Secure high-level keynote speakers (Ministers of Mining, Fortune 500 CEOs).
    *   Approve the master budget and overall event design.
    *   Host the VIP pre-event gala and private closed-door roundtables.

### 2. Content & Program Department
Unlike generic events, an FMF-style event requires top-tier, highly researched intellectual content.

*   **Team Roles:** Content Director, Curator, Speaker Liaison.
*   **Team Member Roles:**
    *   *Content Director:* Sets the agenda and tracks industry trends.
    *   *Speaker Manager:* Handles all speaker logistics, travel, and briefing documents.
    *   *Scriptwriter:* Writes run-of-show (ROS), moderator briefs, and VIP intros.
*   **Activities:**
    *   Research and design plenary sessions, breakouts, and panel discussions (e.g., "The Geopolitics of Critical Minerals").
    *   Invite and secure moderators, industry experts, and academics.
    *   Collect, format, and QA all presentation slides.
    *   Write session synopses for the event app and marketing materials.

### 3. VIP & Delegation Management Department
An FMF-style event involves heavy government and C-suite attendance. Protocol is everything.

*   **Team Roles:** Head of VIP & Protocol, Delegation Managers.
*   **Team Member Roles:**
    *   *Protocol Officer:* Ensures diplomatic and governmental etiquette is followed.
    *   *VIP Hosts:* Assigned 1-on-1 to high-level guests.
    *   *Delegation Coordinators:* Liaises with country embassies and corporate teams.
*   **Activities:**
    *   Manage VIP airport pickups (private aviation coordination).
    *   Secure VIP hotel room blocks and manage hospitality suites.
    *   Organize bilateral meeting rooms for government-to-government (G2G) or business-to-business (B2B) meetings.
    *   Manage strict seating arrangements based on protocol rankings.

### 4. Partnerships & Commercial Department
This team generates revenue and brings in the big industry players.

*   **Team Roles:** Head of Sales, Sponsorship Managers, Exhibition Sales.
*   **Team Member Roles:**
    *   *Key Account Manager:* Closes six-figure sponsorship deals with major mining companies (e.g., Barrick, Rio Tinto).
    *   *Exhibition Manager:* Sells and allocates floor space for country pavilions and corporate booths.
    *   *Contract Administrator:* Manages legal sponsorship agreements.
*   **Activities:**
    *   Create the sponsorship prospectus (Title, Platinum, Gold, Silver tiers).
    *   Pitch booth space to tech providers, mining consultancies, and governments.
    *   Ensure sponsors receive their contracted benefits (e.g., speaking slots, branded lounges).
    *   Manage the on-site "Partner Success" desk.

### 5. Marketing & Communications Department
Responsible for filling the room, building brand prestige, and managing the global press.

*   **Team Roles:** Marketing Director, PR Manager, Digital Manager.
*   **Team Member Roles:**
    *   *PR Manager:* Manages the international press pool and issues press releases.
    *   *Digital & Social Manager:* Runs LinkedIn, Twitter, and the event website.
    *   *Creative Director:* Manages the branding, signage, and stage design.
*   **Activities:**
    *   Build and manage the event website and mobile app.
    *   Execute multi-channel marketing campaigns (email, LinkedIn, industry publications).
    *   Host a Press Center on-site with media walls, interview studios, and press kits.
    *   Manage social media live updates during the event.

### 6. Event Operations & Production Department
The engine that builds the physical event.

*   **Team Roles:** Operations Director, Production Manager, Venue Liaison.
*   **Team Member Roles:**
    *   *Stage/Production Manager:* Runs lighting, audio, LED screens, and teleprompters.
    *   *Logistics Manager:* Manages freight, shipping, and material handling.
    *   *Registration Lead:* Manages the check-in experience and badging.
    *   *F&B (Food & Beverage) Manager:* Plans menus, catering schedules, and dietary needs.
*   **Activities:**
    *   Design the floor plan (Plenary hall, Breakout rooms, Exhibition hall, VIP lounges).
    *   Hire and manage Audio/Visual (AV) contractors.
    *   Oversee venue contracting, security, and crowd flow.
    *   Manage the registration desk, wayfinding signage, and networking zones.

### 7. Technology & Digital Experience Department
Modern mining forums are highly digitized and often hybrid.

*   **Team Roles:** IT Director, Broadcast Producer, App Manager.
*   **Team Member Roles:**
    *   *IT Lead:* Ensures flawless Wi-Fi across the venue.
    *   *App Developer/Manager:* Manages networking features, agendas, and live polling on the event app.
    *   *Virtual Platform Manager:* Runs the live stream for virtual attendees.
*   **Activities:**
    *   Implement smart registration (RFID badges for trackable networking).
    *   Set up high-definition live streaming of all plenary sessions.
    *   Provide robust, secure Wi-Fi for thousands of attendees.
    *   Capture data via the app for post-event analytics (which sessions were most popular).

### 8. Finance & Administration Department
Keeps the event solvent and legally compliant.

*   **Team Roles:** CFO/Finance Director, Procurement Officer, HR Manager.
*   **Team Member Roles:**
    *   *Accountant:* Manages invoices, AP/AR, and cash flow.
    *   *Procurement:* Negotiates contracts with vendors (venues, caterers).
    *   *HR/Staffing Lead:* Hires temporary staff and volunteers.
*   **Activities:**
    *   Create and monitor the master budget.
    *   Process speaker travel reimbursements and vendor payments.
    *   Ensure compliance with local tax and labor laws.
    *   Recruit, train, and uniform the event-day volunteers.

### 9. Sustainability & ESG Department (Crucial for an FMF-style event)
You cannot host a mining forum without practicing what you preach regarding sustainability.

*   **Team Roles:** ESG Director, Sustainability Coordinator.
*   **Team Member Roles:**
    *   *Carbon Offset Analyst:* Measures the carbon footprint of the event.
    *   *Waste Management Lead:* Ensures recycling and zero-waste-to-landfill policies.
*   **Activities:**
    *   Source local, sustainable food options (reduce food miles).
    *   Eliminate single-use plastics on-site.
    *   Produce a post-event ESG report detailing carbon offsets and sustainable practices used during the forum.
    *   Ensure venue accessibility (ADA compliance) and diversity in speaker lineups.

### Summary: How they work together (The Timeline)
1.  **Months 1-6:** Executive & Finance set the budget. Content defines the narrative. Sales starts pitching sponsors.
2.  **Months 6-9:** Marketing launches the website. VIP starts courting delegations. Operations secures the venue.
3.  **Months 9-11:** Sales closes deals. Marketing pushes ticket sales. Tech builds the app.
4.  **Month 12 (Event Month):** Operations takes over the venue. VIP manages protocol. Content runs the stages. Post-event: Finance does the final tally, Tech exports data, and ESG publishes the sustainability report.

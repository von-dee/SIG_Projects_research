To manage an event of the scale and complexity of the Future Minerals Forum (FMF), you are not just building an "event app"; you are building an **Enterprise Event Management Operating System (OS)**. It must handle high-level diplomatic protocol, massive B2B matchmaking, complex multi-track content, and stringent security.

Here is the ultimate, highly detailed feature breakdown for a custom software platform to manage this event from day one of planning to post-event reporting.

---

### 1. Master Dashboard & "War Room" Command Center

*The central nervous system for the Event Director and Operations team.*

* **Real-Time KPI Dashboard:** Live metrics on registration numbers, revenue generated, app downloads, and venue capacity heatmaps.
* **Incident & Crisis Management Module:** A built-in alert system where any staff member can flag an issue (e.g., "Medical Emergency in Hall B" or "VIP delayed"). It routes alerts to the War Room via SMS/App push.
* **Two-Way Radio Integration (VoIP):** Digital walkie-talkie functionality within the app, allowing staff to communicate on dedicated channels (Ops, Security, VIP, AV) via their smartphones, replacing physical radios.
* **Live Run-of-Show (ROS):** A dynamic, minute-by-minute timeline displayed on screens in the War Room. It turns red if a task is behind schedule and auto-notifies the next department in the sequence.
* **Task & Project Management:** Kanban-style boards for departments to assign tasks, set deadlines, and track dependencies (e.g., Content cannot finalize the agenda until Commercial confirms sponsor slots).

### 2. VIP & Protocol Management Module

*Crucial for government events. Standard event software fails at diplomatic protocol.*

* **Protocol Ranking Engine:** A database that assigns hierarchy levels to VIPs (e.g., Head of State = Rank 1, Minister = Rank 2, CEO = Rank 3). The software automatically generates seating charts and motorcade orders based on these ranks.
* **Dynamic Seating Chart Builder:** Drag-and-drop interface for plenary halls and gala dinners. The software flags protocol violations (e.g., accidentally seating a Deputy Minister above a Minister).
* **VIP Itinerary & Motorcade Tracker:** GPS-integrated tracking for VIP shuttles. VIP hosts receive live ETAs of their assigned dignitaries arriving at the venue.
* **Biometric & Security Clearance Integration:** Integration with barcode/RFID scanners and facial recognition cameras at VIP entrances to ensure no unauthorized personnel enter secure zones.
* **Dietary & Personal Preferences Database:** Tracks VIP specifics (e.g., "Minister X requires decaf coffee," "CEO Y requires gluten-free meals, dislikes flashing lights").

### 3. B2B / G2G Matchmaking & Meeting Hub

*The core reason CEOs and Ministers attend: closed-door meetings.*

* **AI-Powered Matchmaking:** Algorithms that analyze attendee profiles, interests, and goals to suggest B2B or Government-to-Government (G2G) meetings.
* **Bilateral Meeting Room Booker:** A visual floor plan of all private meeting rooms. Attendees can request a meeting; the system automatically checks both participants' calendars and books an available room.
* **Calendar Sync & Conflict Resolution:** Syncs with Outlook/Gmail. Prevents double-booking. If a VIP's plenary session runs late, the system automatically pings the B2B counterpart to notify them of the delay and offers a reschedule.
* **Translation/Interpreter Assignment:** If a meeting is booked between a French delegation and a Chinese delegation, the system flags the need for interpreters and prompts the Ops team to assign an interpreter booth to that specific room.

### 4. Content & Stage Management System

*For the Content Director and Stage Managers running the plenary and breakouts.*

* **Speaker Portal:** A secure login where speakers upload their headshots, bios, and presentation decks. The system locks uploads 48 hours before the event to prevent version-control issues.
* **Presentation QA & Conversion Tool:** Automatically scans uploaded PowerPoint/Keynote files for formatting issues, fonts, or video codec problems, and converts them to a unified format for the main AV switcher.
* **Digital Teleprompter Integration:** Scriptwriters write the ROS and moderator scripts in the software; it feeds directly into the on-stage teleprompter systems.
* **Green Room Traffic Light System:** A tablet interface for the Green Room manager. Shows a list of upcoming speakers with a countdown timer. Green = On standby. Yellow = Move to stage wing. Red = On stage.
* **Audience Live Polling & Q&A:** Attendees scan a QR code on their armrest to submit questions. Moderators view and approve questions on a tablet before reading them aloud.

### 5. Commercial & Exhibition Management

*For the Sales team to track revenue, sponsors, and exhibition logistics.*

* **Sponsor ROI Tracker:** Tracks contracted deliverables vs. actuals (e.g., "Sponsor X paid for 5 VIP passes, 1 speaking slot, and a 10x10 booth"). It alerts the sales team if a deliverable is unfulfilled.
* **Exhibitor Portal & Floorplan CAD Integration:** A module where exhibitors log in to submit their booth designs, electrical needs, and load-in schedules. It integrates with the venue’s CAD files to ensure booths don't block fire exits.
* **Lead Retrieval App:** A smartphone app for sponsors/exhibitors to scan attendee RFID badges to capture leads instantly. Leads are pushed to the sponsor's CRM (Salesforce/HubSpot) post-event.
* **Contract & Invoicing Automation:** Generates sponsorship contracts, tracks signatures (DocuSign integration), and triggers automated invoice reminders for unpaid balances.

### 6. Registration, Access Control & Badging

*Must handle 10,000+ people securely and quickly.*

* **Multi-Tier Access Control:** Badging system that prints RFID chips with embedded access levels encoded visually (e.g., Red border = All Access, Blue = Exhibitor Only, Green = General Attendee).
* **Self-Service Kiosk Integration:** Software integrates with physical self-service kiosks for attendees to print badges on-site upon arrival.
* **Visa Support Letter Generator:** Automated generation of PDF invitation letters for international attendees to take to their respective embassies.
* **Real-Time Capacity Management:** Tracks the number of people entering/exiting specific halls via RFID scanners. If a breakout room hits 90% capacity, the app pushes a notification to attendees telling them the room is full.
* **On-Site Badge Printing:** Wireless printing capabilities that pull from the central database, allowing organizers to print replacement badges anywhere in the venue instantly.

### 7. Mobile App (Attendee Facing)

*The digital companion for every delegate.*

* **Offline Mode:** Critical for massive events where venue Wi-Fi might drop. The app caches the agenda, personal schedule, and floor maps locally on the phone.
* **Smart Networking:** Attendees can tap their phones together (NFC) or scan QR codes to exchange digital business cards, instantly populating their CRM.
* **Indoor Navigation (Wayfinding):** Blue-dot tracking on indoor floor maps to help attendees find specific exhibition booths or breakout rooms.
* **Live Translation Integration:** For non-English speakers, the app can stream live audio translations of the plenary sessions directly to their earphones.
* **Push Notification Engine:** Geo-fenced alerts (e.g., "The Minister of Mining is starting in Hall A in 5 minutes" or "Coffee break is now served in the East Atrium").

### 8. Operations, Logistics & Vendor Management

*For the Ops team to manage the physical build and venue.*

* **Venue CAD/Floorplan Builder:** Drag-and-drop tools to design stage layouts, seating arrangements, and exhibition halls.
* **F&B (Food & Beverage) Management:** Tracks meal counts in real-time, cross-referencing dietary restrictions. Sends live updates to the catering company (e.g., "300 people in Hall B, push hot food now").
* **Staff & Volunteer Scheduler:** A shift-management tool that assigns staff to specific zones and times. It sends shift reminders and GPS check-ins.
* **Asset & Inventory Tracker:** Tracks all rented equipment (radios, tablets, AV gear) using QR codes to prevent loss.

### 9. Finance & Administration Module

*For the CFO and Finance team.*

* **Master Budget Builder:** Tracks projected vs. actual spend across all departments in real-time.
* **Multi-Currency & Tax Compliance:** Handles global sponsor payments, calculating local VAT/Taxes automatically.
* **Expense Management:** Ops staff can upload receipts via the mobile app, which are routed to department heads for approval and pushed to accounting software (e.g., QuickBooks, SAP).
* **Procurement & PO System:** Generates Purchase Orders for vendors (AV, Security, Catering) and tracks payment milestones.

### 10. Marketing, PR & Media Center

*For the Marketing team and global press.*

* **Press Accreditation Portal:** A separate registration flow for journalists requiring them to upload media credentials and outlet details for approval.
* **Digital Asset Management (DAM):** A repository for high-res logos, speaker photos, and press releases that credentialed media can download.
* **Live Broadcast Integration:** Controls the live streaming feeds to the virtual platform and social media (YouTube/LinkedIn).
* **Email & SMS Marketing Engine:** Segments the database (e.g., "Send VIP Gala invite only to Rank 1 & 2 attendees" or "Send visa reminder to international delegates").

### 11. ESG & Sustainability Tracker

*Mandatory for an event discussing future minerals.*

* **Carbon Footprint Calculator:** Automatically calculates estimated carbon emissions based on attendee flight distances (calculated from registration data), venue energy usage, and F&B waste.
* **Waste Diversion Tracker:** A module for the venue/catering team to input daily waste metrics (compost, recycle, landfill) to generate a post-event zero-waste report.
* **Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (DEI) Dashboard:** Tracks speaker demographics (gender, nationality) to ensure the event meets diversity targets.

### 12. Post-Event Analytics & Data Engine

*The intelligence layer for the debrief and next year's planning.*

* **NPS (Net Promoter Score) Automation:** Automatically sends customized post-event surveys to attendees, sponsors, and speakers 24 hours after the event.
* **Footfall & Heatmap Analytics:** Aggregates RFID data to show which exhibition booths or breakout sessions were most popular, visualized as a heat map.
* **Economic Impact Report Generator:** Compiles data on B2B meetings held, MOUs signed (tracked via a specific app feature), and overall attendee spend to create a report for government stakeholders.
* **App Engagement Metrics:** Tracks which sessions had the most live-polling activity, most-clicked sponsors, and most-exchanged business cards.

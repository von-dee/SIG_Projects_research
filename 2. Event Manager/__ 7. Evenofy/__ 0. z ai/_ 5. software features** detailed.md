
To build an Enterprise Event Management Operating System (OS) capable of handling an event of the magnitude of the Future Minerals Forum (FMF), you are essentially building a hybrid of a CRM, an ERP, a project management tool, and a mobile networking app. 

Below is an exhaustive, highly technical Product Requirements Document (PRD) broken down by module. Each module details the specific features, data fields, logic, and integrations required.

---

### Module 1: Master Dashboard & "War Room" Command Center
*This is the central nervous system used by the Event Director, Ops Lead, and Security Lead.*

**1.1 Real-Time Data Visualization**
*   **Live Registration Pulse:** Chart showing registrations per hour, mapped against historical data and projected targets.
*   **Revenue Ticker:** Live cumulative revenue from ticket sales, sponsorships, and exhibition booths, filtered by currency.
*   **Venue Capacity Heatmap:** A visual 2D/3D map of the venue colored green (under 50%), yellow (50-80%), red (over 90% capacity) based on live RFID turnstile data.
*   **Wi-Fi & Network Health Monitor:** API integration with the venue’s IT infrastructure to show bandwidth usage and access point status.

**1.2 Incident & Crisis Management (War Room)**
*   **Tiered Alert System:** Staff can log incidents via the mobile app.
    *   *Level 1 (Minor):* Broken chair, spill (Routes to Ops team).
    *   *Level 2 (Major):* Medical emergency, VIP delay (Routes to Ops + Medical + VIP team).
    *   *Level 3 (Critical):* Fire, security breach (Routes to War Room + Local Authorities).
*   **Incident Timeline & Audit Log:** Time-stamped log of when the incident was reported, who acknowledged it, action taken, and resolution time.

**1.3 Live Run of Show (ROS) Engine**
*   **Dynamic Gantt View:** A minute-by-minute timeline of the main stage and all breakouts.
*   **Dependency Tracking:** If "Speaker A" has not arrived in the Green Room 15 mins prior, the system flags the next item in the ROS as "At Risk."
*   **Auto-Push Notifications:** Triggers SMS/App alerts to the Stage Manager and Moderator if the schedule is falling behind.

### Module 2: VIP & Diplomatic Protocol Management
*Standard event software fails at diplomatic protocol. This module ensures no diplomatic incidents occur.*

**2.1 Protocol Ranking Engine**
*   **Hierarchy Database:** Custom fields for "Protocol Rank" (e.g., 1 = Head of State, 2 = Minister, 3 = Deputy Minister, 4 = CEO).
*   **Flagging Logic:** The system automatically blocks seating arrangements that violate protocol (e.g., seating a Rank 3 in the front row while a Rank 1 is seated in the second row).
*   **Geo-Political Conflict Matrix:** A database that flags if dignitaries from currently hostile nations are placed in adjacent VIP holding rooms or seated next to each other.

**2.2 Dynamic Seating & Table Builder**
*   **Drag-and-Drop 3D Seating:** Visual designer for plenary halls and gala dinners.
*   **Table Shape Logic:** Round (10 pax), U-Shape, Boardroom, Theater.
*   **Name Card Export:** Automatically generates printable PDFs for table name cards with titles, flags, and phonetic pronunciations of names.

**2.3 VIP Transportation & Security**
*   **Motorcade Management:** API integration with flight trackers (FlightAware) to monitor VIP private jet arrivals.
*   **Chauffeur App:** A dedicated app for drivers showing passenger details, ETA, gate arrival, and a direct SOS button to venue security.
*   **Biometric Access Integration:** Integration with physical access control systems (HID Global) to allow facial recognition or fingerprint entry into the "Head of State" secure perimeter.

**2.4 VIP Liaison "Shadow" App**
*   A simplified mobile interface for the protocol officer assigned to a specific VIP. It shows:
    *   The VIP's live, personalized minute-by-minute itinerary.
    *   Dietary preferences and allergies.
    *   "Bathroom break" buffers.
    *   Direct chat link to the VIP’s personal security detail.

### Module 3: B2B / G2G Matchmaking & Meeting Hub
*The engine that facilitates closed-door, high-value meetings between governments and corporations.*

**3.1 AI-Powered Matchmaking Algorithm**
*   **Intent Profiling:** During registration, attendees select what they are looking for (e.g., "Downstream Investment," "Lithium Extraction Tech," "Government Permits").
*   **Weighted Scoring:** The algorithm scores compatibility between two attendees on a scale of 1-100 based on company size, intent, and region.
*   **Smart Recommendations:** "You should meet with..." suggestions pushed to attendees 2 weeks prior to the event.

**3.2 Bilateral Meeting Room Booker**
*   **Visual Room Inventory:** A grid showing all private meeting rooms (e.g., 20 rooms x 8 time slots = 160 available slots).
*   **Automated Scheduling:** Attendee A requests a meeting with Attendee B. The system cross-references both calendars (synced with the event agenda) and proposes 3 available time slots and an available room.
*   **Status Tracking:** Requested -> Pending -> Accepted -> Room Confirmed -> Checked-In.
*   **Interpreter Assignment Logic:** If Attendee A speaks French and Attendee B speaks Mandarin, the system flags the meeting as "Requires FR-CN Interpreter" and alerts the Ops team to book an interpreter booth for that room.

**3.3 Instant Meeting Drop Zones**
*   **Flash Meeting Feature:** Attendees can tap their NFC badges together on a physical "Drop Zone" kiosk. The system instantly creates a contact exchange and reserves a 15-minute standing table in the networking lounge.

### Module 4: Content & Stage Management System
*For the Content Director, Stage Managers, and AV crew.*

**4.1 Speaker Management Portal**
*   **Custom Onboarding Flow:** Secure link for speakers to upload Bio, Headshot (high-res), Title, and Abstract.
*   **Presentation QA Pipeline:** 
    *   Speaker uploads PPTX.
    *   System auto-checks for aspect ratio (16:9), embedded fonts, and video codec compatibility.
    *   Converts to a unified PDF/PPTX format.
    *   Locks uploads 72 hours before event (Version Control: "Speaker_V3_FINAL.pdf").
*   **Travel & Rider Forms:** Collects flight details, hotel preferences, and specific AV requirements (e.g., "Needs clicker with timer," "Requires confidence monitor").

**4.2 Stage Management Console (Backstage)**
*   **Traffic Light Count-down:** A tablet interface shown to the speaker on stage. Green = 5 mins left. Yellow = 1 min left. Red = Time is up.
*   **Teleprompter Script Sync:** The scriptwriter’s edits in the cloud instantly sync to the on-stage teleprompter software.
*   **Cue Sheet Automation:** Generates a printable/digital cue sheet for the AV director: "At 10:04 AM, dim lights to 30%, roll VTX Video 2, mic up Speaker B."

**4.3 Q&A & Moderation Tool**
*   **Audience Web App:** Attendees scan a QR code on their seat to submit questions anonymously or with their name.
*   **Moderator Dashboard:** The moderator views a live feed of questions, can upvote the best ones, and can display the question on the main LED screen if needed.
*   **Profanity & Spam Filter:** AI automatically hides inappropriate questions.

### Module 5: Commercial & Exhibition Management
*For the Sales team to track revenue, sponsor deliverables, and exhibition logistics.*

**5.1 Sponsor ROI & Deliverables Tracker**
*   **Contractual Obligation Matrix:** If Sponsor X pays for "Platinum," the system lists all deliverables (e.g., 10 VIP passes, 1 speaking slot, logo on main screen).
*   **Fulfillment Status:** Tracks what has been delivered. (e.g., Passes = Delivered, Speaking slot = Scheduled, Logo = Artwork received and approved).
*   **Post-Event Lead Report:** Automatically generates a CSV of all attendees who scanned their badge at the sponsor’s booth, sent to the sponsor 24 hours post-event.

**5.2 Exhibition Floorplan & CAD Integration**
*   **Drag-and-Drop Floorplan:** Visual builder to allocate booths.
*   **Booth Utility Map:** Overlay showing where electrical drops, water hookups, and compressed air lines are located. Exhibitors select their needs in their portal; the system alerts the venue contractor.
*   **Fire & Safety Compliance:** The software automatically flags if a booth placement blocks a fire exit or exceeds the venue’s maximum weight load per square meter.

**5.3 Exhibitor Portal**
*   **Logistics Management:** Exhibitors input freight arrival times, crate dimensions, and forklift requirements to create a load-in schedule that prevents 10 trucks from arriving at the loading dock simultaneously.
*   **Lead Retrieval License Purchasing:** Exhibitors can buy additional "Lead Scanning" licenses for their staff directly within the portal.

### Module 6: Registration, Access Control & Badging
*Must securely and rapidly process 10,000+ attendees.*

**6.1 Dynamic Registration Paths**
*   **Custom Flows by Persona:** 
    *   *Government:* Requires validation of government email and diplomatic passport number.
    *   *Investor:* Requires portfolio size/proof of funds to unlock VIP investor areas.
    *   *Student:* Requires university ID upload.
*   **Dynamic Pricing Engine:** Automatically adjusts ticket prices based on date, remaining inventory, and persona type.

**6.2 Badging & RFID Encoding**
*   **Badge Design Studio:** Drag-and-drop tool to design badges. Elements (VIP, Speaker, Press) are auto-placed based on registration data.
*   **Access Level Encoding:** The software writes specific access permissions to the RFID chip. (e.g., Access Level 4 = Main Hall + VIP Lounge; Access Level 2 = Main Hall + Exhibition).
*   **Print Station Integration:** API to thermal printers (Zebra, Epson). Supports on-demand printing and pre-printing. 
*   **Reprint Logic:** If an attendee loses a badge, the system voids the old RFID chip and prints a new one with an incremented version number (e.g., "John Doe v2").

**6.3 On-Site Access Control**
*   **Turnstile/Gate Software:** Integration with physical RFID scanners on turnstiles or handheld wands.
*   **Anti-Passback Logic:** Prevents an attendee from passing their badge to a friend to enter the secure VIP zone (once scanned in, it cannot be scanned in again until scanned out).
*   **Live Capacity Triggers:** If a breakout room hits its safe legal capacity, the door scanner beeps red and locks, routing overflow attendees to an overflow room with a live video feed.

### Module 7: Mobile App (Attendee & Staff Facing)
*The digital companion. Requires offline capabilities due to venue Wi-Fi constraints.*

**7.1 Attendee App Features**
*   **Intelligent Agenda:** Attendees can build their schedule. The app suggests sessions based on their "Intent Profile" (from Module 3).
*   **Offline Caching:** SQLite local database caching. If Wi-Fi drops, the agenda, personal calendar, and indoor maps remain fully functional. Data syncs to the cloud when the connection is restored.
*   **Indoor Wayfinding (Blue Dot):** Integration with indoor mapping SDKs (like Mapwize or Situm) using Bluetooth beacons placed around the venue to show a "Google Maps" style blue dot tracking the user indoors.
*   **Smart Networking (NFC/Bluetooth):** If two attendees opt-in, their phones can detect proximity and prompt: "You are near John Doe, CEO of Mining Co. Exchange contact?"
*   **Live Translation (Audio):** Integration with a live translation service. A French attendee plugs in their earphones, selects "French," and listens to a real-time AI translation of the English speaker on stage.
*   **Interactive Map with Sponsor Layers:** Users can toggle layers on the map (e.g., "Show me all Coffee Stations," "Show me Restrooms," "Show me Booths related to Lithium").

**7.2 Staff & Volunteer App Features**
*   **Zone Assignments:** Volunteers log in and only see information relevant to their assigned zone (e.g., Stage 2).
*   **QR Code Scanner Integration:** The phone’s camera acts as a badge scanner for crowd control or lead retrieval.
*   **Task Triage:** A Kanban board showing tasks assigned to that staff member. They can swipe tasks to "Done."
*   **Radio/VoIP Integration:** Push-to-talk functionality over Wi-Fi/Cellular, bypassing the need for physical walkie-talkies.

### Module 8: Operations, Logistics & F&B Management
*For the Ops team to manage the physical build and catering.*

**8.1 F&B (Food & Beverage) Management**
*   **Real-Time Consumption Tracker:** Caterers input how many meals have been served vs. how many are expected. 
*   **Dietary Restriction Matrix:** Cross-references the registration database. A dashboard shows: "Hall A needs 300 standard meals, 50 Vegan, 12 Halal, 3 Celiac."
*   **Automated Push Timing:** The system alerts the F&B manager 15 minutes before a scheduled coffee break to begin brewing/plating.

**8.2 Staff & Volunteer Scheduler**
*   **Shift Generator:** Drag-and-drop tool to build shifts. The system flags if a shift has too few people or lacks a "Zone Manager."
*   **Geofence Clock-In:** Staff can only clock in for their shift if their phone GPS is inside the venue’s geofence.
*   **Break Compliance:** Tracks hours worked and alerts the manager when a staff member legally requires a break.

**8.3 Asset & Inventory Tracking**
*   **QR Asset Tags:** Every piece of rented equipment (radio, tablet, mic, printer) gets a QR sticker. 
*   **Check-out/Check-in System:** When an item is handed to a staff member, it is scanned to their profile. If it’s not returned at the end of the event, the system charges their department.

### Module 9: Finance & Administration Module
*For the CFO and Procurement.*

**9.1 Master Budget & P&L Engine**
*   **Zero-Based Budgeting Templates:** Pre-set templates for event expenses (AV, Venue, Travel, Marketing).
*   **Variance Reporting:** Live dashboard showing Budgeted vs. Actual vs. Forecasted spend. If AV costs overrun, it visually flags the variance in red.
*   **Multi-Currency & FX Hedging:** Handles global payments, integrating with live Forex APIs to calculate exact costs in the base currency.

**9.2 Procurement & PO (Purchase Order) System**
*   **Vendor Portal:** Vendors log in to submit quotes.
*   **3-Way Match:** The system ensures the Purchase Order, the Receiving Report (goods delivered), and the Vendor Invoice all match before Finance is allowed to release payment.

**9.3 Travel & Expense (T&E) Management**
*   **Speaker/Staff Reimbursement Portal:** Upload receipts via mobile app. AI reads the receipt (Merchant, Date, Amount, Tax).
*   **Policy Enforcement:** If a staff member tries to expense a $200 dinner and the policy limit is $75, the system auto-rejects it and routes it to their manager for override.

### Module 10: Marketing, PR & Media Center
*For the Marketing team, PR agency, and global press.*

**10.1 Press Accreditation & Media Center**
*   **Journalist Vetting Flow:** Reporters upload press credentials and outlet details. PR team approves/rejects.
*   **Digital Asset Management (DAM):** A secure library for high-res logos, stock B-roll footage, official press releases, and speaker headshots. Includes dynamic watermarks to track leaks.
*   **Press Conference Booker:** A module where journalists can book 1-on-1 interview slots with CEOs/Ministers in dedicated media interview suites.

**10.2 Multi-Channel Marketing Automation**
*   **Dynamic Segmentation:** Ability to slice the database infinitely (e.g., "Send email to: CEOs, from Africa, who haven't registered yet").
*   **A/B Testing Engine:** Tests subject lines, send times, and email designs to optimize open rates.
*   **Attribution Modeling:** Tracks which marketing channel (LinkedIn Ad, Email, Referral) resulted in a registration to calculate ROI on marketing spend.

**10.3 Live Broadcast & Virtual Platform**
*   **Hybrid Stream Management:** Controls the RTMP feeds pushing to the event website and YouTube/LinkedIn Live.
*   **Virtual Attendee Portal:** For those not attending in person, a web portal to watch live streams, ask questions via chat, and visit virtual exhibition booths.

### Module 11: ESG & Sustainability Tracker
*Mandatory for an event discussing future minerals and energy transition.*

**11.1 Carbon Footprint Calculator**
*   **Flight Emissions API:** Cross-references attendee home airports with the event location to calculate estimated Scope 3 (travel) carbon emissions.
*   **Venue Energy Tracker:** Integrates with the venue’s smart meter to track electricity and water usage during the event.
*   **Offset Procurement:** Automatically calculates the cost to purchase carbon credits to offset the event's footprint and adds this as a line item to the final budget.

**11.2 Zero-Waste & Circular Economy Dashboard**
*   **Waste Hauler Portal:** The venue's waste management company inputs daily weights of compost, recycling, and landfill waste.
*   **Diversion Rate Calculator:** Shows the percentage of waste diverted from landfills in real-time. Displays this metric on digital signage in the venue to showcase the event's commitment to sustainability.

**11.3 DEI (Diversity, Equity, & Inclusion) Analytics**
*   **Speaker/Panel Demographics Tracker:** Tracks gender, nationality, and age of speakers to ensure the event meets stated diversity targets (e.g., "40% female speakers").
*   **Accessibility Compliance:** Tracks provisions made for disabled attendees (wheelchair access, sign language interpreters, closed captioning).

---

### Module 12: Post-Event Analytics & Data Engine
*The intelligence layer for the debrief and next year's planning.*

**12.1 Attendee Behavior & Heatmaps**
*   **Dwell Time Analytics:** RFID data shows how long attendees stayed in specific sessions. (e.g., "Session A had 500 people enter, but 200 left after 15 minutes").
*   **Exhibition Booth Traffic Leaderboard:** Ranks booths by total scans/visits, proving ROI to sponsors.
*   **Network Graph Visualization:** A visual map showing nodes of who met whom, helping identify the most "connected" attendees (influencers) at the event.

**12.2 Automated NPS & Survey Engine**
*   **Triggered Surveys:** Sends customized surveys 24 hours post-event. Different surveys for VIPs, Sponsors, General Attendees, and Speakers.
*   **Sentiment Analysis (NLP):** AI analyzes open-text feedback and categorizes it as Positive, Neutral, or Negative, highlighting key themes (e.g., "Wi-Fi was terrible," "Food was great").

**12.3 Economic Impact & MOU Tracker**
*   **Deal Registration:** A feature where delegations can formally log MOUs (Memorandums of Understanding) or investment deals signed during the event.
*   **Economic Impact Report Generator:** Compiles deal values, total attendee spend (hotels, flights), and sponsor revenue to generate a report for the host government proving the economic value of the forum.

**12.4 Data Purge & Privacy (GDPR/CCPA Compliance)**
*   **Automated Data Retention Policies:** Allows the system to automatically delete or anonymize attendee data (e.g., passport numbers, dietary health data) after a set period (e.g., 90 days) to comply with global privacy laws, unless legally required to retain it.
*   **Right to be Forgotten:** One-click deletion of an attendee's entire history and data footprint within the system.

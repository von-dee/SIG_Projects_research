Executing an event of the magnitude of the Future Minerals Forum (FMF) using your custom Enterprise Event Management Operating System (OS) completely transforms the workflow. The software acts as the central nervous system, replacing scattered Excel sheets, WhatsApp groups, and manual checks with a single source of truth.

Here is the highly detailed, phase-by-phase execution playbook detailing **how to use the system** to pull off the event successfully.

---

### Phase 1: Foundation & Strategy (Months 12 to 9 Before Event)
*The goal is configuring the system architecture, setting the budget, and mapping out the venue and protocol rules before any external-facing work begins.*

**System Activities:**
*   **Master Dashboard Setup:** The Event Director inputs the master budget into the **Finance Module**. The system sets up KPI tracking for revenue targets and registration goals.
*   **Venue CAD & Floorplan Builder:** The Ops team uploads the venue’s architectural CAD files into the **Operations Module**. They digitally map out the plenary hall, exhibition space, and VIP bilateral meeting rooms. The system automatically calculates square meters and flags fire-exit blockages.
*   **Protocol Engine Configuration:** The VIP team inputs the host country’s diplomatic ranking rules into the **Protocol Module**. The system’s logic is set to prevent seating violations (e.g., the system is programmed to reject any seating chart where a Deputy Minister is placed ahead of a Minister).
*   **Access Level Architecture:** The Tech team defines the RFID access matrix in the **Registration Module** (e.g., Level 1 = Head of State Secure Zone; Level 4 = General Exhibition Hall). 

**Key Things to Note:**
*   *Data Architecture is Everything:* If you configure the access levels, protocol rankings, or budget categories incorrectly in the system during Month 9, it will cascade into massive operational failures during the event. Take time to test the system's logic rules before opening registration.

---

### Phase 2: Development & Sales (Months 8 to 5 Before Event)
*The goal is securing revenue and high-profile attendees. The system shifts from configuration to active processing.*

**System Activities:**
*   **Commercial Launch:** The Sales team launches the **Exhibitor Portal**. Sponsors log in, select their booths via the visual floorplan, and pay through the **Procurement & PO System**. The system automatically tracks their contracted deliverables (e.g., "Sponsor X owes 1 speaking slot") in the **Sponsor ROI Tracker**.
*   **Registration Paths Go Live:** The Marketing team activates the **Dynamic Registration Paths**. A Government official registers, and the system prompts them for a diplomatic passport number; an Investor registers, and the system prompts for proof of funds.
*   **Speaker Portal Activation:** The Content team sends secure links to speakers. Speakers log in to the **Speaker Management Portal** to upload bios, headshots, and sign AV rider forms.
*   **AI Matchmaking Pre-Setup:** The Tech team configures the **Matchmaking Algorithm** intent fields. When attendees register, they select their goals (e.g., "Downstream Investment," "Lithium Tech"), and the system begins scoring compatibility between attendees in the background.

**Key Things to Note:**
*   *Cross-Module Dependency:* The system must talk to each other. If the Commercial team sells a "Platinum Sponsorship," the system must automatically trigger the Content team to grant that sponsor a speaking slot in the Content Module, and the Registration team to issue 10 VIP access badges.

---

### Phase 3: Execution & Marketing Push (Months 4 to 2 Before Event)
*The goal is public momentum and locking down complex logistics like seating and F&B.*

**System Activities:**
*   **Dynamic Seating Builder:** The VIP team uses the system’s **3D Seating Drag-and-Drop tool** to map out the gala dinner. The system auto-populates names from the registration database and uses the **Geo-Political Conflict Matrix** to flag if delegates from sanctioned or hostile nations are seated next to each other.
*   **F&B Dietary Matrix Aggregation:** As registrations flow in, the **F&B Module** automatically aggregates dietary requirements. The system generates a report showing exactly how many Halal, Kosher, and Vegan meals are required per hall, pushing this data directly to the catering company.
*   **Mobile App Beta & Soft Launch:** The Tech team launches the **Mobile App**. Attendees can log in, build their personalized agenda, and the system syncs with their Outlook/Gmail calendars. 
*   **B2B Meeting Room Booker Opens:** The system unlocks the meeting room inventory. Attendees start sending meeting requests; the system cross-references both calendars, auto-approves meetings, and reserves private rooms.

**Key Things to Note:**
*   *The "Freeze" on Core Architecture:* At Month 2, the system configuration is frozen. No new room types, access levels, or ticket categories can be created. The system shifts from a "setup" tool to a "processing" tool. 

---

### Phase 4: The Final Countdown (Weeks 4 to 1 Before Event)
*The goal is finalizing data, rehearsing, and locking down the Run of Show (ROS). No new data is accepted.*

**System Activities:**
*   **Presentation QA Pipeline Lockdown:** The **Speaker Portal** locks. No more PPT uploads are allowed. The system’s QA tool auto-converts all presentations to 16:9 PDF format, checks for embedded fonts, and pushes them to the main AV switcher. Version control is locked.
*   **ROS Engine Finalization:** The Content team finalizes the minute-by-minute **Run of Show (ROS) Engine**. The system links teleprompter scripts, lighting cues, and video rolls to specific timecodes.
*   **RFID Badge Encoding & Pre-Printing:** The Registration team exports the final attendee database. The system encodes the access levels onto the RFID chips and triggers the thermal printers to pre-print the 10,000+ badges.
*   **Staff & Volunteer Scheduler:** Ops uses the **Shift Generator** to assign volunteers to specific zones. The system pushes schedules to the Staff App and sets up **Geofence Clock-In** parameters (staff can only clock in if their phone GPS is inside the venue).

**Key Things to Note:**
*   *Enforcing the System's Rules:* At Week 1, human ego will try to bypass the system. A CEO will ask their assistant to upload a "quick change" to their presentation. The system must be programmed to reject it, forcing them to use the version submitted 72 hours prior. This prevents the dreaded "Speaker plugs in a USB on stage and crashes the AV" scenario.

---

### Phase 5: Days Before & During the Event (Showtime)
*The goal is real-time monitoring, invisible crisis management, and flawless stage execution.*

**System Activities:**
*   **War Room Command Center Activation:** The Event Director monitors the **Live Dashboard**. They watch the **Venue Capacity Heatmap** turn colors as RFID turnstile data flows in. 
*   **Stage Management Console:** Backstage, the Stage Manager uses a tablet to control the **Traffic Light Countdown** for speakers. The **Auto-Push Notification** engine sends SMS alerts to the Green Room: "Speaker B, you are needed backstage in 5 minutes."
*   **Real-Time Access Control:** If a breakout room hits 90% capacity, the **Turnstile/Gate Software** locks the doors and beeps red. The system instantly pushes a notification to the overflow room to start playing the live video feed.
*   **VIP "Shadow" App Usage:** Protocol officers use the app to track their assigned VIPs. The system alerts them: "Minister X's motorcade is 5 minutes away (via GPS integration)." The system dynamically adjusts the ROS if a VIP is late, prompting the moderator to extend audience Q&A.
*   **Lead Retrieval & Smart Networking:** Exhibitors use the app to scan attendee badges. Attendees tap phones together (NFC) to exchange digital business cards. Data syncs instantly to the cloud (when Wi-Fi allows) or caches offline.

**Key Things to Note:**
*   *Trusting the System over Panic:* When a crisis hits (e.g., a medical emergency), the staff member logs it in the app as a Level 2 Incident. The system routes it ONLY to the Medical and Ops teams. This prevents the "War Room" from being spammed with minor issues, allowing the Director to focus on the big picture.

---

### Phase 6: Post-Event (Days 1 to 30+ After)
*The goal is data extraction, financial reconciliation, and proving ROI to stakeholders using the system’s analytics.*

**System Activities:**
*   **Automated NPS & Survey Engine:** Exactly 24 hours after the event ends, the system automatically emails customized surveys to attendees, sponsors, and speakers. The **Sentiment Analysis (NLP)** tool analyzes open-text feedback and categorizes it by theme (e.g., "Wi-Fi complaints" vs. "Great content").
*   **Sponsor ROI Lead Reports:** The system automatically compiles all the leads scanned by an exhibitor into a CSV file and emails it directly to the sponsor, fulfilling their contracted deliverables.
*   **Footfall & Heatmap Analytics:** The Marketing team exports the **Dwell Time Analytics** from the RFID data. The system generates a visual heatmap showing which booths were most visited and which breakout sessions had the highest drop-off rates.
*   **ESG Reporting Generation:** The **Carbon Footprint Calculator** pulls attendee flight data and venue smart-meter data to auto-generate a sustainability report. The **DEI Dashboard** outputs the exact percentage of female vs. male speakers.
*   **Finance Reconciliation:** The **Master Budget Engine** reconciles all final invoices. The system flags any vendor overcharges against the original Purchase Orders.
*   **Data Purge (GDPR/CCPA Compliance):** On Day 30, the system’s automated privacy protocols trigger. It securely deletes sensitive biometric and passport data from the database, retaining only non-personal data for next year's forecasting.

**Key Things to Note:**
*   *Data-Driven Debrief:* The post-event debrief meeting is no longer based on opinions ("I thought the food was bad"). It is entirely driven by system data: "The F&B module shows we ordered 500 extra lunches on Day 2; next year, we reduce the F&B contract by 10%." 
*   *The "Next Year" Pivot:* Before the data purge, the system automatically exports the list of attendees who gave a 9 or 10 NPS score and pushes them into a new marketing campaign for next year's event with an early-bird discount code.

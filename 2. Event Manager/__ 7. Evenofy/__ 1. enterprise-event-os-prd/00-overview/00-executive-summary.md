## Executive Summary

The System is a configurable event operations platform that creates one trusted operational record for a large international forum. It replaces spreadsheets and isolated vendor portals while retaining best-of-breed systems at the edge, such as access gates, printers, finance ERP, CRM, and venue systems.

### Product outcomes

- Give the Event Director a current, auditable view of people, programme, incidents, commercial commitments, and venue readiness.
- Make access, protocol, financial, and safety decisions deterministically under peak-load conditions.
- Preserve privacy and least privilege while allowing staff to work through loss of connectivity.

### In scope

The twelve requested modules, a shared identity and authorization layer, an integration hub, event configuration, audit history, and data-retention controls. The platform supports multiple editions, but data is isolated by `tenant_id` and `event_id`.

### Success measures

| Measure | Target |
|---|---:|
| Gate decision latency while online | p95 under 500 ms |
| Offline access decisions | under 250 ms locally |
| Critical run-of-show updates reaching subscribed consoles | 30 seconds |
| Badge reprint traceability | 100% recorded |
| Mandatory ESG evidence coverage | 95% before report sign-off |

### Delivery sequence

Release 1 establishes identity, registration, badging, programme, command center, and operational integrations. Release 2 adds protocol, meetings, exhibitor, logistics, finance, and mobile workflows. Release 3 delivers advanced ESG, attribution, predictive analytics, and multi-event benchmarking.

## Persona Summary

- **VIP / Minister:** guarded, time-constrained principal whose confidence depends on flawless protocol and discreet care.
- **Government Delegate:** policy professional seeking credible meetings, national visibility, and clear delegation logistics.
- **Investor / Executive:** outcome-focused decision maker weighing relationships, deal flow, and use of scarce time.
- **Speaker / Panelist:** public-facing expert needing certainty, calm preparation, and a dignified stage experience.
- **Sponsor Representative:** brand steward expected to prove commercial value, hospitality quality, and lead return.
- **Exhibitor / Booth Staff:** practical operator balancing build-up, booth traffic, leads, and constant problem-solving.
- **Journalist / Press:** deadline-driven observer who needs access, evidence, and a fast route to the right voice.
- **General Attendee:** paid delegate seeking relevance, ease, and meaningful access without insider knowledge.
- **Student / Academic:** curious emerging participant seeking learning, belonging, and credible career connections.
- **Volunteer / Front-line Staff:** service-minded helper who needs unambiguous instructions, support, breaks, and recognition.
- **War Room Staff:** accountable operator managing uncertainty, escalation, and the whole event's rhythm.
- **Virtual Attendee:** remote participant fighting distance, time zones, and reduced serendipity.
- **Driver / Chauffeur:** unseen arrival partner needing exact routes, credentials, and a respectful, safe handoff.

## Journey Map Legend

Each phase follows chronological numbered steps. Every step names what is happening, the person's goal or emotional state, the human and channel touchpoints, likely risks, a possible moment of delight, and the accountable handoff. “Day 1” establishes orientation; “busiest day” tests the experience under pressure. Assumptions: a three-day flagship forum, one primary venue campus, international arrivals, and a companion mobile app with staffed help channels.

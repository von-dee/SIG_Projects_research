> Persona: VIP / Head of State or Minister → Phase: Before the Event

## Before the Event

- **Step 1 — Formal invitation.** The principal's office receives a diplomatic invitation and proposed purpose. They seek confidence that attendance advances national interests; the Protocol Officer, secure email, and diplomatic channel handle it. Risk: an informal or late invitation feels disrespectful. Delight: a personally signed welcome. Handoff: Host Protocol.
- **Step 2 — Clearance and itinerary.** The aide confirms delegation, security, dietary, bilateral, flight, and hotel requirements. Their mindset is protective and exacting; touchpoints are the assigned protocol liaison, secure call, and briefing book. Risk: a late security change destabilizes every movement. Handoff: Protocol and Security.
- **Step 3 — 48-hour countdown.** The aide receives the final motorcade, arrivals, seating, bilateral, and emergency-contact pack and verbally confirms it. The principal wants no surprises. Risk: conflicting versions in circulation. Delight: a short personal welcome from the host. Handoff: Liaison.

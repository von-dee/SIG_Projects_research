> Persona: VIP / Head of State or Minister → Phase: During the Event

## During the Event

- **Step 1 — Day 1 arrival.** A secure meet-and-greet moves the principal from aircraft or hotel through a protected route to a private check-in. They expect calm dignity; touchpoints are aide, liaison, driver, security, and discreet signage. Risk: visible waiting or a route breach. Delight: a perfectly timed welcome. Handoff: Protocol.
- **Step 2 — First engagements.** The principal attends opening moments and brief meetings while the aide manages pace and notes. They seek influence, not spectacle. Risk: an overrun crowds out a bilateral. Handoff: Protocol Officer and Stage Manager.
- **Step 3 — Busiest day.** Consecutive bilaterals, a keynote, media moment, and hosted meal demand protected transitions. Touchpoints include holding room, interpreter, security, and host. Risk: conflicting delegations encounter each other. Delight: a quiet, prepared reset space. Handoff: Chief of Protocol.
- **Step 4 — Day close.** Aide receives a concise next-day brief and the principal decompresses privately. Risk: last-minute changes reach the wrong person. Handoff: Liaison.

> Persona: VIP / Head of State or Minister → Phase: After the Event

## After the Event

- **Step 1 — Departure and farewell.** Protocol provides a punctual, private departure and confirms luggage and onward travel. Risk: a delayed vehicle becomes a diplomatic slight. Handoff: Protocol and Driver.
- **Step 2 — First 48 hours.** The office receives agreed meeting notes, approved photographs, and thank-you correspondence through secure channels. Goal: turn appearances into action. Handoff: Host Office.
- **Step 3 — 30/60/90 days.** Relationship managers follow only on agreed commitments and next-year priorities. Risk: indiscriminate marketing undermines trust. Handoff: Government Relations.

> Persona: Government Delegate → Phase: Before the Event

## Before the Event

- **Step 1 — Nomination and invitation.** The delegate learns of the forum through ministry leadership or official outreach and assesses policy value. Touchpoints: ministry coordinator, email, event site. Risk: unclear eligibility delays approval. Handoff: Government Relations.
- **Step 2 — Accreditation and travel.** They submit credentials, select sessions, arrange visa, flight, and hotel, and seek practical reassurance. Touchpoints: registration desk, travel desk, app, call center. Delight: visa guidance tailored to their passport. Handoff: Delegate Services.
- **Step 3 — Final 48 hours.** A concise delegation brief confirms agenda, meeting locations, dress expectations, and arrival gate. Risk: senior delegation changes leave them unbadged. Handoff: Protocol Desk.

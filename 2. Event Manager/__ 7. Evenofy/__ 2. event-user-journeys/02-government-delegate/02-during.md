> Persona: Government Delegate → Phase: During the Event

## During the Event

- **Step 1 — Day 1 orientation.** They collect credentials, find their delegation lounge, and use the agenda to prioritize. Feeling: keen but wary of missing the right people. Touchpoints: welcome desk, volunteer, app. Handoff: Registration.
- **Step 2 — Policy and networking.** They move between ministerial sessions, country meetings, and exhibition visits. Risk: a late room move causes a missed bilateral. Delight: a host introduces a relevant investor. Handoff: Delegate Liaison.
- **Step 3 — Busiest day and close.** Rapid meetings, hosted lunch, and a reception require quick notes and route help; each night they receive tomorrow's changes. Handoff: Government Relations.

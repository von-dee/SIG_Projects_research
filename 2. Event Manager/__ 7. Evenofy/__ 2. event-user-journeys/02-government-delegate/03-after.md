> Persona: Government Delegate → Phase: After the Event

## After the Event

- **Step 1 — Departure.** Transport and airport guidance close the visit without uncertainty. Handoff: Travel Desk.
- **Step 2 — 48-hour pack.** They receive approved meeting summaries, policy materials, and photos. Risk: sensitive notes sent too broadly. Handoff: Government Relations.
- **Step 3 — 30/60/90 days.** Follow-up tracks introductions and investment commitments, then invites informed participation next year. Handoff: Relationship Manager.

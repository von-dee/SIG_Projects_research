> Persona: Investor / Corporate Executive → Phase: Before the Event

## Before the Event

1. **Invitation and thesis.** An investor hears through a host, portfolio company, or campaign and asks whether the forum will create real access. Touchpoints: personal invite, event site, assistant. Risk: generic messaging suggests wasted time. Handoff: Investor Relations.
2. **Qualification and diary.** Their assistant registers, chooses meetings, and locks travel around priority targets. They need a defensible schedule. Touchpoints: concierge, calendar, meeting host. Delight: thoughtful introductions before arrival. Handoff: Matchmaking Team.
3. **48-hour briefing.** A clean deal-room map, meeting slate, and host contacts arrive. Risk: a target cancels without an alternative. Handoff: Executive Concierge.

> Persona: Investor / Corporate Executive → Phase: During the Event

## During the Event

1. **Day 1 signal scan.** They check in quickly, attend headline sessions, and judge the room's quality. Touchpoints: fast-track desk, app, host. Risk: queues signal poor execution. Handoff: Registration.
2. **Busiest day deal flow.** Back-to-back private meetings, exhibition walk-throughs, and hosted meals test their attention. They want substance and protected time. Risk: noisy meetings or absent decision-makers. Delight: a host makes a timely, credible introduction. Handoff: Matchmaking Concierge.
3. **Evening reset.** They review notes with their team and receive tomorrow's priority changes. Handoff: Account Host.

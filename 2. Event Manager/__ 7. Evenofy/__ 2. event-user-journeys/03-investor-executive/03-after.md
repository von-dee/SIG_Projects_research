> Persona: Investor / Corporate Executive → Phase: After the Event

## After the Event

1. **Departure.** Concierge confirms transport and any saved contacts. Handoff: Hospitality.
2. **48-hour value recap.** Curated introductions, meeting notes, and approved market material arrive, not a marketing blast. Handoff: Investor Relations.
3. **30/60/90 days.** Relationship team checks deal progress, supplies requested data, and invites them into next year's advisory conversation. Handoff: Account Lead.

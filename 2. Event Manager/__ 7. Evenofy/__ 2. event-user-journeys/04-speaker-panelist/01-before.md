> Persona: Speaker / Panelist → Phase: Before the Event

## Before the Event

1. **Invitation and commitment.** A producer explains the session's stakes, audience, and role. The speaker weighs reputation and time. Touchpoints: producer call, formal invite, speaker portal. Handoff: Content Team.
2. **Preparation.** They submit bio, headshot, travel details, release permissions, and talking points while coordinating with moderator. Risk: unclear topic boundaries create stage anxiety. Delight: a useful audience brief. Handoff: Speaker Liaison.
3. **48-hour countdown.** Final stage time, green-room route, moderator contact, and slide lock arrive by app and WhatsApp. Risk: a traveler misses the briefing. Handoff: Content Coordinator.

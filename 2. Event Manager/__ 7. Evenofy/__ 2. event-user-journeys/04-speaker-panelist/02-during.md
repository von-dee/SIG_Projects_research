> Persona: Speaker / Panelist → Phase: During the Event

## During the Event

1. **Day 1 arrival.** They pick up credentials and test routes, then meet the liaison. Feeling: excited, slightly exposed. Handoff: Speaker Desk.
2. **Busiest day stage moment.** They rehearse, wait in a calm green room, take the stage, and navigate media or audience requests afterward. Risk: a late preceding session cuts preparation. Delight: moderator video greeting and a perfectly prepared room. Handoff: Stage Manager.
3. **Day close.** They receive photos, session feedback, and next-day obligations without pressure. Handoff: Speaker Liaison.

> Persona: Speaker / Panelist → Phase: After the Event

## After the Event

1. **Departure.** Travel support resolves final logistics. Handoff: Speaker Desk.
2. **48-hour deliverables.** They receive approved recording, photographs, quotes, and thank-you note. Risk: rights confusion delays sharing. Handoff: Content Team.
3. **30/60/90 days.** They are asked for feedback and, where appropriate, invited to shape next year's programme. Handoff: Programme Director.

> Persona: Sponsor Representative → Phase: Before the Event

## Before the Event

1. **Contract-to-plan.** Sponsor lead translates promised benefits into objectives, hospitality, speakers, and branded assets. Touchpoints: account manager, activation guide, calls. Risk: unclear ownership means a benefit is missed. Handoff: Sponsor Success.
2. **Readiness.** Team registers guests, approves creative, books travel, and rehearses lead capture. Delight: a single practical checklist. Handoff: Brand and Exhibitor Teams.
3. **48-hour countdown.** They receive booth readiness, hosted-guest list, and escalation contacts. Handoff: Sponsor Manager.

> Persona: Sponsor Representative → Phase: During the Event

## During the Event

1. **Day 1 activation.** They inspect branding, welcome guests, and validate promised visibility. Risk: an uncorrected asset error damages trust. Handoff: Sponsorship Operations.
2. **Busiest day.** Booth traffic, hosted meetings, a speaking slot, and VIP hospitality overlap. They need a calm coordinator and fast fixes. Delight: relevant guests arrive prepared. Handoff: Account Manager.
3. **Close.** They reconcile leads and brief their leadership on visible outcomes. Handoff: Sponsor Success.

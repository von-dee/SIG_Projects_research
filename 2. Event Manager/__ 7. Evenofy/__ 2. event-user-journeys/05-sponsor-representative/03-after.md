> Persona: Sponsor Representative → Phase: After the Event

## After the Event

1. **Departure.** Host thanks them and confirms asset collection. Handoff: Hospitality.
2. **48-hour report.** They receive lead handoff, photo proof, and immediate activation highlights. Handoff: Sponsor Success.
3. **30/60/90 days.** A value review connects outcomes to objectives and begins renewal conversation. Risk: inflated metrics erode confidence. Handoff: Commercial Lead.

> Persona: Exhibitor / Booth Staff → Phase: Before the Event

## Before the Event

1. **Space confirmation.** The exhibitor receives booth plan, rules, and deadlines, feeling accountable for a visible investment. Touchpoints: exhibitor portal, floor plan, account manager. Handoff: Exhibition Team.
2. **Build and staffing.** They order utilities, register contractors, ship materials, train staff, and invite customers. Risk: missed compliance documents stop build-up. Handoff: Floor Operations.
3. **48-hour countdown.** Load-in slot, passes, build checklist, and emergency contacts are confirmed. Handoff: Exhibitor Services.

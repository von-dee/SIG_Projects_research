> Persona: Exhibitor / Booth Staff → Phase: During the Event

## During the Event

1. **Day 1 opening.** Staff enter early, test equipment, and welcome first delegates. Risk: a missing pass or power fault destroys opening momentum. Handoff: Floor Manager.
2. **Busiest day.** They qualify leads, host demos, replenish materials, and manage senior visitors. Delight: a volunteer delivers a relevant meeting lead. Handoff: Exhibitor Services.
3. **Day close.** They secure the booth, back up leads, and plan next-day staffing. Handoff: Security and Floor Team.

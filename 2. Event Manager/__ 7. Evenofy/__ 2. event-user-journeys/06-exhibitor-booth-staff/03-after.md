> Persona: Exhibitor / Booth Staff → Phase: After the Event

## After the Event

1. **Dismantle and departure.** Staff follow timed move-out and return assets safely. Handoff: Logistics.
2. **48-hour follow-up.** Lead export and exhibitor survey arrive with clear consent rules. Handoff: Exhibitor Success.
3. **30/60/90 days.** Commercial team reviews outcomes and renewal options. Handoff: Account Manager.

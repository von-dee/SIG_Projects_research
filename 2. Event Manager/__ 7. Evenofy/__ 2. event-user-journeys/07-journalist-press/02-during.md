> Persona: Journalist / Press → Phase: During the Event

## During the Event

1. **Day 1 access.** They collect press credential, locate workroom, and test connectivity. Risk: a slow queue loses the first story. Handoff: Media Desk.
2. **Busiest day.** Keynotes, interviews, filing, and sudden news compete for minutes. They seek fast facts and honest access; touchpoints include PR liaison, mixed zone, photographer, press room. Handoff: PR Lead.
3. **Close.** They receive next-day embargoes and follow-up availability. Handoff: Media Centre.

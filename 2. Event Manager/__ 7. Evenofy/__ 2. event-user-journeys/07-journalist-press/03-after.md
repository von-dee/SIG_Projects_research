> Persona: Journalist / Press → Phase: After the Event

1. **Departure.** Media desk confirms travel support and final interview requests. Handoff: PR.
2. **48-hour pack.** Approved images, transcripts, figures, and spokesperson follow-ups arrive. Risk: unverified claims harm credibility. Handoff: Press Office.
3. **30/60/90 days.** PR shares meaningful milestones, not promotional noise. Handoff: Media Relations.

> Persona: General Attendee → Phase: Before the Event

1. **Discovery and decision.** They see a campaign, colleague referral, or speaker announcement and ask whether the cost and travel are worthwhile. Handoff: Marketing.
2. **Registration and planning.** They buy a pass, book travel, choose sessions, and seek simple help. Touchpoints: site, email, app, call center. Risk: hidden costs or unclear visa advice. Handoff: Registration.
3. **48-hour countdown.** A personal agenda, venue map, badge instructions, and arrival tips reduce nerves. Delight: a relevant introduction suggestion. Handoff: Attendee Services.

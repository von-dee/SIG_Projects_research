> Persona: General Attendee → Phase: During the Event

1. **Day 1 orientation.** They collect badge, navigate the venue, and find their first session. Risk: queueing and confusing signage create immediate frustration. Handoff: Welcome Team.
2. **Busiest day.** Sessions, meals, exhibitor discovery, and networking compete for attention. They need live changes and an easy help route; a volunteer can rescue a wrong turn. Handoff: Guest Services.
3. **Close.** They save contacts and receive tomorrow's highlights. Handoff: App and Community Team.

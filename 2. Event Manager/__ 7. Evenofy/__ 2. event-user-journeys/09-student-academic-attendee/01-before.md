> Persona: Student / Academic Attendee → Phase: Before the Event

1. **Opportunity discovery.** They learn through university, faculty, or scholarship notice and hope for learning and career access. Handoff: Academic Partnerships.
2. **Eligibility and support.** They prove status, arrange travel support, select learning sessions, and may feel intimidated. Risk: eligibility uncertainty excludes worthy participants. Handoff: Registration.
3. **48-hour welcome.** A newcomer guide, mentor prompt, and campus arrival route arrive. Delight: a named peer welcome. Handoff: Student Programme.

> Persona: Student / Academic Attendee → Phase: During the Event

1. **Day 1 belonging.** They find registration, academic lounge, and introductory tour. Handoff: Student Ambassadors.
2. **Busiest day.** They attend technical sessions, poster or mentor moments, and a career event while building confidence to network. Risk: VIP-heavy spaces feel inaccessible. Delight: a facilitated introduction. Handoff: Academic Team.
3. **Close.** They reflect with peers and plan their final sessions. Handoff: Student Programme.

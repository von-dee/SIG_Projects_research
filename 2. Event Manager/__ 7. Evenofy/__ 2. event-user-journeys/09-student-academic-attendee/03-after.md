> Persona: Student / Academic Attendee → Phase: After the Event

1. **Departure.** Group travel and safeguarding check-ins reassure families and institutions. Handoff: Student Services.
2. **48-hour learning pack.** Certificates, recordings, and mentor follow-up arrive. Handoff: Academic Partnerships.
3. **30/60/90 days.** Research calls, internships, and next-year scholarship reminders sustain the pathway. Handoff: Community Team.

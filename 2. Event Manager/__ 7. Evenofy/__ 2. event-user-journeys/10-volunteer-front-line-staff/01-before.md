> Persona: Volunteer / Front-line Staff → Phase: Before the Event

1. **Recruitment.** They hear from a university or community network and want pride, experience, and clarity. Handoff: Volunteer Team.
2. **Vetting and training.** They submit availability, complete training, learn boundaries, and receive uniform and shift plan. Risk: vague instructions create unsafe improvisation. Handoff: Volunteer Manager.
3. **48-hour check-in.** A shift reminder, transport plan, supervisor contact, and weather guidance arrive. Handoff: Shift Lead.

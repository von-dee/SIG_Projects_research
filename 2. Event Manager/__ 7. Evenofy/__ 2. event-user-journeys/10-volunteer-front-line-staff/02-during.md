> Persona: Volunteer / Front-line Staff → Phase: During the Event

1. **Day 1 briefing.** They sign in, meet team, receive a zone task, and feel both proud and nervous. Handoff: Supervisor.
2. **Busiest day.** They guide queues, resolve simple questions, spot distress, and escalate rather than guess. Risk: fatigue or abusive guests. Delight: visible appreciation and a timely break. Handoff: Guest Services.
3. **Close.** They debrief, record issues, and learn next shift plan. Handoff: Volunteer Manager.

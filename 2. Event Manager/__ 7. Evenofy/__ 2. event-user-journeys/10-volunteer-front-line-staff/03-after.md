> Persona: Volunteer / Front-line Staff → Phase: After the Event

1. **Stand-down.** They return uniform and receive safe transport information. Handoff: Volunteer Team.
2. **48-hour recognition.** Thank-you message, certificate, photos, and expense process arrive. Handoff: People Team.
3. **30/60/90 days.** Alumni opportunities and feedback close the loop. Handoff: Community Team.

> Persona: Event Operations / War Room Staff → Phase: Before the Event

1. **Readiness planning.** Ops leads convert plans into owners, contingencies, and decision thresholds. They seek shared certainty. Handoff: Event Director.
2. **Exercises.** Security, medical, venue, and suppliers rehearse incidents and handoffs. Risk: unresolved ambiguity surfaces only live. Handoff: Incident Command.
3. **48-hour command posture.** They confirm staffing, alerts, contacts, and go/no-go checks. Handoff: War Room Lead.

> Persona: Event Operations / War Room Staff → Phase: During the Event

1. **Day 1 opening.** They monitor arrivals, gate flow, stage starts, and early exceptions. Handoff: Ops Lead.
2. **Busiest day.** A security concern, capacity shift, late VIP movement, and programme change may collide. They need concise facts, authority, and calm communication. Risk: parallel teams act on different versions. Handoff: Incident Commander.
3. **Close.** They reconcile incidents, reset resources, and brief night shift. Handoff: Duty Manager.

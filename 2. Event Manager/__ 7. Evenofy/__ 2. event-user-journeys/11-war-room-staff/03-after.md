> Persona: Event Operations / War Room Staff → Phase: After the Event

1. **Stand-down.** They confirm safe egress and close active incidents. Handoff: Ops Lead.
2. **48-hour review.** Timelines and lessons are captured while memories are fresh. Handoff: PMO.
3. **30/60/90 days.** Improvement actions are assigned and tested before the next edition. Handoff: Event Director.

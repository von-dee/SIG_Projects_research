> Persona: Virtual / Remote Attendee → Phase: During the Event

1. **Day 1 joining.** They enter the live lobby, verify stream quality, and learn how to ask questions. Handoff: Virtual Host.
2. **Busiest day.** They watch keynotes across time zones, join virtual roundtables, and seek equal recognition. Risk: delays and no route to ask questions make them passive viewers. Delight: a moderator brings in their question live. Handoff: Digital Producer.
3. **Close.** They save sessions and receive replay timing. Handoff: Virtual Support.

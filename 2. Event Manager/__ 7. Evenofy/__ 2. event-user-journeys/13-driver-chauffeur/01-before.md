> Persona: Driver / Chauffeur → Phase: Before the Event

1. **Assignment.** The driver receives approved passenger, vehicle, route, and contact details, seeking precision and discretion. Handoff: Transport Control.
2. **Credential and route check.** They verify vehicle pass, secure zones, timing, and contingency route. Risk: outdated directions cause a visible delay. Handoff: Security and Protocol.
3. **48-hour confirmation.** Final pickup window, passenger changes, and escalation line are confirmed. Handoff: Transport Desk.

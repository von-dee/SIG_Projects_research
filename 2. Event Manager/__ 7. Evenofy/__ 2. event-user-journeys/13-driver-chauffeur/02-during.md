> Persona: Driver / Chauffeur → Phase: During the Event

1. **Day 1 pickup.** They arrive early, verify passenger handoff, and follow the assigned route. Handoff: Liaison.
2. **Busiest day.** Concurrent movements, road closures, and changing agendas require calm waiting and exact communication. Risk: a driver contacts the principal directly when liaison should mediate. Handoff: Transport Control.
3. **Close.** They confirm final drop-off and next-day readiness. Handoff: Fleet Lead.

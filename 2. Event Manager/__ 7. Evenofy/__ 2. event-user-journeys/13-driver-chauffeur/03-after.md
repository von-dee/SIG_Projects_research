> Persona: Driver / Chauffeur → Phase: After the Event

1. **Departure movements.** They execute final airport or hotel transfer and confirm handoff. Handoff: Transport Control.
2. **48-hour closeout.** Mileage, incidents, and pass return are completed. Handoff: Fleet Lead.
3. **30/60/90 days.** Supplier feedback and future availability are recorded respectfully. Handoff: Transport Procurement.

## Cross-Persona Friction Points

- A VIP motorcade or security freeze can block general-attendee shuttles, exhibitors, and media. Give affected people early, plain-language rerouting while Protocol retains discretion.
- A keynote overrun harms speaker preparation, investor meetings, journalist filing, virtual streams, and F&B peaks. Use one authoritative change message and staffed recovery points.
- Restricted access can make students, delegates, and sponsors feel excluded. Clearly distinguish safety boundaries from hospitality choices and offer valuable alternatives.
- Registration queues compete with VIP discretion and volunteer capacity. Separate arrival paths without making standard delegates feel second-class.
- High-value meeting rooms can displace press interviews and accessibility needs. A neutral escalation owner must arbitrate based on event priorities.
- Late changes to travel or visas cascade across drivers, hotel teams, protocol, and delegate confidence. Maintain one liaison-led communication path.

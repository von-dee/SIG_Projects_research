## Open Questions and Assumptions

- Assumed a three-day flagship forum on a single campus with international arrivals and a sizeable exhibition.
- Confirm the protocol authority, security posture, visa support scope, and rules for diplomatic or press access.
- Confirm which travel, hotel, accessibility, dietary, and safeguarding services the organizer owns versus recommends.
- Validate mobile, WhatsApp, email, phone, and in-person support channels by audience and jurisdiction.
- Define service recovery authority: who may offer rerouting, upgrades, refunds, or personal apologies during disruption.
- Test these maps in co-design sessions with real protocol, venue, exhibitor, press, accessibility, and volunteer representatives before operational use.

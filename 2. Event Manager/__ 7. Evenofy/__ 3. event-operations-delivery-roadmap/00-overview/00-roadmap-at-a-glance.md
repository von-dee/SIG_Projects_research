## Roadmap at a Glance

| Period | Operational objective | Non-negotiable output | Decision gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Months 12 to 9 | Build the event's control foundation | Approved venue, protocol, access, budget baselines | Architecture readiness |
| Months 8 to 5 | Convert plan into revenue and qualified participation | Sellable inventory, live registration paths, speaker pipeline | Commercial and data-quality review |
| Months 4 to 2 | Turn demand into executable experiences | Seating, meals, meetings, mobile beta, locked configuration | Core-architecture freeze |
| Weeks 4 to 1 | Rehearse and finalize | Approved ROS, tested AV, printed credentials, staffed zones | Go/no-go readiness review |
| Showtime | Execute safely and adapt quickly | Controlled incidents, live capacity, reliable stage flow | Daily command review |
| Days 1 to 30+ | Prove value and retain trust | Reconciled accounts, stakeholder reports, privacy actions | Closeout and lessons review |

The roadmap assumes one flagship venue campus, a three-day forum, international delegations, exhibition, live broadcast, and virtual participation.

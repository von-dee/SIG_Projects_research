## Governance and Control Principles

1. **One accountable owner per decision.** Event Director owns cross-functional trade-offs; named leads own budget, protocol, access, programme, commercial, safety, and privacy decisions.
2. **Configure once, test repeatedly.** Access tiers, protocol precedence, venue inventory, and financial categories become controlled baselines before registrations open.
3. **No silent overrides.** Exceptions require named approver, reason, expiry, and visible impact on dependent teams.
4. **Protect sensitive information by purpose.** Passport, biometric, medical, diplomatic, and meeting data are collected only when necessary and handled by authorized teams.
5. **Freeze in stages.** Commercial promises, room types, access levels, agenda, presentation files, and badge data each have an explicit finalization point.
6. **Recover gracefully.** Every critical flow, especially access, stage management, VIP movement, and incident reporting, has an offline/manual fallback and a clear escalation owner.

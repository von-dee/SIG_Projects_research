> Phase 2: Development & Sales → Months 8 to 5 Before Event

## Development & Sales

### Objective

Secure revenue and qualified participation while turning commercial promises into operational obligations that every affected team can see.

### Workstream 1 — Commercial launch

Commercial releases approved sponsorship packages and exhibition inventory. Sponsors choose available spaces, accept terms, pay or commit through approved finance workflow, and receive a clear delivery plan covering branding, hosted guests, speaking rights, meetings, and passes.

**Dependency:** A signed premium sponsorship automatically creates tracked commitments for Content, Registration, Hospitality, Exhibition, and Finance. No sales representative may promise a speaking slot, VIP pass, or physical asset outside approved inventory.

### Workstream 2 — Registration paths and vetting

Marketing opens distinct, understandable routes for government, investor, delegate, press, speaker, exhibitor, student, and staff participants. Each person receives only the questions and supporting-evidence requests relevant to their access and purpose. Review teams manage incomplete, duplicate, and exceptional applications with respectful communications.

**Control:** Highly sensitive evidence is justified, access-restricted, and subject to a defined retention period. High-value investor or official status is verified through an appropriate human review path, not treated as automatic access.

### Workstream 3 — Speaker readiness

Content sends each speaker a secure invitation, session brief, travel guidance, biography request, publicity consent, technical expectations, and primary liaison. Moderators are introduced early enough to align tone and likely questions.

### Workstream 4 — Matchmaking preparation

The meetings team defines participant goals, industry interests, seniority, availability, meeting formats, and privacy choices. Early registrants see transparent preferences, not unexplained automated recommendations.

### Month 5 commercial-and-data gate

- Every sold package has a named fulfillment owner and due date.
- Registration review queues meet agreed service levels.
- Speaker acceptance and replacement plan meet programme targets.
- Matchmaking choices and privacy language are approved.
- Risk: disconnected sales promises create public embarrassment, missing passes, and unstaffed commitments during show week.

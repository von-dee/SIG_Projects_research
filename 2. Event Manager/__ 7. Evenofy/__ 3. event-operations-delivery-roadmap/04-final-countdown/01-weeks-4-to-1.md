> Phase 4: The Final Countdown → Weeks 4 to 1 Before Event

## The Final Countdown

### Objective

Turn approved plans into rehearsed show assets, valid credentials, staffed positions, and a locked run of show.

### Workstream 1 — Presentation and AV quality assurance

The Content team enforces a final asset deadline, validates aspect ratio, fonts, media, rights, captions, and playback, then rehearses the approved version with AV and Broadcast. The speaker receives a clear deadline and a personal escalation route well before lock.

**Control:** After lock, a change requires Stage Manager and AV Lead approval, a compatibility check, and a recorded reason. No untested personal USB is accepted on stage.

### Workstream 2 — Run-of-show finalization

Content, Production, Protocol, Broadcast, Security, and F&B reconcile a minute-by-minute plan: arrivals, holds, walk-ons, scripts, cues, videos, interpretation, interviews, and changeovers. Each stage rehearses normal timing and at least one late-VIP, overrun, and technical-failure contingency.

### Workstream 3 — Badges and arrival operations

Registration validates final cohorts, prints pre-approved credentials, runs sample scans across all required zones, prepares controlled reprint stations, and packages badges for secure pickup. Security holds a revocation and incident process for lost, duplicate, or suspicious credentials.

### Workstream 4 — Staffing and volunteer readiness

Ops assigns every shift, zone, break, supervisor, emergency contact, and backup. Staff receive practical route and escalation briefings; volunteers rehearse how to welcome, redirect, spot vulnerability, and escalate rather than improvise.

### Week 1 go/no-go review

- Approved ROS and stage-by-stage contingency pack.
- Every critical presentation tested and version identified.
- Sampled credentials work at required gates; offline fallback is rehearsed.
- All critical shifts filled, with a contactable reserve.
- Open risks have named owners and response deadlines.

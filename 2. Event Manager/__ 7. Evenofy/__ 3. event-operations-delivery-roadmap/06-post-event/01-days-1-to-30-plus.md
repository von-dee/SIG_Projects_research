> Phase 6: Post-Event → Days 1 to 30+ After Event

## Post-Event

### Objective

Close the event responsibly, prove value with credible evidence, preserve relationships, and dispose of sensitive data according to policy.

### First 24 to 48 hours — thank, listen, and secure

Attendees, speakers, sponsors, exhibitors, media, volunteers, and virtual participants receive a timely, relevant thank-you and a short feedback request. Questions are tailored to the experience they actually had. The team communicates access to recordings, certificates, approved images, meeting follow-up, or lead reports with clear timing.

**Control:** Survey recipients can decline further marketing. Sensitive feedback is routed only to teams that need it, while any safety or safeguarding concern follows the relevant escalation process.

### Days 3 to 14 — reconcile and report

Commercial and exhibitor teams validate lead-delivery obligations and give each sponsor only the contacts collected under the appropriate consent. Finance matches final supplier invoices to approved commitments, goods/services received, and agreed variations. Operations analyzes attendance, queueing, capacity, room use, stage starts, and service consumption. ESG consolidates energy, waste, travel aggregates, procurement evidence, and inclusion measures using documented methodology.

**Control:** A visually impressive heatmap or sentiment score is not treated as proof without context, source quality, and an accountable analyst. Reported conclusions distinguish observed data from estimates.

### Days 15 to 30 — debrief and improve

The Event Director runs a structured, evidence-led debrief. Teams review what happened, why it happened, what was decided, and what needs to change. “We thought” is replaced with indicators such as unused meal volume, no-show rate, gate peaks, meeting completion, delivery exceptions, and verified stakeholder feedback.

Each improvement has an owner, due date, cost implication, and test method. The group separates a one-off venue issue from a recurring design flaw before changing next year's plan.

### Day 30 onward — privacy, retention, and next-year pivot

Privacy and Legal execute the approved retention schedule. Data needed for contractual, financial, legal, safety, or consent records is retained only for the authorized period; sensitive identity evidence is securely deleted or anonymized when its purpose ends. Aggregated, non-identifying lessons may inform forecasting.

Relationship teams may invite highly satisfied, appropriately consented participants into next-year early-interest campaigns. The invitation is valuable and optional, never a substitute for honoring an opt-out request.

### Closeout gate

- Financial close exceptions have named owners and resolution dates.
- Sponsor and exhibitor deliverables have been issued and acknowledged.
- ESG and operational reports identify methods, quality, and limitations.
- Privacy actions are completed or on a legally approved hold.
- Improvement register has transitioned into next-year planning.

## Assumptions and Decision Log

| Topic | Working assumption | Decision required |
|---|---|---|
| Event scale | 10,000+ total credentialed participants and a multi-day programme | Confirm daily peak, venue caps, and staff ratio |
| Venue | One principal campus with exhibition, plenary, meeting rooms, and VIP areas | Confirm drawings, operating hours, and fire authority process |
| Protocol | Host government supplies precedence and diplomatic constraints | Confirm exception authority and information classification |
| Credentials | Tiered physical/digital credentials with a fallback process | Confirm hardware, escort rules, and revocation SLA |
| Presentations | Final tested asset is required before stage entry | Confirm deadline and exceptional-change authority |
| Sensitive data | Passport, identity, medical, and biometric data are minimized | Confirm legal basis, storage region, and retention schedule |
| Matchmaking | Participants opt in to discovery and meeting requests | Confirm whether assistant scheduling and government delegation rules differ |
| Surveys | Feedback requests are tailored by stakeholder type | Confirm send language, frequency, and marketing consent boundaries |

Review this log at every phase gate. Decisions should be dated, owned, and communicated to every team whose commitments or participant messages change.

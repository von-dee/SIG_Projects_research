> Module 1: Admin Command Center → Global Dashboard → KPI Overview

## KPI Overview
**Purpose & users:** Event Director, Career Services, Finance, and Ops view the event's health at a glance.

**Features:** date/event selector; registration totals by participant type; paid revenue, outstanding invoices, ticket goal progress, sponsor and booth sales; comparison to target and prior period; drill-down links; manual refresh timestamp; exportable executive snapshot.

**Workflow:** Choose event and reporting period → review metric cards and trend charts → filter by cohort or sales channel → open the underlying registration, invoice, or sponsor list → assign follow-up where a target is off track.

**States & guardrails:** show stale-source warning, empty-state setup guidance, and role-appropriate financial detail. Goal changes require Finance or Event Director approval and preserve previous target history.

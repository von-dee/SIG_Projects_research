## CAD Upload & Mapping
**Purpose & users:** Facilities and Ops turn venue drawings into a reliable planning map.

**Features:** CAD/PDF/image upload; version list; scale calibration; layer visibility; room, entrance, exit, utility, and accessibility annotation; measurement tool; review comments; publish status.

**Workflow:** Upload drawing → select page/layer → calibrate scale with known measurement → mark operational features → request Facilities review → publish approved base map to dependent pages.

**States & guardrails:** reject unsupported or corrupt files; preserve prior approved map when a new upload is under review. Published maps are versioned, and fire-exit layers cannot be edited by non-authorized users.

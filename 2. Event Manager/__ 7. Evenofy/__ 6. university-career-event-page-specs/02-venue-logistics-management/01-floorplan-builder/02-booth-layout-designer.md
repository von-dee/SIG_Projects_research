## Booth Layout Designer
**Purpose & users:** Ops and Corporate Relations allocate safe, sellable employer spaces.

**Features:** drag-and-drop booth library; grid snapping; zones; fire exits; networking lounges; booth dimensions; status colors; sponsor assignment; collision and egress checks; print/export view.

**Workflow:** Open approved map → place and size booth inventory → reserve emergency and lounge areas → run validation → release approved booths to sales → assign sold booth to employer and lock it.

**States & guardrails:** block placements over exit clearance or overlapping footprints. A sold booth move requires organizer approval and creates an employer notification task.

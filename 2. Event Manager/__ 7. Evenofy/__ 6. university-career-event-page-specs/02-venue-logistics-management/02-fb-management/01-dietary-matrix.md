> Module 2: Venue & Logistics Management → F&B Management → Dietary Matrix

## Dietary Matrix
**Purpose & users:** Catering and Ops forecast safe meal volumes without exposing unnecessary student information.

**Features:** meal-period selector; aggregate dietary counts; attendee type split; location forecast; late-change count; service notes; export for caterer; production cutoff indicator.

**Workflow:** Registration data accumulates → F&B Lead reviews counts by meal/location → adjusts operational buffer → approves catered quantity → sends aggregate order → monitors late changes.

**States & guardrails:** show data freshness and cutoff date. Individual medical details remain restricted; change after cutoff is flagged for manual safety decision.

## Catering Orders
**Purpose & users:** F&B Lead and Finance request, approve, and track campus catering services.

**Features:** order form; menu and service period; quantities; dietary totals; cost estimate; delivery location; vendor notes; approval chain; PO reference; receipt and variance tracking.

**Workflow:** Create order from dietary forecast → select menu/service details → submit for budget approval → issue to catering → confirm delivery and actual quantity → reconcile invoice.

**States & guardrails:** prevent unapproved orders from reaching vendor; changes show cost and cutoff impact. Closed service periods allow variance notes but no silent order edits.

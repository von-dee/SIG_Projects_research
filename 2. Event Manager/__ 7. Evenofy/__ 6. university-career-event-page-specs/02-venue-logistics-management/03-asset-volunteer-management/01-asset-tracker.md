## Asset Tracker
**Purpose & users:** Operations controls custody and condition of tablets, radios, AV gear, and rented items.

**Features:** asset registry; QR scan; checkout/check-in; custodian; location; due time; condition photos; maintenance status; loss report; inventory audit; export.

**Workflow:** Register or import asset → label with QR code → scan to issue to authorized staff → capture acknowledgement → scan return and condition → route damage or loss for review.

**States & guardrails:** block double checkout and flag overdue items. Offline scans queue locally; final return requires condition confirmation.

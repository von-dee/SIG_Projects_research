## Volunteer Scheduler
**Purpose & users:** Volunteer Managers place trained people in the right zone at the right time.

**Features:** shift timeline; volunteer availability; skills, language, and training flags; zone capacity; drag-and-drop assignment; break rules; open-shift alerts; publish and swap workflow; geofence settings.

**Workflow:** Define shifts and required roles → match eligible volunteers → resolve conflicts/breaks → publish schedule → volunteers acknowledge or request swap → supervisor monitors check-in.

**States & guardrails:** prevent overlapping assignments and untrained placement in restricted zones. Geofence failure offers a supervisor-review path for valid exceptions.

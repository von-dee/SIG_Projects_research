> Module 3: Commercial & Sponsorship Engine → Sponsorship Tiers & Packages → Tier Builder

## Tier Builder
**Purpose & users:** Corporate Relations creates consistent, sellable sponsorship packages.

**Features:** package name, price, inventory limits, benefits, booth size, pass allocation, speaking entitlement, branding assets, eligibility, terms, comparison view, draft/published status.

**Workflow:** Create package → add priced deliverables and quantity limits → review margin and inventory → obtain Commercial/Finance approval → publish to sales or employer portal → retire when sold out.

**States & guardrails:** prevent publishing without price, terms, or fulfillment owner. Editing a sold package creates a new version and identifies affected contracts.

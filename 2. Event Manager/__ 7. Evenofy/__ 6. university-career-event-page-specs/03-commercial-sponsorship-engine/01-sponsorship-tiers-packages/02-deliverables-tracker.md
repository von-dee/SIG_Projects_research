> Module 3: Commercial & Sponsorship Engine → Sponsorship Tiers & Packages → Deliverables Tracker

## Deliverables Tracker
**Purpose & users:** Sponsor Success and functional teams prove every contracted benefit was delivered.

**Features:** sponsor filter; benefit checklist; owner; due date; evidence upload; status; dependencies; notes; escalation; sponsor-facing completion summary.

**Workflow:** Contract activates deliverables → assign each to Content, Registration, Exhibition, or Marketing → owners update evidence → Sponsor Manager chases overdue work → validate completion → issue post-event proof.

**States & guardrails:** a deliverable cannot close without owner confirmation and required evidence. Contract changes recalculate obligations while retaining the original record.

## Master Budget
**Purpose & users:** Finance and Event Directors control planned, committed, and actual event spending.

**Features:** cost-center tree; budget, committed, actual, forecast, variance; filters; reforecast scenario; approvals; drill-down to PO/invoice; notes; executive export.

**Workflow:** Establish approved baseline → assign budget to categories → review commitments and actuals → flag variance → propose reforecast or corrective action → approve and publish revised forecast.

**States & guardrails:** only Finance can change baseline; negative available balance warns/blocks new commitments according to policy. All versions retain comparison history.

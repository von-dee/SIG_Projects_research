> Module 3: Commercial & Sponsorship Engine → Finance & Invoicing → Invoice & PO Center

## Invoice & PO Center
**Purpose & users:** Finance manages vendor commitments and sponsor receivables.

**Features:** PO and invoice lists; create/edit form; supplier/sponsor details; line items; tax; attachments; approval route; payment status; matching; overdue alerts; reminders; export.

**Workflow:** Create request → obtain approvals → issue PO → receive supplier invoice or sponsor payment → match to contract/receipt → resolve variance → mark paid or overdue.

**States & guardrails:** duplicate invoice reference is flagged; PO spend cannot exceed approved budget without exception. Payment confirmation is restricted to Finance.

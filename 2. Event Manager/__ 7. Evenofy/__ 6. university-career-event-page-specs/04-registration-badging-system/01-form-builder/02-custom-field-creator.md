> Module 4: Registration & Badging System → Form Builder → Custom Field Creator

## Custom Field Creator
**Purpose & users:** Authorized administrators define the information needed for event and career matching.

**Features:** field label/type; help text; validation; options; conditional display; required status; audience; consent text; reporting eligibility; archival status; form placement.

**Workflow:** Draft field → set privacy purpose and validation → test across relevant paths → obtain review for sensitive fields → publish → monitor quality → retire when purpose ends.

**States & guardrails:** sensitive questions such as GPA or work authorization require a documented purpose and limited audience. Retiring a field preserves past responses only under the retention policy.

> Module 4: Registration & Badging System → Badge & Check-In → Badge Designer

## Badge Designer
**Purpose & users:** Registration and Brand teams create scannable, legible credentials.

**Features:** badge size/template; drag-and-drop fields; QR placement; logos; color/access cues; preview by participant type; print margins; batch assignment; approval and version history.

**Workflow:** Choose stock/template → position identity, QR, role, and branding → preview sample records → validate scanner readability → approve → assign to access role → release for batch or kiosk printing.

**States & guardrails:** prevents QR overlap or out-of-print-area content. Access classification may not be implied by colors alone; the approved template is locked during print production.

> Module 5: Matchmaking & Scheduling Hub → AI Matchmaking Engine → Intent Field Setup

## Intent Field Setup
**Purpose & users:** Career Services defines the language used to pair students with employer opportunities.

**Features:** tag catalog; category hierarchy; definitions; synonyms; audience availability; required/optional status; import; deprecation; usage count; matching weight guidance.

**Workflow:** Create standard tags such as Tech Internships or Full-Time Engineering → group and describe them → make available to appropriate form paths → test search/match outcomes → publish → retire duplicates carefully.

**States & guardrails:** do not expose protected or discriminatory tags. Changing a tag preserves historical selections and maps replacements before retirement.

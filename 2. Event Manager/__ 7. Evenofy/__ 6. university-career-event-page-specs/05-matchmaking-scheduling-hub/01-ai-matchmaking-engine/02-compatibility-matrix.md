> Module 5: Matchmaking & Scheduling Hub → AI Matchmaking Engine → Compatibility Matrix

## Compatibility Matrix
**Purpose & users:** Career Services reviews and improves recommended student-employer connections.

**Features:** ranked match list; filters; score explanation; student/employer summary; availability; opt-in status; hide/boost controls; introduction action; feedback label; bias-monitoring aggregate view.

**Workflow:** Filter for employer or cohort → inspect why a match ranks highly → verify participation consent → send suggestion or concierge introduction → capture outcome feedback → refine approved matching rules.

**States & guardrails:** a score is advisory, never an automatic rejection. Hidden, opted-out, or restricted profiles are excluded; sensitive attributes are not used as hidden decision factors.

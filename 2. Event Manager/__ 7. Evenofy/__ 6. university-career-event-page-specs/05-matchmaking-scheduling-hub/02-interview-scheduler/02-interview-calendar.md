> Module 5: Matchmaking & Scheduling Hub → Interview Scheduler → Interview Calendar

## Interview Calendar
**Purpose & users:** Career Services coordinates all one-on-one interviews from one conflict-aware view.

**Features:** day/week grid; room, employer, student, and status filters; availability; holds; waitlist; calendar sync status; reschedule/cancel; check-in; no-show log; export.

**Workflow:** Select employer/student/room availability → propose or confirm 15-minute slot → send invitations → monitor acceptances → check in participants → handle late cancellation through waitlist or concierge.

**States & guardrails:** block student, recruiter, and room conflicts. External calendar failure leaves the internal booking pending and clearly labeled; only authorized staff see interview notes.

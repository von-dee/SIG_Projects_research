## Resume Aggregator
**Purpose & users:** Career Services produces controlled resume collections for eligible employers and sponsors.

**Features:** consented resume intake; PDF validation; categorization; search/filter; cohort selection; preview; watermark; generated package; expiry; download log; revoke access action.

**Workflow:** Select eligible, opted-in student cohort → validate files and categories → preview inclusion list → approve package → give authorized recipient time-limited access → monitor download acknowledgement.

**States & guardrails:** exclude students without explicit sharing permission. Failed or malicious files are quarantined; generated PDFs carry recipient watermark and package scope.

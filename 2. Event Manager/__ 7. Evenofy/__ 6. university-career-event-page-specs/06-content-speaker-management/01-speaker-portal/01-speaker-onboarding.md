> Module 6: Content & Speaker Management → Speaker Portal → Speaker Onboarding

## Speaker Onboarding
**Purpose & users:** Invited alumni, faculty, and external speakers complete their profile and event readiness.

**Features:** secure invitation; session summary; bio/headshot upload; travel and accessibility needs; release/consent forms; moderator contact; task checklist; support contact; completion status.

**Workflow:** Speaker opens invitation → verifies identity → reviews session → submits profile and requirements → signs forms → Content reviews → speaker receives approved briefing and next steps.

**States & guardrails:** expired links require verified resend; incomplete required tasks prevent readiness completion. Sensitive travel or accessibility information is visible only to assigned support roles.

> Module 6: Content & Speaker Management → Speaker Portal → Presentation QA Pipeline

## Presentation QA Pipeline
**Purpose & users:** Speakers, Content, and AV validate presentation assets before show day.

**Features:** upload; version history; format/aspect validation; font/media warning; accessibility/caption checklist; preview; reviewer comments; approve/reject; 72-hour lock countdown; final-download package.

**Workflow:** Speaker uploads deck → automated checks run → AV reviewer tests and comments → speaker replaces or confirms → reviewer approves final version → pipeline locks at deadline → Stage receives approved asset.

**States & guardrails:** failed checks clearly name remediation. After lock, a change follows an explicit exception request; unapproved USB substitutions are not accepted on stage.

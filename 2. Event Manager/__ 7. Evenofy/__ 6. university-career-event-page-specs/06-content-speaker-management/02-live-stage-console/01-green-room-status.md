> Module 6: Content & Speaker Management → Live Stage Console → Green Room Status

## Green Room Status
**Purpose & users:** Speakers and Stage Managers share a calm, immediate view of stage readiness.

**Features:** now/next/on-deck status; traffic-light indicators; countdown; session title; stage location; stage-manager note; acknowledgement; delay message; accessibility display mode.

**Workflow:** Stage Manager sets active session and speaker order → console updates next speakers → speaker acknowledges call → manager marks backstage/ready/on-stage → status advances or delay notice is issued.

**States & guardrails:** avoid showing confidential speaker details on public screens. A schedule delay retains clear “stand by” guidance rather than an inaccurate countdown.

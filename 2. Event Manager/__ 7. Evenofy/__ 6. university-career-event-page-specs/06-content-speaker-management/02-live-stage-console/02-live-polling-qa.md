## Live Polling & Q&A
**Purpose & users:** Moderators collect and curate student questions and audience polls during sessions.

**Features:** live question queue; upvotes; moderation; anonymity choice; topic tags; poll builder; timer; result display; hide/report action; exportable session transcript.

**Workflow:** Moderator opens session → launches poll or accepts questions → reviews/report filters → approves appropriate questions → displays results or reads selected questions → closes and exports outcomes.

**States & guardrails:** moderation queue protects against abuse; personally identifying content is minimized. Connectivity loss preserves submitted items locally and displays a clear reconnect state.

> Module 7: Employer Portal → Employer Dashboard → Registration & Payment

## Registration & Payment
**Purpose & users:** Corporate recruiters purchase participation, sponsorship, and booth inventory.

**Features:** organization profile; package comparison; booth map; cart; add-ons; terms; billing contact; invoice/payment options; receipt; contract status; support route.

**Workflow:** Sign in or create organization → select tier and available booth → add services → enter billing details → accept terms → pay or submit invoice request → receive confirmation and onboarding checklist.

**States & guardrails:** held inventory expires transparently; a sold booth cannot be double selected. Payment failure preserves cart without confirming the order; only organization administrators can accept commercial terms.

> Module 7: Employer Portal → Employer Dashboard → Recruiter Roster

## Recruiter Roster
**Purpose & users:** Employer administrators prepare their recruiting team for a smooth event experience.

**Features:** invite/add recruiter; role; email; badge status; dietary and parking requests; access/pass allocation; arrival details; remove/reassign; completion tracker.

**Workflow:** Employer admin adds each team member → recruiter completes personal items → admin assigns included passes → organizer reviews special requests → badges and logistics update before event.

**States & guardrails:** pass allocation cannot exceed package entitlement without purchase or approval. Recruiters manage their own sensitive preferences; admin sees completion state, not unnecessary details.

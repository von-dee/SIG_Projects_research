> Module 7: Employer Portal → Talent Acquisition Hub → Student Database Search

## Student Database Search
**Purpose & users:** Authorized recruiters discover opted-in students relevant to open roles.

**Features:** search; filters for major, graduation year, skills, work authorization, and permitted GPA; saved searches; candidate cards; profile preview; resume request/download; notes; interview action; export controls.

**Workflow:** Select approved filters → review candidate results → open an opted-in profile → save, contact, or invite qualified student → record recruiter notes → track follow-up.

**States & guardrails:** availability and fields follow consent and university policy. Bulk export, GPA access, and resume download are limited to approved employer entitlements and logged.

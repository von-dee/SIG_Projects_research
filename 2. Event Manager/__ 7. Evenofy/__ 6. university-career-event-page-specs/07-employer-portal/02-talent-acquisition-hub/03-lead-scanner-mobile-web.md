> Module 7: Employer Portal → Talent Acquisition Hub → Lead Scanner (Mobile Web)

## Lead Scanner (Mobile Web)
**Purpose & users:** Recruiters capture consented student connections on a mobile device.

**Features:** camera scan; manual code entry; candidate confirmation; consent indicator; resume access; recruiter notes/tags; duplicate alert; offline queue; sync status; lead list.

**Workflow:** Open scanner → request student permission → scan QR → confirm correct profile → save approved contact/resume and note → continue scanning → review/sync leads after connectivity returns.

**States & guardrails:** no profile is revealed until a valid, permitted scan. Duplicate scans merge into one lead; offline captures are encrypted and clearly marked pending synchronization.

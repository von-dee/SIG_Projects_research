> Module 7: Employer Portal → Talent Acquisition Hub → ROI Dashboard

## ROI Dashboard
**Purpose & users:** Employer leaders evaluate recruiting outcomes after participation.

**Features:** total leads; unique resumes; interviews booked/completed; recruiter notes; source/booth traffic; conversion funnel; team comparison; export; follow-up checklist.

**Workflow:** Select event and team → review aggregate engagement → drill into approved lead list → assign post-event owners → export permitted report → provide event feedback.

**States & guardrails:** show data freshness and define each metric. Individual student details remain subject to access expiry and consent; benchmark reporting is aggregated.

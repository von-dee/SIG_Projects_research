> Module 8: Student Portal → Student Dashboard → Registration & Resume Upload

## Registration & Resume Upload
**Purpose & users:** Students create a complete, controlled profile for career-event participation.

**Features:** university sign-in; profile details; major/year; career interests; work-authorization preference; accessibility needs; PDF resume upload; preview; data-sharing choices; completion checklist; support.

**Workflow:** Sign in → confirm prefilled university information → complete required event fields → upload and preview resume → select employer-sharing preferences → submit → receive badge and agenda next steps.

**States & guardrails:** validate file type/size and explain each sharing choice. Students can revise profile and consent before published cutoffs; sensitive answers are not exposed to unapproved employers.

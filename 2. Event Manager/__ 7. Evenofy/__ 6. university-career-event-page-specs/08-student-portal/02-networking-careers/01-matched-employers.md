> Module 8: Student Portal → Networking & Careers → Matched Employers

## Matched Employers
**Purpose & users:** Students discover relevant organizations without feeling reduced to an opaque score.

**Features:** employer cards; match reasons; hiring roles; booth/map location; accessibility; save/hide; follow/notify; recruiter event sessions; interview request route; profile visibility controls.

**Workflow:** Review matches → open employer details and explanation → save or hide → plan booth visit or request interview → adjust interests/visibility to improve future suggestions.

**States & guardrails:** recommendations are advisory and explainable. Hidden employers or opted-out students are excluded; no match implies a student is unqualified.

> Module 8: Student Portal → Networking & Careers → Interview Invites

## Interview Invites
**Purpose & users:** Students control their response to employer interview requests.

**Features:** invitation details; employer profile; proposed slots; room/map; accept/decline; alternative time; calendar add; reminders; preparation notes; cancel policy; support route.

**Workflow:** Receive notification → review employer and slot → accept, decline, or propose available alternative → add confirmed meeting to agenda → check in → record personal follow-up after interview.

**States & guardrails:** accepting checks agenda conflicts; declined reason is optional and private. A late cancellation follows a fair policy and gives Career Services an opportunity to help.

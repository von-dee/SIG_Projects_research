> Module 8: Student Portal → Networking & Careers → Digital Business Card

## Digital Business Card
**Purpose & users:** Students share a controlled professional identity with recruiters and peers.

**Features:** QR code; display name; degree/major; selected contact methods; resume-sharing toggle; preview; scan history; regenerate/revoke; accessibility contrast option.

**Workflow:** Choose information to share → preview card → present QR at conversation → recipient scans and confirms permitted details → review sharing history → revoke or regenerate if needed.

**States & guardrails:** card never exposes private contact details without student choice. Revocation invalidates future scans, while a history explains prior sharing.

> Module 8: Student Portal → Post-Event → Placement Tracker

## Placement Tracker
**Purpose & users:** Students optionally record post-event career progress; Career Services views aggregate outcomes.

**Features:** status options; employer selection; offer/interview date; optional notes; reminder schedule; privacy preference; edit history; support resources; aggregate impact consent.

**Workflow:** Student receives follow-up prompt → selects status such as offer received or still looking → optionally names organization and next action → saves → receives relevant support or congratulations → updates later.

**States & guardrails:** status is self-reported and clearly labeled. Employer-specific outcome sharing requires appropriate consent; students can withdraw or correct their entry.

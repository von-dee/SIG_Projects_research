## NPS & Survey Engine
**Purpose & users:** Event Directors and University Administration gather and understand stakeholder feedback.

**Features:** audience-specific survey builder; send schedule; NPS score; response rate; question breakdown; comment themes; sentiment review; filters; privacy-safe exports; action tracker.

**Workflow:** Select audience and approved questions → schedule post-event send → monitor response and reminder cadence → review score/comments → validate themes → assign improvement actions → publish approved summary.

**States & guardrails:** suppress unsubscribed recipients and protect small cohorts from identification. Automated sentiment is a triage aid, with human review before public conclusions.

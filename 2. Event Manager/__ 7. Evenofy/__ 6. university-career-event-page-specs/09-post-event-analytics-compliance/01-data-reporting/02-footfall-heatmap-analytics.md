## Footfall & Heatmap Analytics
**Purpose & users:** Organizers understand venue use, booth interest, and session drop-off.

**Features:** map/time heatmap; booth and room filter; occupancy trend; dwell-time distribution; entry/exit counts; comparison periods; export; methodology and data-quality label.

**Workflow:** Select event day and area → inspect traffic/dwell pattern → compare sessions or booths → identify over/underused spaces → export aggregate insight for debrief and planning.

**States & guardrails:** results use aggregated, privacy-safe data and state scanner coverage. Do not infer individual behavior from incomplete or low-volume observations.

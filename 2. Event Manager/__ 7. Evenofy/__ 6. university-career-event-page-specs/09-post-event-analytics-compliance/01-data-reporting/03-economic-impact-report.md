> Module 9: Post-Event Analytics & Compliance → Data & Reporting → Economic Impact Report

## Economic Impact Report
**Purpose & users:** Leadership assesses career and institutional value created by the event.

**Features:** participation totals; employer count; interviews; leads; placement outcomes; sponsorship revenue; methodology notes; cohort filters; narrative sections; chart export; approval status.

**Workflow:** Select reporting period → review verified inputs → calculate approved measures → add contextual narrative and limitations → obtain leadership sign-off → share stakeholder-ready report.

**States & guardrails:** distinguish actual from estimated results and avoid double counting. Small groups are aggregated or suppressed; placement figures respect student consent.

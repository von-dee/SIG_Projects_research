> Module 9: Post-Event Analytics & Compliance → Data Compliance (FERPA) → Data Retention Settings

## Data Retention Settings
**Purpose & users:** Privacy Officers and authorized administrators set lifecycle rules for sensitive student data.

**Features:** data category; retention period; legal/policy basis; deletion/anonymization action; event scope; scheduled jobs; legal-hold override; preview of affected records; execution log; review reminders.

**Workflow:** Classify data such as GPA or resume → choose approved 30/60/90-day rule → preview impact → obtain Privacy approval → activate → review completion and exceptions.

**States & guardrails:** legal hold pauses disposal without exposing data broadly. Destructive changes require confirmation and audit logging; policy version is retained after records are purged.

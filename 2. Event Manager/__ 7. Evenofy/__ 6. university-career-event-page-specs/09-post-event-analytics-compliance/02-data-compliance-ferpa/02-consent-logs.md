> Module 9: Post-Event Analytics & Compliance → Data Compliance (FERPA) → Consent Logs

## Consent Logs
**Purpose & users:** Privacy and authorized Career Services users prove which student data sharing was permitted.

**Features:** student/purpose/employer filter; consent version; grant/withdraw timestamps; collection channel; scope; related access events; export; correction request; audit history.

**Workflow:** Search a consent question → verify current scope and history → respond to access or withdrawal request → notify connected process where required → retain fulfillment evidence.

**States & guardrails:** logs are immutable and access-restricted. Withdrawal stops future sharing promptly but does not falsely erase legitimate historical transaction evidence.

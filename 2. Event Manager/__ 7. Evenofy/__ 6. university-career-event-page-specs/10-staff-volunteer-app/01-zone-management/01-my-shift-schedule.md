> Module 10: Staff & Volunteer App → Zone Management → My Shift Schedule

## My Shift Schedule
**Purpose & users:** Event-day staff and volunteers see where they are needed and confirm attendance from mobile.

**Features:** upcoming/past shifts; zone/map; start/end/break; supervisor/contact; clock-in/out; geofence status; shift acknowledgement; swap/request help; offline display; reminders.

**Workflow:** Staff reviews published schedule → acknowledges shift → arrives at assigned zone → clocks in → completes work and break → clocks out → records issue or availability update.

**States & guardrails:** geofence failure presents a review request, not a silent rejection. Staff see only their own assignment details; offline clock events synchronize with clear status.

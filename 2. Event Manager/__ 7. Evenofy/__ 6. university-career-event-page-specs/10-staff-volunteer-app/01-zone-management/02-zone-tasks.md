> Module 10: Staff & Volunteer App → Zone Management → Zone Tasks

## Zone Tasks
**Purpose & users:** Front-line teams execute clear, safe duties in assigned areas.

**Features:** prioritized task list; checklist; instructions; map; due time; status; task acceptance; photo/evidence; comments; escalation button; supervisor assignment; offline queue.

**Workflow:** Open assigned shift → review tasks for Stage 2 or Check-in Desk A → accept next task → follow checklist → mark complete with required evidence → raise blocker or incident → supervisor verifies where needed.

**States & guardrails:** restricted tasks are hidden from unassigned users. Completion requiring supervisor inspection remains pending; offline updates show synchronization state and never overwrite a later safety instruction.

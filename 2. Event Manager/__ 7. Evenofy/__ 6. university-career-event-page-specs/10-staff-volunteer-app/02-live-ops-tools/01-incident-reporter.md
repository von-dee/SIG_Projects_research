## Incident Reporter
**Purpose & users:** Staff quickly report spills, medical concerns, AV failures, and other issues without needing to judge every response.

**Features:** category; severity guide; zone auto/manual location; description; photo; immediate danger toggle; submit/acknowledgement; reporter follow-up; offline capture; emergency-call action.

**Workflow:** Select issue category → read immediate-safety prompt → add concise location/details/photo → submit → receive acknowledgement and instruction → remain available or escalate to emergency service when directed.

**States & guardrails:** urgent medical or safety choices prominently offer emergency action. Reporter does not see restricted investigation details; failed submission is safely queued and visibly marked.

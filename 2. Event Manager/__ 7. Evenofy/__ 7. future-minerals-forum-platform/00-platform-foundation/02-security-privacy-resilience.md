# Security, Privacy & Resilience

Classify data as public, internal, confidential, restricted, or diplomatic/security-sensitive. Enforce SSO/2FA, least privilege, tenant/event scope, approval separation, field masking, encryption in transit and at rest, export controls, and an immutable audit trail. PDPL/GDPR consent, purpose limitation, retention, access requests, and legal-hold overrides are policy-driven.

Field clients use encrypted local storage, signed configuration bundles, cached entitlement lists, idempotent outbox queues, conflict handling, and clear offline/freshness indicators. Emergency workflows preserve availability while requiring later reconciliation and supervisor review.

# Lifecycle & Phase Mapping

| Phase | Primary capabilities | Decision gate |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation & Strategy (M12–9) | Commercial packages, budget, event setup, access model | Baseline scope and controls approved |
| Development & Sales (M8–5) | Sales, invitations, visa, speaker outreach | Contracts and invited populations reconciled |
| Execution & Marketing Push (M4–2) | Floorplan, catering, matchmaking, exhibitor self-service | Supplier and inventory readiness signed off |
| Final Countdown (W4–1) | ROS, presentation QA, bilaterals, security registry | Week 2 configuration freeze |
| Showtime | Command Center, stage, security, staff field workflows | Incident command and operational handover |
| Post-event (D1–30) | Reconciliation, outcomes, ESG, surveys, follow-up | Retention and reporting close-out |

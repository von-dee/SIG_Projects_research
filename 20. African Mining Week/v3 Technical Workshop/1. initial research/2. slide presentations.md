
To create a high-quality product promo video like **"How to create Slide presentations with Gemini"** (from the Google Workspace YouTube channel), you need to follow a structured production workflow. These short, polished videos combine clean screen recordings, slick motion graphic animations, a clear voiceover script, and tight video editing.

---

1. **Scriptwriting & Storyboard:**
   Focus on a clear problem-and-solution structure. Introduce the user persona (e.g., *Camille from marketing*), the challenge (building a Q3 review presentation), and show how the product solves each pain point step-by-step. Break the script down into a two-column table with visual cues on one side and spoken voiceover text on the other.
2. **Screen Recording & Software Mockups:**
   Record the user interface interactions in high resolution (1080p or 4K at 60 FPS) with clean cursor movements. For major software launches, visual teams often create stylized 2D/3D mockups of the UI using tools like Figma, After Effects, or Rive to make the UI look smoother and larger than standard screen capture software.
3. **Voiceover & Sound Design:**
   Record a professional voiceover using a studio microphone or hire a voice actor via platforms like Voice123 or Fiverr. Layer in upbeat, modern corporate background music, subtle cursor click sounds, UI chime effects, and sound transitions to keep the audience engaged throughout the video.
4. **Motion Graphics & Post-Production:**
   Assemble the screen capture footage in editing software such as **Adobe Premiere Pro**, **DaVinci Resolve**, or **Final Cut Pro**. Use **Adobe After Effects** or **Motion** to animate text overlays, dynamic zooms, highlighting effects around UI buttons, and smooth transition cuts between feature scenes.
5. **Color Grading & Export:**
   Apply consistent color grading to match company brand colors, optimize lighting contrast, and export using the standard H.264 or HEVC codecs at 1080p or 4K resolution (16:9 aspect ratio) optimized for YouTube playback.

# Reference Guide: Pitching Ideas Effectively

## Main Takeaways

- **Your responsibility is to be heard.** It isn't the listener's job to pay attention; it's the presenter's job to earn it.
- **Sell yourself before you sell the idea.** Building a connection first matters more than leading with numbers and features.
- **Share what the idea means to you.** Explain how you came up with it, why you're invested in it, and why you think it will work.
- **First impressions form fast.** About 50% of the time, the decision is made within the first two minutes.
- **Subconscious signals outweigh conscious content.** Body language and presence shape trust before your words do.
- **You must equal the idea.** Your demeanor should embody what you're selling (stability, fun, seriousness).
- **Be equal to the moment.** Your tone should fit the context of the pitch.
- **Speak the language of your audience.** The more senior the listener, the more you should focus on outcomes, value, and business propositions.
- **Tailor the pitch to the person.** A manager and a CEO need different framing of the same idea.

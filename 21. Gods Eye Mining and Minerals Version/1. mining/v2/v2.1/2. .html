02 // Capabilities

Tactical Mineral Intelligence

From the Pilbara to the Strait of Malacca. Monitor the physical internet of mining in real-time.

Flight Tracking & Cockpit View

Track geological survey flights and heavy-lift cargo helicopters. Jump into cockpit mode for live telemetry and nearby traffic context.

Bulk Carrier Tracking

Monitor ore vessels at Port Hedland or the English Channel. Click tracks to zoom in, cache paths, and watch dock action live.

Voice Control AI

Powered by GPT real-time. "Take me to the Bingham Canyon mine." "Draw a route from Port Hedland to Newman mine." It's that easy.

Mine Site CCTV

View primary crushers, haul roads, and shift changes. Real-time images refreshed for privacy but dense enough to monitor choke points.

Thermal & Disaster Watch

NASA satellite data detects coal seam fires and refinery flares. Overlay earthquake data to monitor induced seismicity around tailings dams.

Physical Internet Map

Visualize submarine cables and power grids. Understand why aluminum smelters cluster near low-carbon nuclear hubs and coastal landing spots.

The Opportunity

An open-source platform with a commercial ceiling.

The MIT License lets you build commercial products on God's Eye View without asking permission, paying royalties, or open-sourcing your own code. You can host it, rebrand it, bolt on premium data, sell it to enterprises, or use it as the backbone for an intelligence service. The floor is free; the ceiling is whatever your market will pay.

📜

MIT License summary: You can use commercially, modify, distribute, private-use, and sell — as long as you include the original copyright notice. No copyleft. No royalty. No share-alike. You keep 100% of your revenue. The only constraint: bundled datasets carry their own terms (see DATA_SOURCES.md), so check each data source's license before reselling.

⚠️

The one line you cannot cross: The project explicitly forbids features for named-person search, face recognition, or tracking individuals. Any business model that monetizes person-level surveillance will be rejected upstream and is not viable on this platform. People are not a query type.

Pricing Playbook

How to price without leaving money on the table.

Real-world pricing frameworks for each model tier. The rule: charge for the outcome, not the access.

🎯 VALUE-BASED PRICING

  • If your port-congestion alert saves a trader $100K on one position, charge $500/mo — it's a no-brainer
  • If your ESG report prevents a $2M regulatory fine, charge $2K/mo — it's cheap insurance
  • If your white-label deploy unifies 12 data silos a $200K/yr analyst was maintaining, charge $300K
  • Never price by "cost + margin" — price by value delivered

📈 TIER PSYCHOLOGY

  • Always offer 3 tiers — the middle one should be the "obvious choice"
  • Free tier must be useful enough to hook, limited enough to upgrade
  • The expensive tier makes the middle one look reasonable (decoy effect)
  • Annual prepay at 20% discount improves cash flow & reduces churn
  • Enterprise: "Contact us" — never show a price above $2,000/mo
💡

The "Land and Expand" pattern: Start with a $500 pilot (Model 02 or 06). Deliver something impressive in 2 weeks. Then propose the $50K/yr platform (Model 01 or 05). Mining companies have budgets — but they buy trust first, then scope. Your first deal is always smaller than your second.

Legal & Ethics

Stay on the right side of the line.

The MIT license gives you broad commercial rights, but data sources, privacy laws, and the project's own ethical guardrails still apply.

📜 LICENSE COMPLIANCE

  • MIT License: include the original copyright & permission notice in your product
  • You are NOT required to open-source your modifications
  • Bundled datasets in local_data/ have their own licenses — check each
  • Each upstream data source (USGS, AISStream, etc.) has its own ToS — read before reselling
  • Some free sources prohibit commercial API resale (AISStream, adsb.lol) — upgrade to commercial tier
  • Google 3D Tiles commercial use requires a direct Google Maps key — not the free Cesium ion path

⚖️ DATA PRIVACY

  • GDPR: if you serve EU users, need DPA, cookie consent, data subject access
  • Do not store individual person data — GEV models systems, not people
  • CCTV feeds are public, but republishing them commercially may need city permission
  • Vessel AIS data is public — but some operators pay for "dark AIS" detection (legal)
  • If hosting user-uploaded data, need a data processing agreement

🚫 THE LINE

  • No named-person search, face recognition, or individual tracking — ever
  • No person-level surveillance features — the upstream project will reject PRs
  • If a client asks for "track my workers" — decline and offer "track my assets" instead
  • ALPR (license plate) integrations exist as community PRs — assess your jurisdiction's laws before deploying
  • SDR radio monitoring of police frequencies is legal in some jurisdictions, illegal in others

🛡️ LIABILITY

  • Data is delayed, incomplete, modeled, or wrong — include this disclaimer everywhere
  • "Not for navigation, emergency response, safety-critical decisions" — put it in your ToS
  • If selling risk scores (Model 08), add "not a guarantee, not insurance advice"
  • If selling commodity alerts (Model 04), add "not investment advice"
  • Get professional liability insurance if serving enterprise clients
  • Form an LLC/corp to shield personal assets

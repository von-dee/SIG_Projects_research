§ 06

The Moat

Five layers of defensibility. By Year 3, the combination of these makes the platform effectively un-copyable — a competitor would need to re-integrate 117 data sources, re-build 25 inference engines, and re-acquire 500+ paying customers.

The Network Effect Thesis

Every new data source improves every inference. Every new customer generates proxy validation data (e.g., "this mine confirmed our shadow-production estimate"). Every new inference recipe becomes a feature no competitor has. By Year 3, the platform's 117-source × 25-inference matrix is a combinatorial asset that cannot be replicated by hiring 10 engineers and raising $50M. It requires 60 months of layered integrations, customer feedback loops, and regulatory trust. That is the moat.

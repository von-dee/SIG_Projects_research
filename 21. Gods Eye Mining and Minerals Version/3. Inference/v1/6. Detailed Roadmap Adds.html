Pricing Strategy & Methodology

Pricing is value-based, not cost-plus. The methodology: price at 10% of the annual decision value the inference unlocks. If the inference saves a $100M tailings dam from failing, the annual subscription is $100k. If it prevents a $10M bad M&A bet, it's $250k.

§ 18

The Moat · 5 Layers of Defensibility

By Year 3, the platform is effectively un-copyable. A competitor would need to re-integrate 117 sources, re-build 25 inference engines, and re-acquire 500+ customers. Here's why each layer compounds.

The Network Effect Thesis

Every new data source improves every inference. Every new customer generates proxy validation data. Every new inference recipe becomes a feature no competitor has. By Year 3, the 117-source × 25-inference matrix is a combinatorial asset that cannot be replicated by hiring 10 engineers and raising $50M. It requires 60 months of layered integrations, customer feedback loops, and regulatory trust. That is the moat.

Voice vs. Hands-On

// WHEN TO USE WHICH

They aren't competitors — they're two interfaces to the same 28 tools. Use them together; the agent state and the panel state are the same state.

Reach for voice when…
· You want a compound action in one breath ("take me to LAX and select the nearest aircraft")
· You're mid-cockpit and your hands are off the mouse
· You want a place briefing while you look at it
· You want a real boundary polygon, not a hand-drawn circle
· You're screen-recording and want a director's hand
· You want to interrogate the live scene ("how many flights over Texas?")
OpenAI Key
Reach for hands-on when…
· You're exploring with no keys configured
· You want pixel-precise framing for a screen capture
· You're calibrating a CCTV pose gizmo
· You want to scrub a Space Mission timeline
· You're drawing exact vertices on the real world
· You want to step through the 250 km contacts roster one by one

Pro Tips

// OPERATOR NOTES
Chain commands. "Take me to Rotterdam" → "Mark the port" → "Start a slow orbit" → "Tell me about it" → "Draw a route to Antwerp" → "Fly that route." Each one builds on the last. The agent keeps context.
Terrain warm-up matters. First visit to a region? Planes and cameras may clip into the ground until the 3D tiles load. Chill in the area for a few seconds before tracking a landing airliner.
Try the rapid-fire tier. "Show me global infrastructure." "Play Orbital Watch." "Set detection density to fifty percent." "Next contact — helicopters only." One sentence each, full mission stage.
Voice is the only thing that costs real money. A live session-spend readout sits next to the mic, with a $2 warning and a $5 hard cap. Toggle STD/Mini to trade quality for cost.
The agent is honest. It only confirms actions that succeeded. If a layer didn't toggle or a route didn't draw, it says so — no false confirmations.
Responsible use. No named-person search. No face recognition. No tracking individuals. People are not a query type here. PRs that cross that line won't be merged.

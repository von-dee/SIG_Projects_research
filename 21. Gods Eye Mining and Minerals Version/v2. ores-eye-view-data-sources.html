Vehicle positionsSimulated haul-truck movement along the networkSimulated client-side — not a live observation of any real vehicle
On accuracy. Coordinates, tonnages, and classifications above are approximate and change as operations open, close, or are re-estimated. Several fields — camera orientation, disturbance footprints, and all haul traffic — are explicitly modeled or simulated rather than observed, and the console labels them as such wherever they're shown. This page describes the kind of source each field would draw from; it is not a live feed of any of these providers.

Data points & sources

Every field the console displays, layer by layer, and where it comes from. Fields marked modeled or simulated are estimates or illustrative reconstructions rather than direct observations — the console labels them as such wherever they appear.

Each row links its primary source, followed by alternative or cross-check sources you can swap in or use to validate a value. The last section lists extra datasets worth adding as new layers.

On accuracy. Coordinates, tonnages, and classifications above are approximate and change as operations open, close, or are re-estimated. Several fields — camera orientation, disturbance footprints, and all haul traffic — are explicitly modeled or simulated rather than observed, and the console labels them as such wherever they're shown. This page describes the kind of source each field would draw from; it is not a live feed of any of these providers.

On links and licensing. Links point to each provider's home, documentation, or dataset page and may move over time. Check each provider's terms before redistributing data: exchange prices (LME, CME), Google 3D Tiles, and commercial AIS feeds all carry licensing or attribution requirements, and OSM data must be credited under ODbL.

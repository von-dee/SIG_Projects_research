- **Base case:** it covers its operating costs (about 20% EBITDA margin) but the return is below a 12% hurdle rate and payback is beyond 8 years. Loan repayments are also tight in Years 1-2.
- **Downside:** this is a loss-making case, which shows how exposed the farm is to milk and cattle prices and feed costs.
- **Break-even milk price:** about 0.46 per litre in cash terms, or about 0.57 once depreciation and interest are included, against a 0.60 assumed price.

The base case is capital-heavy and barely reaches the hurdle rate. Milk yield, milk price and how much you spend on infrastructure are the levers that matter most. Change those first.

**Limits:** I left out VAT, exchange-rate moves and drought-year herd losses beyond the Downside case. Tax is simple, with no loss carry-forward, so check your local farm tax rules.

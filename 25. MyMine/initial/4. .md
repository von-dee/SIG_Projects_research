
Here are feature ideas that would add business value, grouped by who pays for or benefits from them. They are my suggestions, not anything from your document, and each would need feasibility checking.

**Finish what's already on the roadmap first**

- **Plan-vs-actual overlay.** This is probably the highest-value feature for the GM and investor, because it shows whether the mine is delivering to plan.
- **Audit trail.** Add decision and annotation logs with timestamps and who made them. This helps with investor reporting, incident reviews and disputes with contractors.

**Investor and commitments**

- **Commitment tracker.** Turn promises like the forest buffer into monitored rules: the buffer is a saved boundary, and the model flags when mining geometry gets within a set distance. This builds on the closing scene of your story.
- **Auto-generated investor reports.** Produce a monthly snapshot with survey date, progress and layer views, so Naledi gets a record without a site call.
- **ESG and compliance reporting.** Export evidence of buffer compliance, hazard briefings held and exclusion zones in place, in formats lenders and regulators accept.

**Operations and productivity**

- **Time-slider replay.** Step back through past surveys to see how a bench or pit wall changed. This is useful for reviews, claims and training.
- **Volume and progress measurement.** Calculate material moved between two surveys directly from the model, then reconcile it against plan and reported production.
- **Equipment and haul-route view.** Show live positions with simple rules, such as vehicles entering an exclusion zone, as an alert for supervisors only.
- **Task and action tracker.** Pin actions from a review to locations in the model with an owner and due date, so decisions don't live only in meeting notes.

**Safety and training (careful with claims)**

- **Induction completion records.** Log who attended a briefing, which supports your HSE value case.
- **Scenario-based training modules.** These could include walkthroughs of past incidents or emergency routes. They need real content and review by safety professionals.
- **Zone change notices.** Push a notice when an exclusion zone moves. Keep this as communication support, not an early-warning or certified safety function.

**Maintenance**

- **Equipment history pinned to assets.** Attach service records, manuals and past expert annotations to each machine in the model, so every breakdown builds a knowledge base.
- **Session recording and replay.** Record expert-assist calls so future crews can learn from them.

**Platform and commercial**

- **Role-based access and external guest links.** This lets OEMs, auditors and lenders see only what they should, and it makes remote assist practical.
- **Integrations.** Connect to fleet management, SCADA, maintenance systems and survey tools through standard connectors. This lowers the cost of adding data sources beyond the first one or two.
- **Multi-site portfolio view.** For owners with several mines, one dashboard comparing sites is a strong upsell.
- **Offline-first mobile capture.** Let site staff take photos and notes tagged to model locations and sync later, which fits the edge-sync story.

**If I had to prioritise:** plan-vs-actual, the audit trail, the commitment tracker and investor reports. They sit closest to your investor-confidence story and mostly reuse data you already plan to hold. Role-based access should come early too, since remote assist and outside experts depend on it.

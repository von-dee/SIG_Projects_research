# Côte d'Ivoire Mining Atlas: Product Specification

Companion to the published web app. Data as of the 21 Oct 2026 sector note. "n/d" means not disclosed in the note.

## 1. Purpose and audience
One place for investors, counsel and policy readers to see who mines what, under which terms, and what could change. The review of the note asked for mine-by-mine tables, a pipeline, regulation tables, a regional comparison, risks, scenarios and open items. Each is a page.

## 3. Data model

- **Mine**: id, name, metal, owner, region, stage, lat/lon (approximate), stakes, latest output, outlook, tax-holiday status, notes.
- **Project**: name, company, stage, first production, note, capex, funding, maturity (analyst estimate).
- **Mineral**: name, status, potential, detail.
- **Player**: name, role, assets, note.
- **Title / Fiscal term / Law**: reference lists for the Regulation page.
- **Risk**: name, category, likelihood 1-5, impact 1-5, description. Scores are an analyst view.
- **Watch event**: when, what, tag.
- **Open item** and **Source**: for traceability.

## 4. UX decisions
- Left rail with three groups. Collapses to a Menu button below 820 px.
- Global search jumps to Mines and filters live. On Players it filters that table.
- Detail opens in a right drawer and closes with Escape or the Close button.
- Light, dark and system themes. Watchlist, units and theme persist in the browser only.
- Colour carries state: green producing, amber development, red high risk, gold for gold metal.
- Every chart has a text alternative or tooltip. Keyboard focus is visible.

## 5. Calculation rules
- 1 oz = 31.1035 g. 1 koz = 0.0311 t.
- Fiscal calculator: revenue = koz x 1000 x price. Royalty = revenue x (royalty % + extra ad valorem pts). Taxable profit = revenue - costs - royalty (floored at 0). Corporate tax is zero while a holiday is active. State free carry = carry % of after-tax profit. State take = royalty + tax + carry share. This is a simplified model. The tax rate, price and cost are examples.
- Scenario path: existing output declines at the chosen rate each year from 2026. Koné, Assafou, Séguéla and other projects add output on fixed ramp curves. Compare 2030 with 100 t.

## 6. Known gaps (carried from the note review)
Reserves, all-in sustaining costs, capex, title fees and durations, FX and repatriation rules, ICSID and arbitration terms, EITI status, Doropo (AngloGold Ashanti), Afema owner (Turaco Gold), Allied Gold ownership change, exact SODEMI stakes, Ghana/Mali/Burkina/Guinea fiscal terms. These show as n/d and are listed on the Sources & open items page.



Here's a list of the main players in mining and metals intelligence, starting with the two you named. This is from my general knowledge, so check current product lines and pricing with each vendor.

**The two you named**

1. **S&P Global Market Intelligence** is the former SNL Metals & Mining. It has a large database of mines, projects, ownership, reserves and resources, exploration budgets, and company financials. It's widely used for corporate and M&A research.
2. **Wood Mackenzie** provides asset-level data, cost curves, supply and demand forecasts, and strategy consulting across metals, mining, energy and power. It is well known for commodity outlooks and benchmarking.

**Alternatives**

3. **Benchmark Mineral Intelligence** covers battery and energy-transition minerals (lithium, nickel, cobalt, graphite, rare earths). It offers price assessments, supply chain data, and forecasts.
4. **Fastmarkets** is a price reporting agency for metals, mining, and raw materials such as lithium, cobalt, steel, and scrap, and also publishes market news and forecasts. Its prices are often used as contract benchmarks.
5. **CRU Group** offers market analysis, price assessments, and cost models for base metals, steel, fertilizers, and mining inputs. It also does consulting.
6. **Argus Media** is a price reporting agency for energy and commodities, with growing coverage of metals and battery materials.
7. **Project Blue** focuses on critical and specialty minerals (e.g. bauxite/alumina, gallium, germanium, rare earths), with supply chain and pricing analysis.
8. **Metals Focus** specializes in precious metals (gold, silver, PGMs) and some base metals, with supply, demand, and price forecasts. It's well regarded for gold and silver.
9. **Mining Journal Intelligence** (part of Aspermont) provides project and company data, news, and industry reports aimed at mining professionals.
10. **GlobalData** covers mining projects, companies, deals, and market forecasts as part of a broad cross-industry platform.
11. **Fitch Solutions (BMI)** produces country risk analysis, commodity forecasts, and mining industry reports. It's useful for jurisdiction and political risk.
12. **BloombergNEF** is strong on energy transition and battery metals, covering demand outlooks, supply chains, and technology cost trends.
13. **Rystad Energy** is mainly an energy research firm, but it has expanded into metals and mining supply cost and project analysis.
14. **SRK Consulting** is an independent technical consultancy, not a data vendor. It does resource estimation, mine design, feasibility studies, and technical due diligence.
15. **Mining Weekly Research (Creamer Media)** focuses on African and South African mining, with company and project coverage and industry data.

**Quick picks**

- **Mine and company databases:** Mining Journal Intelligence and GlobalData
- **Battery and critical minerals:** Benchmark, Project Blue, BloombergNEF
- **Pricing benchmarks:** Fastmarkets, Argus, CRU
- **Precious metals:** Metals Focus
- **Country and political risk:** Fitch Solutions (BMI)
- **Technical and independent studies:** SRK

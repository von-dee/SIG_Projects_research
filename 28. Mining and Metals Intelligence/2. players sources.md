
I'll look up the official data portals and report pages for each of the 15 providers, then compile them into one list with links.

Almost all of these providers are subscription businesses, so their "data repos" are paid platforms and most reports sit behind a paywall or a lead-capture form. Every link below came from search results, and I haven't opened each one. Where a provider only showed a homepage or a thin result, I say so rather than guess at a URL.

**1. S&P Global Market Intelligence**

- Data: [Capital IQ Pro Metals &amp; Mining](https://www.spglobal.com/marketintelligence/en/campaigns/world-mining-intelligence-coverage). It includes 15+ years of mining deal histories and mine cost-competitiveness benchmarking.
- Reports:
  - [World Exploration Trends hub](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/resources/world-exploration-trends-2026): the 2026 edition found global exploration budgets fell for a third straight year, to $12.40B.
  - [Corporate Exploration Strategies 2025 overview](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/2025/11/ces-2025-overview-exploration-in-numbers)
  - [World Exploration Trends 2021](https://pages.marketintelligence.spglobal.com/world-exploration-trends-2021-report-access.html) (borderline for your five-year window)
  - Monthly Commodity Briefing Service: [copper](https://pages.marketintelligence.spglobal.com/copper-briefing-service-feb2023-EMC-demo-request.html), [lithium and cobalt](https://pages.marketintelligence.spglobal.com/lithium-and-cobalt-CBS-demo.html), [gold](https://pages.marketintelligence.spglobal.com/EMC-2405-GL-MM-DC-CIQPro-DCD-Commodity-Briefing_Gold-CBS-download--demo.html)

**2. Wood Mackenzie**

- Data: [Lens metals and mining methodology](https://support.woodmac.com/en/articles/14481082-lens-metals-mining-markets-methodology). It covers the metals market forecasts and cost data behind the platform.
- Reports: [Outlook reports store](https://www.woodmac.com/store/outlook-reports/). The only mining-specific report page I found was from 2016, so I can't point you to dated mining reports within your window.

**3. Benchmark Mineral Intelligence**

- Data:
  - [Intelligence platform](https://www.benchmarkminerals.com/intelligence): over 2,500 price assessments.
  - [API docs](https://api-docs.benchmarkminerals.com/)
  - Price pages: [lithium](https://www.benchmarkminerals.com/lithium/prices), [nickel](https://www.benchmarkminerals.com/nickel), [cobalt](https://www.benchmarkminerals.com/cobalt)
- Reports: a free account gives you three pieces of content a month. See the [Source article on the platform](https://source.benchmarkminerals.com/article/benchmark-intelligence-making-sense-of-the-energy-transitions-most-complex-supply-chains) and the [lithium price methodology PDF](https://a.storyblok.com/f/333611/x/07e78f664b/benchmark-lithium-price-assessment-methodology.pdf).

**4. Fastmarkets**

- Data: [Metals and mining prices](https://fastmarkets.com/metals-mining-prices). Their pages quote between 950+ and 1,500+ prices, depending on which page you read.
- Reports: I found no specific dated report pages. Forecasts and analysis are sold through their platform.

**5. CRU Group**

- Data: [crugroup.com](https://www.crugroup.com) (services and communities).
- Reports:
  - [Emerging Miners Market Essentials (PDF)](https://www.crugroup.com/globalassets/campaigns/emerging-miners-market-essentials/emerging-miners-market-essentials.pdf)
  - [Copper Insights on CME](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/metals/base/copper-insights.html)
  - The homepage also features special reports on CBAM and US tariffs.

**6. Argus Media**

- Data: [Battery Materials service](https://view.argusmedia.com/BatteryMaterials). It has over 200 price assessments and long-term forecasts. Also [Non-Ferrous Markets](https://www.argusmedia.com/ru/metals/argus-non-ferrous-markets) (Russian-language page).
- Reports: [Battery materials hub](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/markets/battery-materials). It lists "The Coming Battery Supply Chain Shock" and "Battery Energy Storage: Market Dynamics and Outlook to 2030" as featured items.

**7. Project Blue**

- Data and reports: [projectblue.com](https://projectblue.com), which I could only verify as a homepage. A USGS notice shows the agency buying a one-year subscription for 26 reports covering 22 critical mineral commodities.

**8. Metals Focus**

- Data: it is the official data provider to the World Gold Council, The Silver Institute and the World Platinum Investment Council. Those bodies' free data is a useful route, but I didn't verify their links.
- Reports:
  - [Precious Metals Investment Focus 2025/26 coverage](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/gold-set-to-soar-even-higher-in-2026-metals-focus-2025-10-27): it forecasts gold averaging $4,560/oz in 2026.
  - [Precious Metals Investment Focus 2024/25 coverage](https://www.crown.co.za/latest-news/modern-mining-latest-news/30432-metals-focus-publishes-precious-metals-investment-focus-2024)
  - [Metals Focus topic page](https://www.miningweekly.com/topic/metals-focus)

**9. Mining Journal Intelligence**

- Data: [mining-journal.com](https://www.mining-journal.com). It also has a Mining IQ Risk Analytics tool.
- Reports:
  - Project Pipeline Handbook: [2021](https://mining-journal.com/edition/1000338/mining-journal-intelligence-project-pipeline-handbook-2021), [2022](https://mining-journal.com/edition/1000335/mining-journal-intelligence-project-pipeline-handbook-2022), [2023](https://mining-journal.com/edition/1000337/mining-journal-intelligence-project-pipeline-handbook-2023), [2024](https://www.mining-journal.com/edition/1000774/mining-journal-intelligence-project-pipeline-handbook-2024). The 2024 edition picks the top 50 projects from a database of 450+.
  - Newer titles listed on the site: Project Pipeline Index 2025, World Risk Insights 2025, ESG Index 2025, Investor Sentiment Insights 2026. I didn't get direct links for these.

**10. GlobalData**

- Data: [Mines and Projects database](https://www.globaldata.com/product/mining-mines-projects-database/). It tracks 34,070 mines and projects across 168 countries. There is also a [marketplace listing](https://globaldata.com/marketplace/mining/mining-projects/).
- Reports: [Mines and Projects report store](https://www.globaldata.com/store/industry/mines-and-projects/page/5/). Titles include North America Mining Review (2026), Global Manganese Mining to 2035 and China's Mining Fiscal Landscape 2025.

**11. Fitch Solutions (BMI)**

- Data: the BMI platform is subscription-only, and I found no public landing page.
- Reports:
  - [Mining Weekly&#39;s BMI coverage](https://www.miningweekly.com/topic/bmi) tracks its published mining outlooks.
  - [Industrial metals forecast upgrade](https://www.mining.com/fitch-solutions-bmi-upgrades-industrial-metals-forecasts/): BMI sees copper averaging $11,900/t in 2026.

**12. BloombergNEF**

- Data: BNEF terminal and client access only.
- Reports:
  - [Transition Metals Outlook 2024](https://about.bnef.com/?p=202177): a free summary is available online, and the full report is for clients.
  - [2024 press release](https://about.bnef.com/insights/finance/mining-industry-needs-2-1-trillion-dollars-in-new-investment-by-2050-to-meet-net-zero-demand-for-raw-materials-finds-bloombergnef-in-new-report/)
  - [First Transition Metals Outlook, 2023](https://about.bnef.com/insights/commodities/transition-metals-become-10-trillion-opportunity-as-demand-rises-and-supply-continues-to-lag/)

**13. Rystad Energy**

- Data: [Energy Metals solution](https://www.rystadenergy.com/services/energy-metals-solution) and [metals and materials overview](https://www.rystadenergy.com/energy-themes/supply-chain/energy-metals/). The metals coverage leans toward pricing for tubulars and energy metals rather than mining projects.
- Reports: [GES Report 2025](https://pages.rystadenergy.com/GES-Report-2025). This is an energy report, not mining-specific.

**14. SRK Consulting**

- Data: none. [srk.com](https://www.srk.com) is a consultancy site.
- Reports: SRK's independent technical reports surface through US filings. These SEC exhibits are consent letters that name the SRK-authored reports, not the reports themselves.
  - [Albemarle, Greenbushes, Salar de Atacama, Silver Peak and Wodgina](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/915913/000091591324000016/exhibit2321231202310-k.htm)
  - [MP Materials, Mountain Pass](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801368/000180136823000009/mpmcexhibit232123122srk.htm)
  - [SRK feature in Mining Journal](https://www.mining-journal.com/partners/partner-content/4410382/future-exploration-innovation-driving-discovery)

**15. Mining Weekly Research (Creamer Media)**

- Data: Research Channel Africa, a PDF report library covering mining, energy and infrastructure sectors.
- Reports: [Africa Mining Projects in Progress 2024](https://engineeringnews.co.za/article/africa-mining-projects-in-progress-2024-2024-04-03). Other titles sit behind the subscription.

Wood Mackenzie, Fastmarkets, Fitch BMI and Project Blue are the weakest on specific dated report links, because their catalogues are gated.

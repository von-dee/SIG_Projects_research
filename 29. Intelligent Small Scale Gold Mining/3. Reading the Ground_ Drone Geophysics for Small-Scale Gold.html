Four features, four magnetic signatures

Each panel shows a cross-section with the magnetic profile a drone would record when flying across it.

Magnetic dyke

Strong, sharp linear high

An igneous sheet cutting across older rock. Its magnetite gives a narrow positive anomaly. It’s a structural marker and helps date events.

Fault or shear zone

Linear break, offset or truncation

Displaced blocks cut magnetic trends. Alteration along it can add a linear low. Faults are the main plumbing for gold-bearing fluids.

Quartz vein

Neutral, non-magnetic

Silica filling a fracture. Quartz is diamagnetic so the vein itself barely registers. Its host structure and altered walls are what you map.

Magnetite destruction

Localised low or “shadow”

Hot fluids replace magnetite with non-magnetic minerals. The resulting low is one of the best pointers to an alteration halo around gold.

From flight plan to first trench

The kit is only half the job. Each step needs someone who can plan, process and interpret.

1

Plan

Set line spacing and height for the target. Check local drone rules and permits first.

2

Fly

The drone flies the lines while a ground base station logs corrections and daily field changes.

3

Process

Correct, level and grid the data in software such as GeoHammer or MAGNETO.

4

Interpret

Trace dykes, faults and lows. Targets sit where a fault meets a magnetite-destruction zone.

5

Ground-truth

Pit, trench or drill the best targets only. Geophysics narrows the search, it doesn’t confirm gold.

Limits to know about

Veins aren’t magnetic

Magnetics finds the faults, host rocks and alteration around veins, not the veins.

LiDAR sees surfaces

A buried gravel channel only shows if it leaves a surface expression. Confirm by pitting.

Dense forest

Canopy cuts the number of ground points that reach the surface.

You need an interpreter

Processing and interpretation skills are half the cost. Vendor basics training starts around $1,150.

Realistic payback

For a single small licence, buying rarely pays back. The case gets strong when you survey for several licence holders, or when the data directly stops wasted drilling and digging.

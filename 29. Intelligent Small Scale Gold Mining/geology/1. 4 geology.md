
The four features—**Magnetic Dykes**, **Faults / Shear Zones**, **Quartz Veins**, and **Magnetite Destruction**—represent interconnected components of a **hydrothermal mineralising system** and its corresponding **geophysical magnetic map signatures**.

---

### 1. The Geological & Hydrothermal Relationship

In mineral exploration, these four features interact along a common structural and fluid pathway:

* **Faults / Shear Zones (The Conduit):** Serve as the fundamental plumbing system, creating fractures and structural traps that allow deep-seated hydrothermal fluids and magma to ascend through the Earth's crust.
* **Magnetic Dykes (The Igneous Markers):** Represent intrusive igneous sheets that frequently utilize these structural fractures to intrude into host rocks.
* **Magnetite Destruction (The Chemical Alteration):** As hot, chemically active hydrothermal fluids travel along the fault zones, they react with surrounding rocks, breaking down primary magnetic minerals like magnetite into non-magnetic minerals (e.g., pyrite, carbonate, chlorite).
* **Quartz Veins (The Mineralized Host):** Precipitate directly out of silica-rich hydrothermal fluids in the open fractures or dilational jogs of the fault zone, serving as the main sites for ore deposition (such as gold and sulfides).

---

### 2. The Geophysical / Magnetic Signature Relationship

On an aeromagnetic map, the four features contrast directly with one another to guide mineral targeting:

| Feature                         | Physical Nature              | Magnetic Signature                        | Exploration Function                                    |
| :------------------------------ | :--------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Magnetic Dyke**         | Intrusive igneous sheet      | **Strong Linear High**              | Structural marker & cross-cutting timeline              |
| **Fault / Shear Zone**    | Fracture / displacement zone | **Linear Discontinuity / Offset**   | Identifies fluid pathways & structural traps            |
| **Magnetite Destruction** | Hydrothermal fluid reaction  | **Localized Low ("Bleached Zone")** | Vectors toward hydrothermal alteration halos            |
| **Quartz Vein**           | Precipitated silica infill   | **Neutral / Non-Magnetic**          | Pinpoints direct ore deposition sites within structures |

---

### Summary

In short, **faults** direct the fluid flow, **magnetite destruction** creates a magnetic low identifying where those fluids reacted with rock, **quartz veins** host the economic mineralization within those altered zones, and **magnetic dykes** help map the broader structural framework.

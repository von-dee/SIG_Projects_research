Here is the redrawn 14-day Ghanaian meal plan.
All instances of kontomire have been completely removed and replaced with iron-rich gboma leaves, ayoyo, and local spinach/waterleaf.
The plan continues to isolate calcium and caffeine while pairing non-heme plant iron with Vitamin C boosters to maximize absorption.
------------------------------
## 🗓️ Week 1 Meal Plan## Day 1

* Breakfast: Hausa Koko (millet porridge) served with a small handful of roasted groundnuts. Skip dairy milk; sweeten lightly if desired.
* Lunch: Red-Red (Gobɛ). Black-eyed peas cooked in palm oil, paired with fried ripe plantains. Garnish with a fresh, raw tomato and onion salad on the side for Vitamin C.
* Dinner: Light Goat Soup with boiled yam. Ensure the soup includes a generous blend of fresh tomatoes and hot peppers.

## Day 2

* Breakfast: Two boiled eggs with a side of sliced avocado and a freshly squeezed orange.
* Lunch: Leftover Light Goat Soup paired with fufu or boiled plantain.
* Dinner: Gboma Leaf Stew (Abomu style) made with African eggplant leaves, smoked mackerel, and palm oil. Serve with boiled green plantains or cocoyam.

## Day 3

* Breakfast: C-REAL Fortified Cereal or plain oatmeal made with water. Top with sliced strawberries or a side of fresh pawpaw (papaya).
* Lunch: Leftover Gboma Leaf Stew with boiled rice or yam.
* Dinner: Fresh Mackerel Pepper Soup served with a side of boiled sweet potatoes.

## Day 4

* Breakfast: Tom Brown (roasted corn porridge) served with a small side of roasted groundnuts. No dairy milk.
* Lunch: Beef Jollof Rice served with a large side portion of steamed cabbage, carrots, and green bell peppers.
* Dinner: Grilled chicken breast served with Garden Egg Stew and boiled white rice.

## Day 5

* Breakfast: Avocado toast on whole-wheat bread topped with two fried eggs and sliced tomatoes.
* Lunch: Leftover Beef Jollof Rice with a side of fresh salad tossed in lemon juice dressing.
* Dinner: Ayoyo Soup (jute leaves) with fish and minced beef, served with tuo zaafi.

## Day 6

* Breakfast: Millet Porridge topped with pumpkin seeds and paired with a fresh tangerine or orange.
* Lunch: Leftover Ayoyo Soup with tuo zaafi or boiled yam.
* Dinner: Egusi Stew made with fortified melon seeds, local spinach, and chunks of lean beef. Serve with boiled yam.

## Day 7

* Breakfast: Scrambled eggs cooked with lots of diced onions and bell peppers, served with a slice of whole-wheat toast.
* Lunch: Waakye (rice and beans) served with fish, a boiled egg, and a fresh tomato-based wele sauce.
* Dinner: Leftover Egusi Stew paired with eba or boiled plantain.

------------------------------
## 🗓️ Week 2 Meal Plan## Day 8

* Breakfast: Hausa Koko (millet porridge) paired with koose (fried bean cakes). Enjoy a fresh orange afterward.
* Lunch: Leftover Waakye with fish and fresh salad.
* Dinner: Snail and Smoked Fish Stew served with boiled green plantains.

## Day 9

* Breakfast: Two boiled eggs, sliced avocado, and a side of fresh mango or pawpaw.
* Lunch: Leftover Snail and Smoked Fish Stew with boiled yam or rice.
* Dinner: Groundnut Soup made with lean chicken breast, served with omo tuo (rice balls).

## Day 10

* Breakfast: Fortified oatmeal porridge cooked with water, mixed with ground flaxseeds or pumpkin seeds.
* Lunch: Leftover Groundnut Soup with rice or boiled plantain.
* Dinner: Pan-seared Beef Liver (Akwaba) quickly sautéed with green bell peppers, onions, and tomatoes. Serve with boiled potatoes.

## Day 11

* Breakfast: Scrambled eggs mixed with finely chopped fresh waterleaf (bokoboko) and tomatoes, wrapped in a whole-wheat tortilla or eaten with bread.
* Lunch: Leftover sautéed Beef Liver with rice or yam.
* Dinner: Palm Nut Soup made with fresh mackerel and crabs, served with fufu or boiled rice.

## Day 12

* Breakfast: Tom Brown porridge served with a side of roasted cashew nuts or groundnuts.
* Lunch: Leftover Palm Nut Soup with rice or boiled plantain.
* Dinner: Waterleaf & Egusi Stew cooked with corned beef, served with boiled sweet potatoes.

## Day 13

* Breakfast: C-REAL Fortified Cereal served with a side of fresh sliced strawberries or citrus fruits.
* Lunch: Leftover Waterleaf & Egusi Stew with boiled yam.
* Dinner: Grilled Tilapia marinated in ginger, garlic, and hot pepper, served with banku and fresh raw pepper sauce (containing raw tomatoes).

## Day 14

* Breakfast: Avocado slices and hard-boiled eggs with a side of fresh fruit salad (oranges, pawpaw, and mango).
* Lunch: Leftover Grilled Tilapia with banku or boiled rice.
* Dinner: Black-Eyed Pea Soup made with diced carrots, tomatoes, and lean beef chunks, served with warm crusty whole-wheat bread.

## 💡 Absorption Tips for the Alternative Greens

* Moringa Boost: Feel free to dry fresh moringa leaves, crush them into powder, and sprinkle a spoonful over the morning porridges (Hausa Koko or Tom Brown). It adds a major iron boost without triggering an allergic reaction.
* Squeeze the Lime: Because gboma, ayoyo, and waterleaf contain plant-based iron, remember to squeeze fresh lime or lemon juice over your stews just before eating to unlock the iron content.

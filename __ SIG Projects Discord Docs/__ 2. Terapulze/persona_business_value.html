Basis for These Estimates

Each persona below is scored on the same three dimensions: the time the task takes done manually versus with TerraPulze, the cost that time and rework represents, and the business outcome that follows from getting there faster.

Figures are directional estimates built from typical West African junior-exploration cost structures (drilling, field mobilization, technical advisory and consulting rates) and the workflow steps identified in the Persona Workflow Analysis. They are illustrative of the scale of value TerraPulze can create, not audited client figures — treat them as a starting frame to validate with real pilot data.

Persona 1

Field Geologist

Decides where to look, and whether what's on the ground matches the model.

Time: Desk-Based Target Selection

Manual
~15 days
With TerraPulze
~1 day

AI-generated, confidence-rated anomalies replace the manual desk study that would otherwise precede every field mobilization.

Cost Avoided

Avoiding one unproductive field mobilization (crew, vehicle, fuel, accommodation) saves roughly $3,000–$8,000 per trip. Avoiding a single unproductive 100m drill hole guided to a false-positive target saves $15,000–$30,000 at typical $150–$300/m drilling costs.

Business Value

More field days spent verifying real anomalies instead of searching blind; a faster, evidence-backed path from raw concession to drill-ready target; offline navigation removes round-trips to base for data.

Manual
~6 days
With TerraPulze

Cost Avoided

On a typical $500K–$2M annual junior exploration budget, a 10–15% improvement in allocating spend toward higher-probability targets represents $50,000–$300,000 redirected away from lower-priority ground, plus reduced analyst/GIS technician hours each review cycle.

The Bottom Line

Across all six personas, the pattern is the same: TerraPulze does not replace expert judgment, it removes the weeks of manual reconciliation that used to sit in front of it. Every persona gets the same underlying evidence, formatted for the decision they actually have to make.

Days → Hours  |  Ad-hoc → Standardized  |  Reactive → Continuous

The compounding value sits in the shared evidence layer: a target validated by a field geologist strengthens the case an Exploration Manager makes to a CEO, which is the same evidence a Consultant checks, a Banker prices, and a Regulator approves — collected once, trusted everywhere it's reused.

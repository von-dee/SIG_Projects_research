01 / The Challenge

Current Data Challenges

Fragmented Data

Mining intelligence is locked in silos, scattered across manual logs, spreadsheets, and disconnected departments.

Poor Visibility

Lack of real-time geospatial oversight makes it near impossible to accurately monitor active mineral production.

Limited GIS systems

Legacy GIS tools are complex to install, hard to update, and lack the collaborative web-centric agility teams need today.

Difficult Reporting

Generating compliance, regulatory, or operational reports takes weeks of manual collation, leaving stakeholders in the dark.

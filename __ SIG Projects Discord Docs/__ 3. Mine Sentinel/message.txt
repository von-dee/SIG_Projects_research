#  [TIPS]
## Ideal customer profile eg for a company

Key Components to Include Firmographics: Industry, Size, Revenue, Location.Budget: Ability to pay for your solution.Technology Used: Specific software they use that makes them a better fit.Pain Points: The specific problem you solve (e.g., security, efficiency).Ideal User/Buyer: Job titles (e.g., "VP of Sales," "Engineering Manager").A well-defined ICP enables shorter sales cycles and higher customer retention

## Questionnaire template
Interview Questionnaire
Section 1 — Background
What is your role and what does your organization do in the mining sector?
How involved are you in mining data, mineral reporting, or GIS monitoring?
What kind of mining or mineral information do you work with most often?

Examples:

gold production
concession data
geological surveys
environmental reports
export data
Section 2 — Current Workflow
How do you currently collect or access mining-related data?
What tools or software do you currently use?
How is mining information shared internally?
How long does it usually take to generate reports or retrieve mining insights?
Is your current workflow mostly:
manual
spreadsheet-based
GIS-based
software-driven
Section 3 — Pain Points (MOST IMPORTANT)
What are the biggest challenges you face with mining data or monitoring today?
What information do you wish was easier to access?
Are there any delays, inaccuracies, or reporting bottlenecks?
Have there been situations where poor visibility or missing data caused problems?
How difficult is it to consolidate mining data from multiple sources?
How often do these problems occur?
daily
weekly
monthly
rarely
Which of these problems is the MOST painful?
Section 4 — Existing Alternatives
Are there existing platforms or systems you currently rely on?
What do you like about them?
What do you dislike or wish they could do better?
Have you tried building internal systems for this before?
Section 5 — GIS & Monitoring Validation
How important is geospatial/map-based visibility for your work?
Would interactive mining maps improve your workflows?
What mining insights would you want to see on a dashboard?

Examples:

production
concessions
illegal mining
exports
environmental indicators
Section 6 — Budget & Purchasing Signals
Does your organization currently pay for:
analytics tools
GIS platforms
mining intelligence software
Who typically approves software purchases?
What factors make a platform worth paying for?
Would faster or centralized mining intelligence save:
time
money
operational effort
compliance effort
How valuable would that be operationally?
Section 7 — Priority & Urgency
Compared to other operational problems, where does this rank?
Is this problem urgent enough that leadership discusses it regularly?
If a reliable platform existed today, how likely would your organization be to test it?
Section 8 — Closing Questions
What would an ideal mining intelligence platform look like for you?
What features would be absolutely essential?
Is there anyone else you think we should speak with?
Would you be open to:
early access
pilot testing
follow-up discussions
Questions You SHOULD Ask Verbatim

These are especially important.

Ask:

“Tell me about the last time this problem happened.”

This gets real examples instead of opinions.

Ask:

“How are you solving this today?”

This reveals:

competitors
workarounds
spending behavior
Ask:

“What happens if this problem is not solved?”

This reveals:

urgency
business impact
willingness to pay

**The Intelligent Mine: Connecting AI Across the Full Mining Value Chain, From First Drill Hole to Final Rehabilitation**

**Description:**
Most mining operations run AI as a set of isolated tools — one system for market and opportunity analysis, another for geological modelling, another for scheduling, another for maintenance, another for ESG reporting after the fact. This technical workshop argues that AI's real value isn't optimizing any single stage in isolation — it's connecting all of them through one continuously updated intelligence layer, one that also keeps operators aligned with regulators and government requirements at every stage.

Using a single opportunity-to-closure case thread, this session walks through how that connectivity works in practice:

* **Market Research & Opportunity Finding:** How AI screens commodity trends, jurisdictional risk, and geological databases to rank exploration targets before a single hole is drilled
* **Exploration → Resource Estimation:** How AI-ranked drill targets feed directly into bankable resource models
* **Government & Regulatory Alignment:** How AI tracks permitting timelines, licensing conditions, and evolving mining regulations, keeping operators compliant and audit-ready with national and regional authorities throughout the mine's life
* **Planning:** How that estimate drives a live, continuously updated mine plan instead of a static annual one
* **Extraction & Processing:** How blast and grade-control precision feeds higher-value ore to a plant that optimizes recovery in real time
* **Logistics:** How fleet and haulage data keep that ore moving to port without silent value loss
* **ESG & Closure:** How the same underlying data, enriched with safety and environmental signals, produces an audit-ready ESG record and a funded closure plan years ahead of need

Participants will leave with a practical framework for evaluating AI investments not by department, but by how well they connect across the value chain — from the first market signal to final rehabilitation — while staying aligned with the regulatory bodies that govern the sector. The session will also address what "integrated" AI actually requires in terms of data architecture, regulatory alignment, and cross-functional buy-in.

**Format:** Technical panel/workshop | **Audience:** Mine planners, technical directors, digital transformation leads, government and regulatory affairs teams, ESG and compliance teams, investors

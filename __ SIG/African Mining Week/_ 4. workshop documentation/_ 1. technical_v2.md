## Title
**The Intelligent Mine: Connecting AI Across the Full Value Chain — From First Drill Hole to Final Rehabilitation**

*(Alternative, punchier option: "The Intelligent Mine: One AI Layer, Every Stage, Full Compliance")*

## Brief

**Description:**

Most mining operations run AI in silos — one tool for opportunity screening, another for geological modelling, another for scheduling, another for maintenance, another for ESG reporting bolted on at the end. This technical workshop makes the case that AI's real value isn't optimizing any single stage — it's connecting all of them through one continuously updated intelligence layer, kept aligned with regulators at every step.

Using a single case thread that runs from opportunity to closure, the session shows how that connectivity works in practice:

- **Market Research & Opportunity Finding** — How AI screens commodity trends, jurisdictional risk, and geological databases to rank exploration targets before a single hole is drilled
- **Exploration → Resource Estimation** — How AI-ranked drill targets feed directly into bankable resource models
- **Government & Regulatory Alignment** — How AI tracks permitting timelines, licensing conditions, and evolving regulations to keep operators compliant and audit-ready across the mine's life
- **Planning** — How that resource estimate drives a live, continuously updated mine plan instead of a static annual one
- **Extraction & Processing** — How blast and grade-control precision feeds higher-value ore to a plant optimizing recovery in real time
- **Logistics** — How fleet and haulage data keep that ore moving to port without silent value loss
- **ESG & Closure** — How the same underlying data, enriched with safety and environmental signals, produces an audit-ready ESG record and a funded closure plan years ahead of need

Participants leave with a practical framework for evaluating AI investments by *connectivity across the value chain* — not by department — and a clear picture of what "integrated" AI actually demands in data architecture, regulatory alignment, and cross-functional buy-in.

**Format:** Technical panel/workshop
**Audience:** Mine planners, technical directors, digital transformation leads, government and regulatory affairs teams, ESG and compliance teams, investors

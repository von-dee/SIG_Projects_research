## Title
**The Intelligent Mine: Connecting AI Across the Full Value Chain — From First Drill Hole to Final Rehabilitation**

## Brief

**Description:**

Most mining operations run AI in silos — one tool for opportunity screening, another for geological modelling, another for scheduling, another for maintenance, another for ESG reporting bolted on at the end. This technical workshop makes the case that AI's real value isn't optimizing any single stage — it's connecting all of them through one continuously updated intelligence layer, kept aligned with regulators at every step.

Using a single case thread that runs from opportunity to closure, the session demonstrates how that connectivity works in practice, powered by today's advanced technology stack:

- **Market Research & Opportunity Finding** — Machine learning models fuse commodity-price forecasting, satellite and hyperspectral imagery, and geospatial risk-scoring engines to rank exploration targets and jurisdictional viability before a single hole is drilled.
- **Exploration → Resource Estimation** — AI-ranked drill targets feed directly into geostatistical and deep-learning ore-body models, compressing months of manual resource estimation into a continuously updated, bankable block model.
- **Government & Regulatory Alignment** — Natural language processing and generative AI monitor permitting timelines, licensing conditions, and shifting regulatory text in real time, generating audit-ready compliance documentation and flagging exposure before it becomes a violation.
- **Planning** — The live resource model drives a digital twin of the mine, replacing the static annual plan with a continuously re-optimized schedule that reacts to grade, price, and equipment data as it arrives.
- **Extraction & Processing** — Sensor-based grade control (XRF, PGNAA), computer vision on blast fragmentation, and real-time plant optimization algorithms route higher-value ore to the mill and adjust recovery parameters on the fly.
- **Logistics** — IoT-instrumented fleets, autonomous haulage systems, and predictive analytics keep ore moving to port with dynamic routing and zero silent value loss from bottlenecks or downtime.
- **ESG & Closure** — The same data backbone, enriched with IoT safety sensors and satellite-based environmental monitoring, generates a real-time, audit-ready ESG record and feeds a funded, AI-modelled closure plan years ahead of need.

Participants leave with a practical framework for evaluating AI investments by *connectivity across the value chain* — not by department — and a clear picture of what "integrated" AI actually demands in data architecture, regulatory alignment, and cross-functional buy-in.

**Format:** Technical panel/workshop
**Audience:** Mine planners, technical directors, digital transformation leads, government and regulatory affairs teams, ESG and compliance teams, investors

## Title
**The Intelligent Mine: Connecting AI Across the Full Value Chain — From First Drill Hole to Final Rehabilitation**

## Brief

**Description:**

Most mining operations run AI in silos — one tool for opportunity screening, another for geological modelling, another for scheduling, another for maintenance, another for ESG reporting bolted on at the end. This technical workshop makes the case that AI's real value isn't optimizing any single stage — it's connecting all of them through one continuously updated intelligence layer, kept aligned with regulators at every step.

Using a single case thread that runs from opportunity to closure, the session shows how that connectivity works in practice — grounded in what leading operators are already doing, and what it's worth in dollars:

**Market Research & Opportunity Finding**
Machine learning fuses commodity forecasting, satellite/hyperspectral imagery, and geospatial risk-scoring to rank targets before drilling begins. *Example:* KoBold Metals analyzed a century of geological data, satellite imagery, geochemical surveys, and field reports to identify patterns invisible to human analysis at its Mingomba copper project in Zambia. The discovery, achieved in just three years, represents a fraction of the traditional decade-long exploration cycle. **Benefit:** compressing a 10-year discovery cycle to 3 years accelerates a $2.3 billion project to first revenue by roughly 7 years — a material NPV uplift purely from time-value of capital, alongside savings of weeks and millions of dollars in drilling costs from AI classifiers filtering out false-positive targets.

**Exploration → Resource Estimation**
AI-ranked targets feed directly into continuously updated block models rather than static annual reserve statements, shortening the path from discovery to a bankable resource.

**Government & Regulatory Alignment**
NLP and generative AI track permitting conditions and regulatory text changes in real time, producing audit-ready documentation and flagging exposure before it becomes a violation — critical in jurisdictions like Zambia and the DRC where KoBold has had to negotiate permitting and licensing terms directly with government to advance projects on compressed timelines.

**Planning**
The live resource model feeds a digital twin, replacing the static annual mine plan with one that re-optimizes continuously against grade, price, and equipment data.

**Extraction & Processing**
Sensor-based grade control, computer-vision blast analysis, and real-time plant optimization route higher-value ore to the mill. *Example:* BHP's AI-controlled autonomous truck fleet in Australia achieved 22% higher extraction efficiency and a 20% cost reduction.

**Logistics**
IoT-instrumented fleets and autonomous haulage keep ore moving without silent value loss. *Examples:* Rio Tinto's $940 million AutoHaul programme automated its Pilbara rail network — now running up to 53 fully autonomous trains simultaneously — while eliminating almost 1.5 million kilometres of road travel a year previously needed to ferry drivers. On the truck side, Rio Tinto's autonomous haul fleet delivered roughly 10% fuel efficiency gains, a 15% productivity increase, and an 8% cut in maintenance costs — netting over $100 million a year. On maintenance specifically, BHP's machine-learning maintenance program generated $5.5 million in savings at a single mine in one year, while improving two-week-ahead maintenance planning accuracy from 10% to 85% and cutting missed spare-parts availability from 13% to 1%. At the individual-asset level, early detection of a combustion fault on a single Komatsu 930E haul truck saved an estimated $190,000 and 20 hours of downtime.

**ESG & Closure**
The same data backbone, enriched with safety sensors and satellite-based environmental monitoring, produces an audit-ready ESG record and feeds a funded, AI-modelled closure plan years ahead of need — turning closure from a bolted-on end-of-life cost centre into a forecastable line item.

---

Participants leave with a practical framework for evaluating AI investments by *connectivity across the value chain* — not by department — backed by real numbers: years shaved off discovery timelines, double-digit percentage cuts in extraction and haulage costs, and multi-million-dollar maintenance savings per site per year. The session also covers what "integrated" AI actually demands in data architecture, regulatory alignment, and cross-functional buy-in.

**Format:** Technical panel/workshop
**Audience:** Mine planners, technical directors, digital transformation leads, government and regulatory affairs teams, ESG and compliance teams, investors

# Workshop Brief: The Intelligent Mine

## Connecting AI Across the Full Value Chain, From First Drill Hole to Final Rehabilitation

### Brief

Most mining operations still run on non-intelligent systems: static spreadsheets for scheduling, manual core logging for geological models, paper-based permitting trackers, reactive maintenance logs, and ESG reports compiled by hand months later. Where AI has been adopted, it's usually bolted onto one of these systems in isolation, a forecasting tool here, a computer vision model there, without ever touching the others. This technical workshop argues that the real transformation isn't installing AI in one department; it's replacing that patchwork of disconnected, static systems with a single, continuously updated intelligence layer that learns across the full value chain and stays aligned with regulators at every step.

Using a single case thread that runs from opportunity to closure, the session demonstrates what the shift from non-intelligent to intelligent systems looks like in practice, powered by today's advanced technology stack:

* **Market Research & Opportunity Finding:** Rather than relying on manual desktop studies and static commodity-price spreadsheets, machine learning models integrate commodity-price forecasting, satellite and hyperspectral imagery, and geospatial risk-scoring engines to rank exploration targets and assess jurisdictional viability before drilling a single hole.
* **Exploration → Resource Estimation:** Rather than manual core logging and spreadsheet-based resource estimates, AI-ranked drill targets feed directly into geostatistical and deep-learning orebody models, compressing months of manual work into a continuously updated, bankable block model.
* **Government & Regulatory Alignment:** In place of paper-based permitting trackers and manual compliance reviews, natural language processing and generative AI monitor permitting timelines, licensing conditions, and evolving regulatory text in real time, generating audit-ready compliance documentation and flagging exposure before it becomes a violation. The same intelligent layer extends to on-the-ground oversight: GPS- and telematics-based tracking of earth-moving and mining equipment replaces manual activity logs with verifiable records against permit conditions; spatial and aerial intelligence, including satellite, drone, and remote-sensing imagery, monitors concession boundaries, land disturbance, and illegal operations; and blockchain-backed gold traceability replaces paper chain-of-custody records, tying material from pit to point of sale and closing the gap between what is mined and what is declared.
* **Public Sentiment Analysis:** Where community sentiment was once gauged through occasional consultations and manual media reviews, natural language processing now continuously scans news media, social platforms, and community forums to track public mood around a project, surfacing emerging opposition, misinformation, or local grievances long before they escalate into permitting delays, protests, or reputational damage, and providing operators with an early, evidence-based read on their social licence to operate.
* **Planning:** The static annual mine plan gives way to a live resource model that drives a digital twin of the mine, a continuously re-optimised schedule that reacts to grade, price, and equipment data as it arrives, rather than being revisited once a year.
* **Extraction & Processing:** Manual grade control and periodic plant tuning are replaced by sensor-based grade control (XRF, PGNAA), computer vision for blast fragmentation, and real-time plant optimisation algorithms that route higher-value ore to the mill and adjust recovery parameters on the fly.
* **Logistics:** Where dispatch once relied on radio calls and paper logs, IoT-enabled fleets, autonomous haulage systems, and predictive analytics keep ore moving to port through dynamic routing and eliminate silent value loss caused by bottlenecks or downtime.
* **ESG & Closure:** Instead of ESG reports compiled by hand months after the fact, the same data backbone, enriched with IoT safety sensors and satellite-based environmental monitoring, generates a real-time, audit-ready ESG record and feeds a funded, AI-modelling closure plan years in advance of need.

Participants leave with a practical framework for evaluating AI investments by connectivity across the value chain, not by department, and a clear picture of what it takes to move from a patchwork of static, disconnected systems to a single intelligent layer spanning data architecture, regulatory alignment, and cross-functional buy-in.
